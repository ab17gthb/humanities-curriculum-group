Writing Task: Misreading or Honest Logic

The prompt. Write a developed essay of about 300 words answering the lesson question from this lesson's documents: what did the Republican Party promise, and what did the Supreme Court try to settle in Dred Scott? Judge whether the Dred Scott decision was the Court misreading the Constitution or reading its proslavery logic honestly to its end, and defend the judgment against the strongest rival reading in the record. Your essay must account for the platform the decision voided and for Curtis's dissent from the ratification record. Use at least three pieces of evidence from the evidence bank, and explain how each piece supports your claim.

The bank is drawn from the lesson reading and The Dred Scott File. Write the history in your own sentences, and use the facts rather than copying the bank's wording. Keep the paired records whole wherever you use them: Taney's history beside Curtis's franchise record, and the platform's promise beside the holding that voided it.

Evidence Bank

1. The Republican Party, organized in 1854 from the Kansas-Nebraska fight, joined ex-Whigs, Free Soilers, and anti-Nebraska Democrats on the single plank that Congress must bar slavery from the federal territories, leaving slavery untouched where state law protected it and putting it in the course of ultimate extinction.

2. The free labor creed under the plank held that labor was honorable and the laborer had a right to rise by his work to property and independence, while slavery degraded work and monopolized the common lands. The same party often supported Black exclusion, and Oregon and Indiana had written it into law.

3. In 1856 the party's first nominee, John C. Frémont, carried eleven of the sixteen free states and lost to James Buchanan. A wholly northern party had nearly won the presidency in its first campaign, and its platform claimed that prohibiting slavery in the territories was Congress's right and imperative duty.

4. Dred Scott, enslaved in Missouri, lived in Illinois and at Fort Snelling and sued in 1846 on the ground that residence on free soil had made his family free. The case took eleven years, and Scott's wife and daughters stood inside the same claim.

5. On March 6, 1857, Chief Justice Taney held for seven justices that Black people could not be citizens of the United States, that residence on free soil had not freed Scott, and that Congress had never had power to bar slavery from the territories, so the Missouri Compromise had been unconstitutional from the start and the Republican platform stood voided.

6. Justice Curtis dissented from the ratification record: free Black men were citizens and voters in five of the thirteen original states and were among those who ordained the Constitution, and he objected that the majority had reached the Missouri Compromise after its own citizenship holding removed Scott's standing. Lincoln's House Divided speech of June 1858 charged the decision as part of one design to make slavery national, and in 1860 the voided party elected Lincoln president on the voided platform.

Teacher Guide and Rubric

Full credit requires:

  • A judgment choosing one reading, misreading or honest logic, stated directly and held through the essay. Either reading can earn full credit if the voided platform and Curtis's dissent are carried and the rival reading is answered. An essay that narrates the decision in order without judging what the Court did has summarized the lesson rather than answered it. (Blueprint 7, Blueprint 8)
  • At least three pieces of evidence from the bank, used accurately, drawn from at least two different parts of the record, the party and its platform, Scott's claim, the three holdings, or the dissent and the speech. An essay built on Taney's opinion alone cannot judge the opinion. (Blueprint 8)
  • The Republican promise stated wherever the verdict leans toward honest logic: Congress must bar slavery from the territories, a promise the platform framed as Congress's right and imperative duty, and the decision did to that promise exactly what its critics said, voided it before it could be enacted. (Blueprint 7)
  • Curtis's dissent accounted for, not decorated with: free Black citizens and voters in five original states at ratification, and the objection to reaching the Missouri Compromise at all. A misreading essay that skips the franchise record has asserted what it needed to prove. (Blueprint 8)
  • The strongest rival reading stated at full strength and answered. Against a misreading verdict: the Constitution's fugitive clause, its protection of the trade to 1808, and its counting of the enslaved gave Taney provisions to build on. Against an honest logic verdict: a logic that required ignoring recorded Black citizenship was chosen, not found, and Curtis, no abolitionist judge, said so from inside the Court. (Blueprint 7, Blueprint 8)
  • The settlement's fate assigned at the close: the House Divided charge of 1858, the voided party's victory in 1860, secession, and the citizenship holding's later reversal by amendment, the record of a decree the country refused to keep. (Blueprint 8)

Model opening for teacher reference. The Dred Scott decision was a misreading, and the proof sits inside the Court's own building. Taney held that Black people had never been part of the people who made the Constitution, resting the holding on what the founding generation was said to have believed. Curtis answered from what that generation had done: free Black men voted in five of the original states and helped ordain the document Taney said excluded them. A court that must set aside recorded votes to find a uniform founding opinion is not discovering the Constitution. It is selecting the parts of the record its conclusion requires, and the country, which elected the voided party's candidate in 1860, treated the selection as politics in robes.

Common weak answers. A misreading essay built on bank 6 alone cannot explain why Taney's opinion ran to the length it did on the Constitution's slavery provisions, or why seven justices signed it. An honest logic essay built on bank 5 alone cannot explain Curtis.
