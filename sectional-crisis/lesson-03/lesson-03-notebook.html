Popular sovereignty in Kansas produced two governments and a small war because...

Teacher Answer Key

EpisodeWhat popular sovereignty promisedWhat happened insteadWhat it settled or left open
The repealNothing yet. The repeal only cleared the ground, by declaring the Missouri line of 1820, which had barred slavery from the northern Louisiana Purchase lands, inoperative and void.The repeal was Douglas's price for southern votes for organizing the territories and advancing a railroad west from Chicago.It settled that Congress would no longer rule slavery out of a territory in advance. It left the whole question to whoever could win Kansas.
The doctrineThat a territory's own people, left perfectly free by Congress, would form and regulate their domestic institutions in their own way, and the question would leave national politics.The act named no residency rule, no timetable for the decision, and no umpire for a disputed vote.It settled the theory on paper. It left every practical question, who votes, when, and who counts, to the contest itself.
The settlers' raceThat settlers moving to Kansas would carry the question with them and decide it by living there.Both sections organized the migration. New England emigrant aid societies sent free state settlers, and Missourians crossed a short border in far larger immediate numbers.It settled that Kansas would be contested ground from its first month. It left the decision to numbers, and numbers could be imported.
The election of March 1855That the territory's voters would elect its first legislature.Thousands of Missourians rode in, voted, and rode home. The legislature they seated expelled its free state members, and free state settlers built a rival government at Topeka.It settled which government Washington would recognize, the proslavery one. It left Kansas with two governments claiming one territory.
The violence of May 1856That neighbors would settle the question by ballots, under a doctrine meant to replace congressional fighting with local decision.A proslavery posse sacked Lawrence, Brooks beat Sumner nearly to death at his Senate desk, and Brown's party killed five men at Pottawatomie, all in one week.It settled that the contest would be carried on by force as well as votes. It left the doctrine with no answer to violence done in its name.
LecomptonThat Kansans would vote on their constitution and thereby decide slavery for their state.The referendum offered the constitution with or without slavery while protecting slavery already present under either choice, and offered no way to reject the constitution. Buchanan endorsed it; Douglas broke with him; Congress refused it.It settled that Kansas would not enter as a slave state, and it entered free in 1861. It left popular sovereignty disowned by its author in the case that had made it famous.

The lesson in one sentence. Strong answers join the design to the record: the act sent the slavery question to Kansas with no rule for who counted as the people, no timetable, and no umpire, so organized outsiders voted, rival governments formed, and when ballots failed to settle it, armed men tried. Credit answers that name the fraudulent election or the rigged referendum alone only when the sentence explains how the act's own silences let that event decide the question.
