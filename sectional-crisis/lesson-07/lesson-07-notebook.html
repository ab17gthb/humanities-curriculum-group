Teacher Answer Key

The recordWhat happened, or was declaredWho actedWhat it changed about the war
Contraband, 1861 and 1862From May 1861 enslaved people escaped to Fort Monroe and Butler held them as contraband of war. Congress followed with the First Confiscation Act and, in April 1862, compensated emancipation in the District of Columbia.The enslaved people who walked to the lines acted first. Butler, and then Congress, ratified what they had done.Freedom entered the war as a fact on the ground before it existed as policy.
The Proclamation, January 1, 1863Lincoln declared free the enslaved people in the states and parts of states in rebellion and ordered the government and its armed forces to recognize and maintain that freedom.Lincoln, as commander in chief, under his war powers.The Union was now committed against slavery where its armies advanced, while slavery stood untouched in Delaware, Maryland, Kentucky, Missouri, and the exempted districts.
Black soldiers, 1863 and 1864Some 180,000 Black men served in the United States Colored Troops, at ten dollars a month against thirteen until equal pay in June 1864. The 54th Massachusetts assaulted Fort Wagner on July 18, 1863.Black leaders who recruited, the men who enlisted, and the regiments that protested unequal pay and held their protest for eighteen months in the 54th's case.Black men became makers of the war's outcome, and their service claimed a citizenship the postwar settlement would have to answer.
Gettysburg and Vicksburg, July 1863Meade stopped Lee's second invasion at Gettysburg on July 1 to 3, at a combined cost of some fifty thousand casualties. Vicksburg surrendered to Grant on July 4 after a six week siege.The Army of the Potomac under Meade, and Grant's army on the Mississippi.The Confederacy lost its hope of winning on northern soil and was split in two in the same week.
The Address and the riotsOn November 19, 1863, Lincoln rededicated the war at Gettysburg to the proposition of equality. In July, New York's draft riots killed about one hundred and twenty people, most of the mob's victims Black New Yorkers, before troops restored order.Lincoln at the cemetery, and, in the riots, the city's crowds and the regiments sent from Gettysburg to stop them.The same year holds the war's highest statement of purpose and its ugliest home front violence.

The war transformed in one paragraph. Strong answers hold the declaration and its carriers together. Emancipation changed what the war was for when the proclamation of January 1, 1863, committed the Union to maintain the freedom of the people in the rebellious states, opened its ranks to them, and was restated at Gettysburg as a test of equality. The change was carried by more hands than the president's: the enslaved who reached the lines from 1861, the leaders who recruited, and the 180,000 men who served. The paragraph must end with what stood unovercome: slavery legal in four loyal states, the exempted districts, or a northern city that lynched Black residents in July.
