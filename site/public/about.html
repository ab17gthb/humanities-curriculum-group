About

Humanities Curriculum Group publishes social studies curriculum written for real classrooms: print-first materials, honest pacing, and tests built to a blueprint that is published with the unit instead of kept private.

Who builds it

The curriculum is built by Alex Brouhard, a social studies teacher who teaches US History and AP Psychology. The materials are designed the way his own classroom runs: readings that carry the content, activities that use real photographs and documents, and writing space on the page where students do the work.

The print standard

Teachers print these materials, usually on school copiers. So the documents are black text on white paper, with photographs where they teach something and open space where students write. Nothing in a student document depends on color printing.

The Knoweth family

Humanities Curriculum Group is part of Knoweth, alongside Rycal, which gives AP students spaced-repetition study plans, and The Primary Source Project, a library of audited primary sources with a DBQ builder. The Primary Source Project bank supplies the source layer for these units.

The planner on this site is a working prototype that runs on the free Progressive Era unit. It stores nothing and needs no account. The full planner, with saved plans and whole-year pacing, is in development.
