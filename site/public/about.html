About

Humanities Curriculum Group publishes social studies curriculum written for real classrooms: print-first materials, honest pacing, and tests built to a blueprint that is published with the unit instead of kept private.

Who builds it

The curriculum is built by a working social studies teacher. The materials are designed the way a real classroom runs: readings that carry the content, activities that use real photographs and documents, and writing space on the page where students do the work.

The print standard

Teachers print these materials, usually on school copiers. So the documents are black text on white paper, with photographs where they teach something and open space where students write. Nothing in a student document depends on color printing.

Sister projects

Humanities Curriculum Group has two sister projects: Rycal, which gives AP students spaced-repetition study plans, and The Primary Source Project, a library of audited primary sources with a DBQ builder. The Primary Source Project bank supplies the source layer for these units.

The planner on this site runs on every unit in the course. Planning the free Progressive Era unit needs no account, and plans save on your device as you work. Sign in and your year and unit plans save to your account, so they are waiting on any device, with whole-year pacing and a teacher home view drawn from your own plans.
