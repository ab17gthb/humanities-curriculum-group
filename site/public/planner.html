The Planner

Set the first day of the unit and your test date, and the planner proposes pacing from the test blueprint. Pick assignments from the plate and it lays them on your school days, lands the test at the end, and checks your picks against all fifteen blueprint entries. If a day gets eaten by an assembly or a field trip, mark it in the schedule and that day's work bumps forward on its own. Days off from your year plan are honored here automatically. Your picks and dates save as you go.

Four days on working conditions and muckraking lead to a fifth day on government reform. Change the start date or swap the last lesson to see what moves and which unit test objectives your picks cover.

The Progressive Era

Essential question: How did Americans respond to the problems created by industrialization, urbanization, and political corruption?

Each unit keeps its own plan: picks, dates, sections, pins, and additions are saved separately per unit, and switching units never moves the other unit's plan.

Your sections

Teach the unit to more than one class? Each section keeps its own calendar: start date, test date, marked days, pins, rotation, and period length. Your picks, order, and splits are shared across sections.

1 · Your dates

Content is paced in lesson-days, one lesson-day per period of about 45 minutes. A block of about 90 minutes fits about two lessons per meeting, so entries pair up on one date and the unit takes about half as many meetings. Period length is set per section.

Proposed pacing

Instructional days divided across the lessons in proportion to how much of the test blueprint each lesson carries, with the last day held for review.

2 · Pick from the plate

Core items start checked. Guided notes run the same day as their reading and take no extra time. Half-day items pair up on one day.

Check or uncheck activities. Reading notes take no extra time, and two half-day activities can share a date.

Teaching something of your own instead? Add it below and tag what it covers, and it counts toward coverage like any other pick.

Add your own resource

Tag the blueprint entries this item teaches or assesses. Coverage an item earns this way is marked as yours in the coverage check.

Add a TPSP resource

From The Primary Source Project's Progressive Era library. Click a source to add it to your plan; a few arrive pre-tagged to the blueprint where the match is clear, and you can retag any of them with Edit.

3 · Your schedule

Drag any item to another day and everything after it reflows. Pin an item (📍) to lock it to its date; pinned items hold their date while the rest of the plan moves around them. Split in the picker divides a one-day lesson into two half days. Use the menu on any day to mark it assembly, substitute, field trip, testing, or no class; marked days hold no lessons and the work bumps forward. If you will be out but your class still meets, use Mark sub day on that day instead: the date and the test day hold, the day teaches nothing from the plan, its work moves later, and you can leave the substitute a note right on the day.

Mark a date as no class to see the work move. The sample uses five weekdays and does not schedule the full unit test.

To reuse a plan next year: export it, import it here, then set the new first day and test date. Everything reflows onto the new calendar, pins included.
