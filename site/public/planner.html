Proposed pacing

Instructional days divided across the lessons in proportion to how much of the test blueprint each lesson carries, with the last day held for review.

2 · Pick from the plate

Core items start checked. Guided notes run the same day as their reading and take no extra time. Half-day items pair up on one day.

Check or uncheck activities. Reading notes take no extra time, and two half-day activities can share a date.

Teaching something of your own instead? Add it below and tag what it covers, and it counts toward coverage like any other pick.

Add your own resource

Add a TPSP resource

From The Primary Source Project's Progressive Era library. Click a source to add it to your plan; a few arrive pre-tagged to the blueprint where the match is clear, and you can retag any of them with Edit.

3 · Your schedule

Move lessons or mark days off

Use the move controls or drag a lesson to a new date. Use the pin button to release a pinned date. Mark an assembly, field trip, or no-class day to move the work. For a substitute, choose Mark sub day and add a note.

Mark a date as no class to see the work move. The sample uses five weekdays and does not schedule the full unit test.

To reuse a plan next year: export it, import it here, then set the new first day and test date. Everything reflows onto the new calendar, pins included.
