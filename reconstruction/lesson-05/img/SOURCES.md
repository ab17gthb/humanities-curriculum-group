## Sources quoted in this lesson, with editions

- Petition of the Freedmen of Edisto Island, activity Station 5 and quick check: the audited TPSP bank record edisto-island-petition-1865 in reconstruction.json, whose sourceNote names Critical Explorers, with original spellings preserved. Quoted verbatim from the bank record's text field, extracted programmatically during this build so no transcription drift is possible. The bank record's own cuts stand marked with ellipses in brackets. The petition's return in this lesson, after its first reading in Lesson 1, is disclosed on the activity's face and in the quick check's background note, as the unit skeleton directs.
- Special Field Orders No. 15, activity Station 1: a labeled teaching summary built from the public record, labeled a summary on its face in the activity, in this file, and in the teacher key. No period text is quoted at that station. The builder searched for a transcription from the National Archives or the Freedmen and Southern Society Project during this build and did not verify one from either named source, so under the unit spec's verification rule the order is summarized, not quoted.
- A sharecropping contract's standard terms, activity Station 2: a labeled teaching summary drawn from the audited TPSP bank record sharecropping-contract-1879 in reconstruction.json, whose sourceNote names the Gilder Lehrman Institute, by way of the Chicago Daily Tribune, April 30, 1879. The summary discloses on its face that it is drawn from an 1879 Louisiana contract and why. The contract itself, dated 1879, after this unit's 1877 close, is NOT reprinted in any document in this lesson, per the unit spec's boundary call.
- The freedmen's school movement, activity Station 3: a labeled teaching summary built from the public record of the Freedmen's Bureau's education reports and the missionary societies' reports, labeled a summary on its face. No period text is quoted at that station.
- The Freedmen's Savings Bank, activity Station 4: a labeled teaching summary built from the public record of the bank's charter, reports, and failure, labeled a summary on its face. No period text is quoted at that station.

## Figures stated as counts

- Some four hundred thousand acres reserved and some forty thousand freedpeople settled under Special Field Orders No. 15, as stated in the term definition in the unit spec and in the unit skeleton for this lesson.
- Sixteen thousand (16000) acres taken up on Edisto Island, as stated in the petition's own text in the bank record.
- Some quarter of a million students enrolled in the freedmen's schools by 1870, as stated in the term definition in the unit spec.
- The Freedmen's Savings Bank's failure: some three million dollars owed to more than sixty thousand depositors, as stated in the public record summarized at activity Station 4. The bank's thirty seven branches over its life are stated the same way.
- A share of the crop commonly half, as stated in the term definition in the unit spec.

## Gaps recorded honestly

- Special Field Orders No. 15 is summarized, not quoted, for the verification reason stated above. No sentence of any teaching summary in this lesson may be quoted as a period text, and the activity's teacher key says so.
- No verified public domain period image of an African Methodist Episcopal church congregation, of a Freedmen's Savings Bank branch, or of a sharecropping family at work in the 1865 to 1877 period was located during this build. The period photographs of sharecropper families that search returned are Farm Security Administration photographs from the 1930s, a different era, and they were not used, because picturing this lesson's families with Depression era photographs would misdate the record. The churches, the bank, and the crop work are carried in prose, in the summaries, and in the deck's text and stat slides.
- No image of violence is used anywhere in this lesson. The burning of schoolhouses and threats against teachers are stated in text at Station 3 and nowhere pictured, following the unit's image discipline.
- The South Carolina school photograph (image 3) is cataloged with a question mark in its title; the deck describes it as a freedmen's school in South Carolina following the cataloged title and does not claim a more specific place or date for it.
