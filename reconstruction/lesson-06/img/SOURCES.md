# Image and Source Records for Reconstruction Lesson 6, The Counterrevolution

All images are public domain works. Downloads were made on October 22, 2026. Every downloaded file was opened and verified as an image with PIL before use, and its size as downloaded is recorded below. Document copies are stored in the lesson's assets folder; masters are in img/; deck versions are cropped to portrait (1046 by 1500) or to 16:9 for the title slide and stored in slides-build/img/; no other alteration.

## Image discipline for this lesson (binding)

No image of the Ku Klux Klan, of night riders, of victims, or of any act of violence appears anywhere in this lesson's documents, deck, or teacher notes. The violence is taught in testimony and text, stated plainly and never lingered on. The lesson's visuals are portraits (Grant; Forrest as a wartime portrait), a government building (the United States Capitol), and text and stat slides. The discipline is named on the face of the reading, the activity, and the notebook page.

## Gaps recorded honestly

- No verified public domain likeness of Abram Colby was located during this build. A Wikimedia Commons file search for his name on October 22, 2026 returned no portrait file (the results were unrelated books and documents). Colby is therefore carried in testimony and text only, and no image is attached to his name anywhere in the lesson. No catalog number is invented for him.
- Under this lesson's image discipline, no image of the Klan, of night riders, of victims, or of any act of violence was sought or used, in any document, in the deck, or in the teacher notes.
- The Forrest portrait is a wartime photograph (1861 to 1865), not a portrait from his Klan years; the deck captions it as a wartime portrait.
