# Slides + Teacher Notes Rollout (authorized by Alex, 2026-10-23)

Standard (pilot: Colonial America Lesson 1, approved 2026-10-23):
- Teacher notes republished in guide format (Holtzman model): slide-by-slide, On-the-slide locator, bracketed delivery cues, Picture lines for image slides, talking-point bullets, stats on their own bold lines, Ask questions with answers where the slide poses one, Note enrichment, vocabulary verbatim (term: definition) per vocab slide.
- Deck speaker notes carry the SAME guide content (applied by script from the guide HTML), so deck and printout cannot drift.
- Slides reworked: content bullets are short complete sentences carrying the whole fact (3-4 per slide, every one ending with a period — verified by scan); content slides pair with a verified public-domain/CC0 image where the image teaches, or stay text by judgment; stat slides, dividers, vocab, question, and closing slides keep their form.
- No meta language on student-facing slides (a slide never talks about "the lesson" itself); meta talk lives in teacher-facing notes only.
- Per-lesson close: guide PDF replaces lesson-XX-teacher-notes.pdf and is uploaded to storage; rebuilt deck replaces lesson-XX-slides.pptx and is uploaded; a Google Slides copy is created/updated; image sources recorded in the lesson's img/SOURCES.md.
