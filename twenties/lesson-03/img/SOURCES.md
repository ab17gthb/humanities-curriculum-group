# Image Sources for The Twenties Lesson 3, America on Wheels

All images are real public domain images held by the Library of Congress, the State Library of Queensland, the Seattle Municipal Archives, the Digital Public Library of America, or the Internet Archive, and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-03/assets/` on October 21, 2026. No image was altered beyond resizing for print. Each download was checked against its catalog record and viewed before use. Disclosures are stated plainly where an image does not show exactly what a first glance might suggest.

## 1. family-car-touring-queensland-1925.jpg (used in Activity Station 1)

- Title: S.P. Campbell and family touring the Glass House Mountains region in a motor car, ca. 1925
- What it shows: The Campbell family posed beside their motor car on a touring outing, with a cane picnic basket. The car and the family group are clearly visible.
- Creator: Unknown photographer
- Date: About 1925
- Repository: John Oxley Library, State Library of Queensland, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:StateLibQld_1_161430_S.P._Campbell_and_family_touring_the_Glass_House_Mountains_region_in_a_motor_car,_ca._1925.jpg
- Public domain basis: State Library of Queensland places this image in the public domain.
- Disclosure: The family is Australian and the location is Queensland, Australia, not the United States. The caption in the activity states this in full. The photograph is used only to show the family touring pattern; all figures in Station 1 are United States registration figures from federal highway statistics.

## 2. paved-highway-durham-county-1922.jpg (used in Activity Station 2)

- Title: Rock Asphalt Highway, Federal Aid Project No. 71, Durham County, as printed in the Biennial Report of the State Highway Commission of North Carolina (1922)
- What it shows: A completed paved rock asphalt highway curving through trees, with guard rails. It is a finished road, not road construction in progress. The caption printed beneath the photograph is part of the original page.
- Creator: North Carolina State Highway Commission (photographer not named)
- Date: Published 1922
- Repository: Internet Archive Book Images scan of the 1922 report, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Biennial_Report_of_the_State_Highway_Commission_of_North_Carolina_(1922)_(14596642370).jpg
- Public domain basis: Published in the United States in 1922. Wikimedia Commons and the Internet Archive mark the scan as having no known copyright restrictions.
- Disclosure: The hunt for this station was a paving or construction photograph. No verified 1920 to 1929 construction photograph with a clean public domain record was found, so the station uses this verified photograph of the finished result of federal aid paving instead, and its source line and caption say exactly that.

## 3. auto-tourists-camp-washington-1923.jpg (used in Activity Station 3)

- Title: Auto tourists camp, 8/20/23
- What it shows: Automobiles parked beside rows of tents at an auto tourists camp, with the Washington Monument visible in the distance, Washington, D.C.
- Creator: National Photo Company
- Date: August 20, 1923
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LCCN 2016847862, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Auto_tourists_camp,_8-20-23_LCCN2016847862.jpg
- Public domain basis: Glass negative made in 1923 by the National Photo Company and held by the Library of Congress with no known restrictions on publication.

## 4. filling-station-toledo-1924.jpg (used in Activity Station 5)

- Title: Clyde Mock's Hi-Speed filling station, Toledo, Ohio, 1924
- What it shows: A small filling station building with two globe pumps in snow. Signs read Hi-Speed Gas Station and advertise hot dogs, refreshments, lunch, and coffee. The catalog record places the station at present day Airport Highway and Reynolds Road, Toledo, Ohio, and describes it as a filling station and eatery.
- Creator: Unknown photographer
- Date: 1924
- Repository: Digital Public Library of America, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clyde_Mock%27s_Hi-Speed_filling_station,_Toledo,_Ohio,_1924_-_DPLA_-_24f40d239321be66522848483230e4ae.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph dated 1924 and distributed by the Digital Public Library of America as public domain.

## 5. california-avenue-seattle-1925.jpg (used in the Quick Check)

- Title: California Ave SW from SW Hill St, October 30, 1925
- What it shows: California Avenue SW in Seattle, looking north. Streetcar tracks of the Number 3 West Seattle line run down the center of the street, a paved automobile lane runs on each side, automobiles are parked and driving on the lanes, and houses line both sides. Catalog subjects are residential streets and street railroad tracks.
- Creator: Unknown photographer, Seattle Municipal Archives (Seattle Photographs, PH Coll 266.244)
- Date: October 30, 1925
- Repository: Seattle Municipal Archives, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:California_Ave_SW_from_SW_Hill_St,_October_30,_1925_(SEATTLE_855).jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1925 for a municipal archive and classified public domain on Wikimedia Commons.
- Disclosure: This street is a city residential street with streetcar tracks, not a new automobile suburb. The quick check is written to match what the photograph shows: the two systems sharing one street in 1925. It does not claim the street is a suburb built for drivers.
