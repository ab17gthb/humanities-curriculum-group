# Lesson 5 deck images: sources

All images are real public domain images via Wikimedia Commons, the Library of Congress, the National Archives, or the Indiana Historical Society. No AI-generated or stock-site images. Portrait files are center crops made with PIL from the sources below, resized to 1046 by 1500 pixels for the right-side photo slots in deck_common; the title file is a 16:9 crop resized to 1920 by 1080. Cropping only; subjects kept in frame, nothing stretched.

- `title-169.jpg`: Derivative 16:9 crop of the lesson asset `agents-pouring-liquor-sewer-1921.jpg`. See `lesson-05/img/SOURCES.md` entry 1. The mount border of the original print was trimmed before cropping.
  - Title: New York City Deputy Police Commissioner John A. Leach, right, watching agents pour liquor into sewer following a raid during the height of prohibition
  - Creator: Photographer not named in the Library of Congress record
  - Date: About 1921
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LCCN 99405169, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Public domain basis: Photograph taken about 1921 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.

- `pouring-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the same lesson asset `agents-pouring-liquor-sewer-1921.jpg`. Same title, creator, date, repository, and public domain basis as `title-169.jpg` above.

  - Public domain basis: Work of the United States federal government. Public domain.

  - Date: DISCLOSURE. Wikimedia Commons dates the file to 1930, outside the 1920 to 1929 span of the lesson's main story, and the news bureau caption attached to the print is dated February 26, 1931. The speaker notes for the slide that uses it state both dates plainly, exactly as the lesson documents do.
