# Image Sources for The Twenties Lesson 5, The Noble Experiment

All images are real public domain images held by the Library of Congress, the National Archives, the Indiana Historical Society, or the Federal Bureau of Investigation, and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-05/assets/` on October 21, 2026. No image was altered beyond conversion to JPEG and resizing for print. One image (the Capone portrait) is dated outside the 1920s on its own catalog record; that disclosure appears below and in its caption in the activity.

## 1. agents-pouring-liquor-sewer-1921.jpg (used in the activity, Station 2)

- Title: New York City Deputy Police Commissioner John A. Leach, right, watching agents pour liquor into sewer following a raid during the height of prohibition
- What it shows, verified against the file: Two men pour a barrel of liquor into an open sewer in a New York street while Deputy Police Commissioner John A. Leach, in a suit and straw hat, watches at right beside a uniformed police officer. A police department truck stands behind them.
- Creator: Photographer not named in the Library of Congress record
- Date: About 1921 (the Library of Congress catalogs the photograph to the height of Prohibition, and published captions date it circa 1921)
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LCCN 99405169, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:New_York_City_Deputy_Police_Commissioner_John_A._Leach,_right,_watching_agents_pour_liquor_into_sewer_following_a_raid_during_the_height_of_prohibition_LCCN99405169.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken about 1921 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired. The Library of Congress places no known restrictions on the image.

## 4. capone-1930.jpg (used in the activity, Station 3)

- Title: Al Capone in 1930
- What it shows, verified against the file: Al Capone seated in a three-piece suit at the Chicago Detective Bureau.
- Creator: Chicago Bureau (Federal Bureau of Investigation), Wide World Photos, as cataloged by Wikimedia Commons
- Date: DISCLOSURE. Wikimedia Commons dates the file to 1930, which is outside the 1920 to 1929 span of the lesson's main story, and the news bureau caption attached to the print is dated February 26, 1931. The photograph therefore shows Capone at the start of the federal prosecutions that ended his career, not during the 1920s years in which he built his organization. The caption in the activity states both dates and the reason the image is used.
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Al_Capone_in_1930.jpg
- Public domain basis: Wikimedia Commons classifies the photograph as public domain (work attributed to the Chicago Bureau of the Federal Bureau of Investigation).
