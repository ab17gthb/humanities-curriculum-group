# Image Sources for The Twenties Lesson 6, The Culture Wars

All images are real public domain photographs held by the Library of Congress, the Smithsonian Institution Archives, the Boston Public Library, or the U.S. National Archives, and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-06/assets/` on October 21, 2026. Each download was opened and inspected to confirm what it shows. No image was altered beyond resizing for print.

## 1. klan-march-washington-1925.jpg (used in Activity Station 3)

- Title: K.K.K. parade, [8/8/25]
- Creator: National Photo Company
- Date: August 8, 1925
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LCCN 2016840297, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:K.K.K._parade,_(8-8-25)_LCCN2016840297.jpg (catalog: https://lccn.loc.gov/2016840297)
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1925 and published in the United States before 1929. The Library of Congress lists no known restrictions on publication for the National Photo Company collection.
- What it shows: An elevated view of robed Ku Klux Klan members marching in formed ranks down a wide Washington, D.C. street, with crowds of spectators on the sidewalks. The Library of Congress title and catalog date establish the August 8, 1925 parade. Contemporary reporting, including Washington Post coverage summarized by WETA Boundary Stones, places the parade route on Pennsylvania Avenue. The in-text caption therefore says Washington, D.C., August 8, 1925, and attributes the Pennsylvania Avenue route to the parade as reported, not to anything visible in the frame alone.

## 2. scopes-outdoor-proceedings-1925.jpg (used in Activity Station 4)

- Title: Tennessee v. John T. Scopes Trial: Outdoor proceedings on July 20, 1925, showing William Jennings Bryan and Clarence Darrow. (2 of 4 photos)
- Creator: Watson Davis, managing editor of Science Service, covering the trial
- Date: July 20, 1925
- Repository: Smithsonian Institution Archives, Record Unit 7091 (Science Service records), via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tennessee_v._John_T._Scopes_Trial-_Outdoor_proceedings_on_July_20,_1925,_showing_William_Jennings_Bryan_and_Clarence_Darrow._(2_of_4_photos)_(2898243103).jpg
- Public domain basis: Smithsonian Institution photograph released with no restrictions.
- What it shows: William Jennings Bryan seated at left center, wearing a bow tie, while Clarence Darrow stands at right questioning him, with the crowd behind the platform. The Smithsonian description confirms the identities, the outdoor session of July 20, 1925, moved outside because of the heat, and that Bryan is seated at left being interrogated by Darrow.

## 3. sacco-vanzetti-handcuffed-1923.jpg (used in Activity Station 5)

- Title: Bartolomeo Vanzetti (left), handcuffed to Nicola Sacco (right), Dedham, Massachusetts Superior Court
- Creator: Unknown photographer
- Date: 1923
- Repository: Boston Public Library, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sacvan.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1923 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.
- What it shows: Vanzetti and Sacco seated side by side, handcuffed to each other, at the Superior Court in Dedham. Disclosure: the photograph dates from a 1923 court appearance during the appeals period, not from the 1921 trial itself. The caption in the activity gives the 1923 date and does not claim it shows the trial.

## 4. angel-island-interview-1923.jpg (used in the Quick Check)

- Title: Immigration Interview on Angel Island
- Creator: Unknown photographer, U.S. Public Health Service historical photograph file
- Date: 1923, as cataloged by the National Archives
- Repository: U.S. National Archives and Records Administration, NARA ID 7452280, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Immigration_Interview_on_Angel_Island_-_NARA_-_7452280.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph from a U.S. federal government records series held by the National Archives, classified public domain on Wikimedia Commons.
- What it shows: A young Chinese immigrant seated at a small table answering three officials, two of them in uniform, at the Angel Island immigration station in San Francisco Bay. The interview setting matches the station practice described in the quick check background note: detailed questioning of Chinese applicants under the exclusion laws, with answers checked against witnesses.
