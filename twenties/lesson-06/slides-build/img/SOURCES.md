# Lesson 6 deck images: sources

All images are real public domain images via Wikimedia Commons, the Library of Congress, the Smithsonian Institution Archives, the Boston Public Library, or the U.S. National Archives. No AI-generated or stock-site images. Portrait files are crops made with PIL from the sources below, resized to 1046 by 1500 pixels for the right-side photo slots in deck_common; the title file is a 16:9 crop resized to 1920 by 1080. Cropping only; nothing stretched.

- `title-169.jpg` and `klan-port.jpg`: Derivative crops of the lesson asset `klan-march-washington-1925.jpg`. See `lesson-06/img/SOURCES.md` entry 1.
  - Title: K.K.K. parade, [8/8/25]
  - Creator: National Photo Company
  - Date: August 8, 1925
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LCCN 2016840297, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:K.K.K._parade,_(8-8-25)_LCCN2016840297.jpg
  - Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1925 and published in the United States before 1929. The Library of Congress lists no known restrictions on publication for the National Photo Company collection.
  - Note: the catalog establishes Washington, D.C., and the date. Captions in this deck claim Washington, D.C., only; the Pennsylvania Avenue route is attributed to contemporary reporting, as in the lesson documents. The title crop also trims the glass negative border at the left edge of the source scan.

- `angel-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the lesson asset `angel-island-interview-1923.jpg`. See `lesson-06/img/SOURCES.md` entry 4.
  - Title: Immigration Interview on Angel Island
  - Creator: Unknown photographer, U.S. Public Health Service historical photograph file
  - Date: 1923, as cataloged by the National Archives
  - Repository: U.S. National Archives and Records Administration, NARA ID 7452280, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Immigration_Interview_on_Angel_Island_-_NARA_-_7452280.jpg
  - Public domain basis: U.S. federal government photograph held by the National Archives.

- `ellis-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of a new download for this deck (not a lesson asset).
  - Title: Photograph of a Group of Immigrants Outside a Building on Ellis Island
  - Creator: Unknown photographer, U.S. federal records photograph
  - Date: No date is stated in the National Archives catalog listing retrieved for this file; the photograph belongs to the Ellis Island immigration station era.
  - Repository: U.S. National Archives and Records Administration, NARA ID 595669, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Photograph_of_a_Group_of_Immigrants_Outside_a_Building_on_Ellis_Island_-_NARA_-_595669.jpg
  - Public domain basis: U.S. federal government photograph held by the National Archives, classified public domain on Wikimedia Commons.
  - Note: the speaker notes describe this image only as a National Archives photograph of immigrants at Ellis Island, with no date claimed.

- `scopes-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the lesson asset `scopes-outdoor-proceedings-1925.jpg`. See `lesson-06/img/SOURCES.md` entry 2.
  - Title: Tennessee v. John T. Scopes Trial: Outdoor proceedings on July 20, 1925, showing William Jennings Bryan and Clarence Darrow. (2 of 4 photos)
  - Creator: Watson Davis, Science Service
  - Date: July 20, 1925
  - Repository: Smithsonian Institution Archives, Record Unit 7091, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tennessee_v._John_T._Scopes_Trial-_Outdoor_proceedings_on_July_20,_1925,_showing_William_Jennings_Bryan_and_Clarence_Darrow._(2_of_4_photos)_(2898243103).jpg

  - Note: the crop trims the white margins of the scan and centers on Bryan seated at left center; Darrow stands at the right edge of the crop. The speaker notes say exactly that.

- `sacco-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the lesson asset `sacco-vanzetti-handcuffed-1923.jpg`. See `lesson-06/img/SOURCES.md` entry 3.
  - Title: Bartolomeo Vanzetti (left), handcuffed to Nicola Sacco (right), Dedham, Massachusetts Superior Court
  - Creator: Unknown photographer
  - Date: 1923
  - Repository: Boston Public Library, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sacvan.jpg
  - Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1923 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.
  - Disclosure: the photograph dates from a 1923 court appearance during the appeals period, not from the 1921 trial itself. The narrow portrait crop centers on Vanzetti; Sacco is at the right edge, joined by the handcuffs. Both points are stated in the speaker notes of the slide that uses it.
