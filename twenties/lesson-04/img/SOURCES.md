# Image Sources for Twenties Lesson 4, The Politics of Prosperity

All images are real public domain images held by the Library of Congress, the Missouri History Museum, or the Nationaal Archief (the Dutch National Archives), and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-04/assets/` on October 21, 2026. No image was altered beyond resizing for print. Each download was opened and checked against its catalog record before use.

## 1. coolidge-portrait-1924.jpg (used in Activity Desk 2)

- Title: President Calvin Coolidge, 1924 portrait photograph
- What it shows: A head and shoulders portrait of President Calvin Coolidge, facing slightly right
- Creator: Harris and Ewing, Washington, D.C. photographers
- Date: About 1924
- Repository: Library of Congress, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:President_Calvin_Coolidge,_1924_portrait_photograph_(3x4_cropped).jpeg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken and published in the United States in 1924. Copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons classifies the file as public domain.

## 2. washington-conference-1921.jpg (used in the Activity introduction)

- Title: Disarmament Conference in Session, Continental Hall of the D.A.R.; Keystone View Company stereograph no. 23314 (Commons file title: "Conference Delegate in Session, Continental Hall, Washington, D.C.")
- What it shows: The Washington Conference (Conference on the Limitation of Armament) in session in Memorial Continental Hall, Washington, D.C., delegates seated at tables arranged in a square, with the galleries filled
- Creator: Keystone View Company
- Date: November 21, 1921 (date given in the Missouri History Museum catalog record)
- Repository: Missouri History Museum, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%22Conference_Delegate_in_Session,_Continental_Hall,_Washington,_D.C.%22.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph made in 1921 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.
- Disclosure: The source is a stereograph card. The wide single frame is reproduced as one image in the activity; nothing in the frame is a composite.

## 3. teapot-dome-cartoon-1924.jpg (used in Activity Part 2)

- Title: Bargain Day in Washington
- What it shows: The Capitol and the White House tagged Sold and being hauled away on trucks, the Washington Monument being removed, and sale signs offering American institutions, cabinet members, honor, and ethics at bargain prices
- Creator: Ellison Hoover, cartoonist
- Date: 1924, published in Life magazine
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ellison_Hoover_Cartoon_Teapot_Dome_Scandal_1924.jpg
- Public domain basis: Cartoon published in the United States in 1924. Copyright expired.

## 4. kellogg-briand-signing-1928.jpg (used in the Quick Check)

- Title: The signing of the Kellogg Pact by the German Gustav Stresemann (Nationaal Archief caption, translated)
- What it shows: German foreign minister Gustav Stresemann signing the Kellogg-Briand Pact at the French Foreign Ministry in Paris, with the representatives of the other signing nations around the table
- Creator: Unknown photographer (Wikimedia Commons suggests possibly Erich Salomon; the attribution is not certain and is not claimed in the document caption)
- Date: August 27, 1928
- Repository: Nationaal Archief (GaHetNa), the Dutch National Archives, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BriandKellogg1928b.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Nationaal Archief places this photograph in the public domain with no known copyright restrictions.
- Disclosure: The Commons original is small, 500 by 350 pixels. It prints acceptably at the 3.9 inch width used in the quick check but is softer in detail than the other images in this lesson.
