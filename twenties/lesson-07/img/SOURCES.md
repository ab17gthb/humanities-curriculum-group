# Image Sources for Twenties Lesson 7, The Great Migration and the Harlem Renaissance

All images are real public domain photographs held by the Library of Congress or distributed through Wikimedia Commons, and were downloaded into `lesson-07/assets/` on October 21, 2026. Each download was opened and inspected: the file is a valid JPEG and shows what its caption in the documents says it shows. No image was altered beyond resizing for print.

## 1. garvey-unia-parade-1922.jpg (used in the activity, Station 5)

- Title: Marcus Garvey (1922)
- What it shows: Marcus Garvey in a plumed uniform and medal, seated in an open car during a Universal Negro Improvement Association parade in Harlem, with an aide beside him. Verified by inspection of the downloaded file.
- Creator: Photographer not credited in the Wikimedia Commons category record from which the file was taken
- Date: 1922 (date carried in the Commons file name and record)
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons, file "Marcus Garvey (1922).jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marcus_Garvey_(1922).jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken and published in the United States in 1922, before 1930. Copyright expired.

## 2. garvey-at-desk-1924.jpg (used in the quick check)

- Title: Marcus Garvey, full length, seated at desk
- What it shows: Marcus Garvey seated at a paper covered desk, holding a gavel, in formal dress with a badge on his lapel. Verified against the catalog description and by inspection of the downloaded file.
- Creator: George Grantham Bain Collection (news photograph)
- Date: August 5, 1924
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, George Grantham Bain Collection, reproduction number LC-USZ61-1854, card 2003653533, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Marcus Garvey 1924-08-05.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marcus_Garvey_1924-08-05.jpg
- Public domain basis: Bain Collection news photograph of 1924, published in the United States before 1930 and held by the Library of Congress without copyright restriction. Copyright expired.

## 3. langston-hughes-1936.jpg (used in the activity, Station 4)

- Title: Langston Hughes
- What it shows: Portrait of Langston Hughes in a suit and tie against a backdrop of newspaper pages. Verified by inspection of the downloaded file.
- Creator: Carl Van Vechten
- Date: 1936. DISCLOSURE: this portrait was made in 1936, after the 1920s covered in this lesson. The caption in both documents states the 1936 date and states that Hughes published his first book in 1926. The image is not presented as a 1920s photograph.
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Carl Van Vechten Photographs collection, digital ID cph.3a42821, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Langston Hughes by Carl Van Vechten 1936.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Langston_Hughes_by_Carl_Van_Vechten_1936.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Library of Congress states that there are no known copyright restrictions on the Carl Van Vechten Photographs collection, that the restrictions on the collection expired in 1986, and that it believes the images are in the public domain. The photograph is reproduced without cropping or color change, with credit to the photographer, as the Van Vechten estate requests.

## 4. bessie-smith-1936.jpg (used in the reading)

- Title: Bessie Smith
- What it shows: Portrait of Bessie Smith holding a feather fan. Verified by inspection of the downloaded file, and the portrait matches the catalog records at the Museum of Modern Art and the Beinecke Library for Van Vechten's sitting of February 3, 1936.
- Creator: Carl Van Vechten
- Date: February 3, 1936. DISCLOSURE: a 1936 portrait, after the decade of this lesson, like the Hughes portrait. The caption in the reading states the 1936 date and states what Smith did in 1923. The image is not presented as a 1920s photograph.
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons, file "Bessie Smith (1936) by Carl Van Vechten.jpg"; the sitting is also cataloged by the Museum of Modern Art (Bessie Smith, February 3, 1936) and Yale University Library
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bessie_Smith_(1936)_by_Carl_Van_Vechten.jpg
- Public domain basis: Carl Van Vechten Photographs collection at the Library of Congress, no known copyright restrictions, as in entry 3.
