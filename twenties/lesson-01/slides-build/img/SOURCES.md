# Lesson 1 deck images: sources

All images are real public domain images via Wikimedia Commons or the Library of Congress. No AI-generated or stock-site images. Portrait files are center crops made with PIL from the sources below, resized to 1046 by 1500 pixels for the right-side photo slots in deck_common; the title file is a 16:9 crop resized to 1920 by 1080. Cropping only; subjects kept in frame, nothing stretched.

- `title-169.jpg`: 16:9 derivative crop of the lesson asset `highland-park-assembly-line-1913.jpg`. See `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md` entry 1.
  - Title: Assembly line at the Ford Motor Company's Highland Park plant
  - Creator: Unknown photographer; Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (LCCN2011661021)
  - Date: 1913
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Assembly_line_at_the_Ford_Motor_Company%27s_Highland_Park_plant_LCCN2011661021.jpg
  - Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1913 and held by the Library of Congress with no known restrictions. Wikimedia Commons classifies it as public domain.
  - Note: the photograph dates to 1913, before the 1920s; that date is disclosed in the speaker notes of the title slide.

- `highland-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the same lesson asset `highland-park-assembly-line-1913.jpg`. Same title, creator, date, repository, source URL, and public domain basis as `title-169.jpg` above; see `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md` entry 1. The 1913 date is disclosed in the speaker notes of the slide that uses it.

- `workers-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the lesson asset `ford-assembly-line-workers-1913.jpg`. See `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md` entry 2.
  - Title: Ford assembly line, 1913
  - Creator: Unknown author
  - Date: 1913
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ford_assembly_line_-_1913.jpg
  - Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1913 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons classifies it as public domain.
  - Note: the photograph dates to 1913, before the 1920s; that date is disclosed in the speaker notes of the slide that uses it.

- `modelt-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of a new download for this deck (not a lesson asset).
  - Title: Early Ford automobile
  - Creator: Unknown photographer; Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, glass negative No. 10187 (LCCN2011660900)
  - Date: 1908, as dated in the Library of Congress listing carried by PICRYL
  - Repository: Library of Congress, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Early_Ford_automobile_LCCN2011660900.jpg
  - Download URL used: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Early_Ford_automobile_LCCN2011660900.jpg?width=1600
  - Public domain basis: Glass negative photograph held by the Library of Congress with no known restrictions on publication. Wikimedia Commons classifies it as public domain.
  - Note: the photograph shows an early Model T touring car on display and predates both the moving line and the 1920s; that date gap is disclosed in the speaker notes of the slide that uses it.

- `frigidaire-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the lesson asset `frigidaire-ad-1922.png`. See `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md` entry 3.
  - Title: Frigidaire iceless fridges, 1922 newspaper advertisement
  - Creator: Frigidaire, a product of the General Motors Corporation; placed by Cox and Dance, distributors
  - Date: 1922
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frigidaire_iceless_fridges_1922.png
  - Public domain basis: Advertisement published in the United States in 1922, before 1929. Copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons classifies it as public domain.

- `piggly-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the lesson asset `piggly-wiggly-interior-1918.png`. See `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md` entry 5.
  - Title: Piggly Wiggly store interior, 1918
  - Creator: Clarence Saunders, founder of Piggly Wiggly
  - Date: 1918
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Piggly_Wiggly_store,_1918.png
  - Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1918 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons classifies it as public domain.
  - Note: the photograph dates to 1918, before the 1920s; it shows the self service chain store form the 1920s adopted at scale, and that date and reason are disclosed in the speaker notes of the slide that uses it.

- `radio-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of a new download for this deck (not a lesson asset).
  - Title: Family group listening to radio?
  - Creator: Harris and Ewing, photographer
  - Date: 1921, per the Library of Congress catalog record (LCCN2016894380)
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Family_group_listening_to_radio%3F_LCCN2016894380.jpg
  - Download: 1920px thumbnail served by the Wikimedia Commons API imageinfo record for the file.
  - Public domain basis: Glass negative photograph taken in 1921 and held by the Library of Congress. Wikimedia Commons classifies it as public domain.
  - Note: the family listens around a horn speaker set while one boy wears headphones, the normal home radio arrangement of the first broadcasting years.

Not used: the lesson asset `woolworth-store-chicago-1928.jpg` (830 by 533 pixels) was not cropped for this deck because its resolution is too low for a full-height portrait slot; the Piggly Wiggly interior carries the chain store section instead.
