# The Twenties Unit: Build Manifest

Every finished item, its file, its page count, and the test-blueprint entries it teaches or assesses. Blueprint numbers are from twenties-skeleton.md. Every document ships as an HTML source with a PDF of the same name, built with templates/build_pdf.py and render-checked before listing. The full unit is 51 documents and 228 printed pages, plus 8 slide decks (217 slides) with a printable teacher notes document for each deck.

**Unit essential question:** How did the prosperity of the 1920s change American life, and why was that prosperity weaker than it looked?
