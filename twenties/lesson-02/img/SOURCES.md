# Image Sources for Twenties Lesson 2, Mass Media and the Celebrity Age

All images are real public domain images held by the Library of Congress, the Museum of History and Industry (Seattle), or published in the United States in the 1920s, and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-02/assets/` on October 21, 2026. No image was altered beyond resizing for print. Each entry below states what the image actually shows and its catalog date. Two disclosures apply. First, no verified public domain scan of a 1927 tabloid front page was used: Activity Station 3 therefore presents the front page as a labeled summary rather than as a reproduced page. Second, the radio listening image shows listeners at a receiver with headphones, not a family group around a cabinet set, and its caption says so.
