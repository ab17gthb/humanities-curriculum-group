# Image Sources for The Twenties Lesson 8, The Weak Spots and the Crash

All images are real public domain images held by the Library of Congress, the State Archives of Florida (Florida Memory), or the Museum of History and Industry, and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-08/assets/` on October 21, 2026. Each download was opened and verified as a valid image, and its content and date were checked against its catalog record. No image was altered beyond resizing for print. No Farm Security Administration photographs are used in this lesson; every photograph dates from the 1920s or, for the crash photograph, from October 1929 itself.

## 1. beltsville-farm-1924.jpg (used in Activity Station 1)

- Title: Beltsville, (Md.), farm, (8-18-24)
- What it shows: Farm buildings and fields at Beltsville, Maryland, photographed from across an open field
- Creator: National Photo Company
- Date: August 18, 1924
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LCCN 2016849376
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beltsville,_(Md.),_farm,_(8-18-24)_LCCN2016849376.jpg
- Public domain basis: National Photo Company photograph published in the United States in 1924. Copyright expired; the Library of Congress places no restriction on its use.
