---
Act as an experienced U.S. history teacher. Create a printable worksheet activity for high school students on [TOPIC].

ACTIVITY STRUCTURE
Title it "[TITLE]."
Part 1, "Four Stops." Create four short stations. Each station has: (a) a short source, either a real historical excerpt of 40 to 70 words or a description of a real photograph, with the author or photographer, the work, and the year; and (b) two questions, one asking what the source shows, and one asking who was affected and how.
Part 2, "Your Verdict." A closing task that makes students rank, judge, or decide using evidence from at least two stations, in a short paragraph.
End with a teacher answer key on its own page, with short model answers for every question and a brief rubric for the closing task.

SOURCE RULE
Use only real excerpts you can attribute accurately. If you are not certain of an exact quotation, paraphrase it and label it "Summary of" instead of using quotation marks. Do not invent quotations, statistics, or sources.

FORMAT RULES (this will be printed on a school copier)
- First line of the page: Name, Date, and Period with blanks, spread across the full width of the page.
- Then the title, then one or two sentences of directions to students.
- Plain black text only. No color, no shaded boxes, no decorative elements.
- Do not print ruled lines for answers. Leave open blank space after each question, about an inch for short answers and two inches for the paragraph.
- Student pages: two pages maximum. Formal, direct wording. No em dashes anywhere.
