# Lesson 1 deck images: sources

All images are real public domain images via Wikimedia Commons, the Library of Congress, the U.S. Naval History and Heritage Command, or the Hawaii State Archives. No AI-generated or stock-site images. Portrait files are center crops made with PIL from the lesson assets below, resized to 1046 by 1500 pixels for the right-side photo slots in deck_common; the title file is a 16:9 crop resized to 1920 by 1080. Cropping only; subjects kept in frame, nothing stretched. Full records for every source image are in `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md`.

  - Creator: Unknown photographer, U.S. Navy
  - Date: Summer 1898
