# Image Sources for Imperialism and World War I Lesson 1, The Turn Overseas

All images are real public domain images held by the Library of Congress, the U.S. Naval History and Heritage Command, the Hawaii State Archives, or HathiTrust, and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-01/img/` on October 21, 2026. No image was altered beyond resizing for print. Where an image's date differs from the date of the subject it illustrates, the caption in the student document discloses the difference.

## 1. uss-indiana-1898.jpg (used in the activity introduction)

- Title: USS Indiana (BB-1), Naval History and Heritage Command photograph NH 61210
- What it is: A photograph of the battleship USS Indiana underway. Indiana was one of the first three battleships authorized by the Naval Act of 1890.
- Creator: Unknown photographer, U.S. Navy
- Date: Summer 1898
- Repository: U.S. Naval History and Heritage Command, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:USS_Indiana_(BB-1)_-_NH_61210.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken by or for the United States Navy in 1898; a work of the U.S. federal government, classified public domain by Wikimedia Commons.

## 2. mahan-portrait-1904.jpg (used in Activity Station 1)

- Title: Alfred Thayer Mahan, head-and-shoulders portrait, facing front
- What it is: A studio portrait of Alfred Thayer Mahan, the naval officer whose 1890 book Station 1 quotes.
- Creator: J. E. Purdy, Boston, Massachusetts
- Date: About 1904
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (digital ID cph.3c20219), via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alfred-Thayer-Mahan.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph made about 1904 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.
- Disclosure: The portrait postdates Mahan's 1890 book by about fourteen years. The student caption says so and presents the image as a portrait of the author, not as an image of the 1890 debate.

## 3. chrisman-sisters-sod-house.jpg (used in Activity Station 2)

- Title: Chrisman Sisters by Solomon Butcher
- What it is: The four Chrisman sisters posed in front of their family's sod house in Custer County, Nebraska, a frontier settlement photograph of the kind Turner's readers would have recognized.
- Creator: Solomon D. Butcher
- Date: 1886 (Library of Congress dating, from the Nebraska State Historical Society collection, digital ID nbhips lca 106 10604v). The Wikimedia Commons file page dates the same photograph to 1887.
- Repository: Library of Congress (American Memory, Built in America / Nebraska history collections), via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chrisman_sisters_by_Solomon_Butcher.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1886 or 1887 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.
- Disclosure: The one-year disagreement between the Library of Congress dating (1886) and the Wikimedia Commons file page (1887) is stated in the student caption and recorded here. No other copy of the photograph with a firmer date was verified.

## 4. honolulu-annexation-1898.jpg (used in Activity Station 3)

- Title: American Blue Jackets and Marines from the USS Philadelphia landed at Honolulu Harbor to take part in the Annexation Day ceremonies
- What it is: American sailors and Marines landing at Honolulu Harbor on August 12, 1898, for the ceremonies transferring Hawaii to the United States at Iolani Palace.
- Creator: Attributed to the Hawaii State Archives (photographer not named)
- Date: August 12, 1898
- Repository: Hawaii State Archives, via the University of Hawaii annexation collection and Wikimedia Commons; the Commons file was scanned from Onipa'a: Five Days in the History of the Hawaiian Nation, the centennial observance volume on the overthrow
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:American_Blue_Jackets_and_Marines_from_the_USS_Philadelphia_landed_at_Honolulu_Harbor_to_take_part_in_the_Annexation_Day_ceremonies.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1898. Wikimedia Commons classifies it as public domain.
- Disclosure: The photograph shows the outcome of the annexation drive (August 1898), not the drive itself. The student caption says so, noting that it was taken five years after the overthrow of the Hawaiian monarchy.

## 7. liliuokalani-1891.jpg (used in the quick check)

- Title: Liliuokalani, c. 1891
- What it is: A signed gelatin silver portrait photograph of Liliuokalani, the last sovereign of the Hawaiian Kingdom. The sheet carries her 1916 inscription to Josephus Daniels, written after her overthrow; the Commons file is a digital restoration by Adam Cuerden of the Library of Congress print.
- Creator: James J. Williams, Honolulu (digital restoration by Adam Cuerden)
- Date: About 1891 (inscription dated October 1916)
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (digital ID ppmsca.53150), via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Liliuokalani,_c._1891.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken about 1891 in Hawaii and published before 1929. Copyright expired; Wikimedia Commons classifies the file as public domain.
- Disclosure: The student caption notes that the photograph was signed and inscribed by the Queen in 1916, after her overthrow, so students do not read the inscription as part of the 1891 sitting.
