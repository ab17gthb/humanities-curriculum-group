# Lesson 3 image sources

All images are real public-domain works held by the Library of Congress or distributed through Wikimedia Commons. Files are stored in `../assets/` and referenced by the lesson documents.

## aguinaldo-1898.jpg
- Title: Emilio Aguinaldo, ca. 1898 (portrait)
- Creator: Unknown photographer
- Date: About 1898; published in 1899 in the book War in the Philippines
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons, file "Emilio Aguinaldo (ca. 1898).jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Emilio%20Aguinaldo%20(ca.%201898).jpg
- Public-domain basis: Published in 1899; public domain because of age, per the Commons file page. Note: the scan held by Commons is small (209 by 299 pixels), so the portrait prints small by design in the activity; the caption carries the about 1898 date rather than presenting the portrait as dated 1899, the year of the debate.

## school-begins-puck.jpg
- Title: "School Begins"
- Creator: Louis Dalrymple (1866-1905), cartoon published in Puck magazine, vol. 44, no. 1144, January 25, 1899
- Date: January 25, 1899
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, via Wikimedia Commons, file "School Begins (Puck Magazine 1-25-1899).jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/School%20Begins%20(Puck%20Magazine%201-25-1899).jpg
- Public-domain basis: Published in the United States in 1899; public domain in the United States (published before January 1, 1931). Disclosure carried in the quick check itself: the cartoon draws its Filipino, Hawaiian, Puerto Rican, and Cuban figures as racial caricatures, the standard style of American political cartooning in 1899, and the teacher key names that fact for students.
