# Lesson 3 slide deck image sources

All images are real public-domain works from the Library of Congress or Wikimedia Commons. Files in this folder are crops made with PIL from the lesson assets in `../../assets/` (see `../../img/SOURCES.md` for the full lesson image record); crops use the deck system's 16:9 title format or its right-side portrait slot and are never stretched.

## school-port.jpg
- Title: "School Begins" (portrait crop)
- Creator: Louis Dalrymple (1866-1905), cartoon published in Puck magazine, vol. 44, no. 1144
- Date: January 25, 1899
- Repository: Derivative crop of the lesson asset `../../assets/school-begins-puck.jpg`; Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/School%20Begins%20(Puck%20Magazine%201-25-1899).jpg
- Public-domain basis: Published in the United States in 1899; public domain in the United States. Disclosure carried in the deck's speaker notes and in the lesson quick check: the cartoon draws its Filipino, Hawaiian, Puerto Rican, and Cuban figures as racial caricatures, the standard style of American political cartooning in 1899.

## aguinaldo-port.jpg
- Title: Emilio Aguinaldo, ca. 1898 (portrait, used at native size)
- Creator: Unknown photographer
- Date: About 1898; published in 1899 in the book War in the Philippines
- Repository: The lesson asset `../../assets/aguinaldo-1898.jpg` itself; Wikimedia Commons, file "Emilio Aguinaldo (ca. 1898).jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Emilio%20Aguinaldo%20(ca.%201898).jpg
- Public-domain basis: Published in 1899; public domain because of age, per the Commons file page. Disclosure carried in the deck's speaker notes: the surviving scan is small (209 by 299 pixels), so the portrait projects soft by design; the about 1898 date is stated rather than presenting the portrait as dated 1899.
