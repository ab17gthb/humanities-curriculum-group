# Image Sources: Imperialism and World War I Lesson 2, The Spanish-American War (slide deck)

All images are real public-domain works. No image was generated. Crops and resizes only, made with PIL; subjects were never stretched. Full entries for the three lesson images live in `lesson-02/img/SOURCES.md`; the entries below record the deck derivatives and the deck-only downloads.

## 1. title-169.jpg (lesson asset derivative)

- **Title:** Our Victorious Fleets in Cuban Waters
- **Creator:** Currier and Ives (publisher)
- **Date:** 1898
- **Repository:** Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (item 2001700391)
- **Source URL:** https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/pnp/pga/00800/00849v.jpg (item page: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2001700391/)
- **Public-domain basis:** Published in the United States in 1898, before January 1, 1931; copyright expired.
- **Derivative:** 16:9 center crop of `lesson-02/img/our-victorious-fleets.jpg` for the title slide.

## 2. gillam-port.jpg (lesson asset derivative)

- **Title:** Remember the Maine! And Don't Forget the Starving Cubans!
- **Creator:** Victor Gillam
- **Date:** May 7, 1898 (published in *Judge* magazine)
- **Repository:** Wikimedia Commons; also carried in the audited TPSP bank (record id `gillam-remember-the-maine-1898`)
- **Source URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0c/Remember_the_Maine%21_And_Don%27t_Forget_the_Starving_Cubans%21_-_Victor_Gillam.jpg
- **Public-domain basis:** Published in the United States in 1898, before January 1, 1931; copyright expired.
- **Derivative:** portrait crop (1046x1500) of `lesson-02/img/remember-the-maine-gillam.jpg` centered on Uncle Sam and the open album.

## 3. maine-port.jpg (NEW download, derivative crop)

- **Title:** Wreck of the U.S.S. Maine, ca. 1898
- **Creator:** Photographer not named in the archival record (United States government photograph)
- **Date:** About 1898
- **Repository:** National Archives (NARA record 512929), via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Maine_(1890) (the article carries the NARA photograph of the sunken Maine in Havana harbor)
- **Public-domain basis:** Photograph taken about 1898 and held by the National Archives; published before January 1, 1931; copyright expired.
- **Derivative:** portrait crop (1046x1500) centered on the wreck and its standing mast.

## 4. mckinley-port.jpg (NEW download, derivative crop)

- **Title:** William McKinley, head-and-shoulders portrait, facing right
- **Creator:** Author not listed in the file record
- **Date:** Prior to his death in 1901 (per the Wikimedia Commons file record)
- **Repository:** Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/File:William_mckinley.jpg
- **Public-domain basis:** The Commons file record marks the image PD-US: a United States work published before 1931; copyright expired. The file record also notes the hosted version is retouched (cropped, contrast, slight sepia) from its original.
- **Derivative:** portrait crop (1046x1500) keeping McKinley's head and shoulders in frame.

## 5. dewey-port.jpg (NEW download, derivative crop)

- **Title:** Admiral Dewey at the Battle of Manila (NH 84510-KN)
- **Creator:** Contemporary colored print, artist not named in the Naval Historical Center record
- **Date:** Contemporary print of the Battle of Manila Bay, 1 May 1898
- **Repository:** U.S. Naval Historical Center (now Naval History and Heritage Command), courtesy of the Naval Historical Foundation, Collection of C.J. Dutreaux, via Wikimedia Commons (file: Admiral Dewey painting NH 84510-KN.jpg, 525 x 715)
- **Source URL:** http://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/OnlineLibrary/photos/images/h84000/h84510kc.htm
- **Public-domain basis:** Contemporary 1898 print reproduced as a U.S. Naval Historical Center photograph; published before January 1, 1931; copyright expired.
- **Derivative:** portrait crop (1046x1500) of the full print, which depicts Commodore George Dewey on the bridge of USS Olympia during the battle.

## 6. rough-port.jpg (lesson asset derivative)

- **Title:** Colonel Roosevelt and his Rough Riders at the top of the hill which they captured, Battle of San Juan
- **Creator:** William Dinwiddie (1867 to 1934), photographer
- **Date:** 1898. Posed group portrait on the captured hilltop, taken after the capture and commonly dated to about July 3, 1898; this dating is disclosed in the speaker notes of the slide that uses it, as in the printed lesson.
- **Repository:** Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LCCN 96521936, via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a3/Colonel_Roosevelt_and_his_Rough_Riders_at_the_top_of_the_hill_which_they_captured%2C_Battle_of_San_Juan%29_-_by_William_Dinwiddie_LCCN96521936.jpg
- **Public-domain basis:** Photograph taken in 1898 and published in the United States before January 1, 1931; copyright expired. The lesson file is itself cropped from the LOC scan to remove the archival color target, as recorded in `lesson-02/img/SOURCES.md`.
- **Derivative:** portrait crop (1046x1500) of `lesson-02/img/rough-riders-dinwiddie.jpg` centered on Roosevelt and the troopers.

- **Title:** Battle of Manila Bay
- **Creator:** James Gale Tyler (J. G. Tyler)
