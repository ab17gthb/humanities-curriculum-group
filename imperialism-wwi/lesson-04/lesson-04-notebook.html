The pattern. None of these instruments made the place a formal American colony. In one or two sentences, explain what the United States held in each case instead of a colony, using two rows as evidence.

Teacher Answer Key

InstrumentWhere usedHow it projected powerCost on the receiving end
Diplomatic notes (the Open Door)China, 1899 and 1900.Hay's notes of September 6, 1899, asked each power for equal trade treatment inside its sphere of influence, and the note of July 3, 1900, asked the powers to preserve China as one country. American trade gained access with no territory to govern.China was not consulted as an equal. After the Boxer Rebellion the Boxer Protocol of 1901 charged China an indemnity of about 333 million dollars over 39 years.
Armed expeditionChina, 1900.United States troops joined the eight-nation expedition that fought to Beijing in August 1900 and lifted the siege of the legations.Chinese Christians died in the tens of thousands. Boxers and Chinese soldiers died in large numbers, and foreign troops occupied the capital region.
Informal bargainJapan and San Francisco, 1907 and 1908.Under the Gentlemen's Agreement, Japan stopped issuing passports to laborers bound for the mainland United States, and San Francisco withdrew its school segregation order. No law and no treaty were needed.Japanese families lost the chance to migrate for work, and discrimination continued: California barred Japanese immigrants from owning farmland in 1913.
A canal and a zonePanama, 1903 to 1914.United States warships protected Panama's independence from Colombia in 1903, and the treaty gave the United States a canal zone ten miles wide. The canal, opened August 15, 1914, moved the navy and trade between the oceans in weeks.Colombia lost the province of Panama. About 5,600 workers, most of them West Indian laborers, died during the American building years, by the official count.
Customs control under a police claimThe Dominican Republic, 1905, under the Roosevelt Corollary of 1904.The Corollary claimed an international police power for the United States in cases of chronic wrongdoing or unpaid debts. In 1905 the United States took over Dominican customhouses and paid foreign creditors a fixed share.Dominicans lost control of the revenue their government depended on, and the claim was later used to justify occupations of Cuba, Nicaragua, Haiti, and the Dominican Republic.
Loans backed by forceNicaragua, 1909 to 1912 and after.Under dollar diplomacy the United States backed the overthrow of President Zelaya in 1909, arranged American bank loans, and placed an American collector over Nicaraguan customs.When Nicaraguans revolted in 1912, about 2,500 Marines and sailors landed to protect the government the loans supported, and a Marine guard stayed for years.

The pattern. Strong answers name what the United States held instead of a colony: promises about trade in China, a bargain about migration with Japan, a zone and a waterway in Panama, the customhouses of Caribbean governments, and the loans and customs revenue of Nicaragua. In each case the United States controlled the thing it needed, trade access, a route, or a government's income, while the formal government of the place stayed in local hands and local people paid the costs.
