# Lesson 5 image sources

All images are public domain and are held in lesson-05/img/.

- `lusitania-port.jpg`
  - Title: RMS Lusitania coming into port, possibly in New York, 1907-13 (cropped version)
  - Creator: George Grantham Bain (Bain News Service)
  - Date: between 1907 and 1913
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, George Grantham Bain Collection (digital ID cph.3g13287)
  - Source URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d9/RMS_Lusitania_coming_into_port%2C_possibly_in_New_York%2C_1907-13-crop.jpg
  - Public-domain basis: Photograph from the Bain News Service, published in the United States between 1907 and 1913; copyright expired. Library of Congress Bain Collection photographs carry no known restrictions on publication.
  - Disclosure: The photograph predates the sinking. It shows the Lusitania in service between 1907 and 1913; the ship was sunk on May 7, 1915. Every caption that uses this image states that the photograph was taken before the sinking.

- `zimmermann-telegram.jpg`
  - Title: Zimmermann Telegram as Received by the German Ambassador to Mexico
  - Creator: German Foreign Office (telegram as transmitted); copy held by the U.S. National Archives
  - Date: January 19, 1917 (date of the copy as received in Mexico)
  - Repository: U.S. National Archives and Records Administration (NARA ID 302025)
  - Source URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/Zimmermann_Telegram_as_Received_by_the_German_Ambassador_to_Mexico_-_NARA_-_302025.jpg
  - Public-domain basis: A 1917 government telegram held by the U.S. National Archives; public domain.

- `preparedness-parade.jpg`
  - Title: Preparedness parade, Wash., D.C., 1916
  - Creator: National Photo Company
  - Date: 1916
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, National Photo Company Collection (LCCN 2016852541)
  - Source URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/40/Preparedness_parade%2C_Wash.%2C_D.C.%2C_1916_LCCN2016852541.jpg
  - Public-domain basis: Photograph published in the United States in 1916; copyright expired. Library of Congress National Photo Company photographs carry no known restrictions on publication.

- `wilson-congress.jpg`
  - Title: President Woodrow Wilson addressing Congress
  - Creator: Photographer not identified in the Library of Congress catalog record
  - Date: 1917
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (LCCN 95504407)
  - Source URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5c/President_Woodrow_Wilson_addressing_Congress_LCCN95504407.jpg
  - Public-domain basis: Photograph published in the United States in 1917; copyright expired.
  - Disclosure: The Library of Congress catalog dates this photograph to 1917 and titles it as Wilson addressing Congress; the catalog record does not name the specific address, so captions in these materials do not claim it shows the war message of April 2, 1917 specifically.
