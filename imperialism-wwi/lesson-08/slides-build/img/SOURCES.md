# Slide Image Sources: Imperialism and World War I Lesson 8

Derived crops for the slide deck, each made from a verified public-domain original in the lesson img/ folder (see ../img/SOURCES.md for full records). Cropping and resizing only. No image postdates its subject.

## title-169.jpg and big-four-port.jpg
- Derived from: big-four.jpg, Council of Four at the Paris Peace Conference
- Creator: Edward N. Jackson, United States Army Signal Corps
- Date: May 27, 1919
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons (file: Big four.jpg); original in United States Army Signal Corps records, held through the Library of Congress
- Identification, left to right in the full photograph: David Lloyd George (Britain), Vittorio Orlando (Italy), Georges Clemenceau (France), Woodrow Wilson (United States)
- title-169.jpg is a 16:9 crop for the title slide. big-four-port.jpg is a portrait crop centered on Clemenceau and Wilson for the right-side photo layout.

## orpen-port.jpg
- Derived from: signing-of-peace-orpen.jpg, The Signing of Peace in the Hall of Mirrors, Versailles, 28th June 1919
- Creator: William Orpen (1878 to 1931), official British war artist
- Date: 1919
- Repository: Imperial War Museums, via Wikimedia Commons
- Portrait center crop for the right-side photo layout.

## harding-port.jpg
- Derived from: harding-1920.jpg, Warren G. Harding, head-and-shoulders portrait, facing front
- Creator: Moffett Studio, Chicago
- Date: June 21, 1920
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LC-USZ62-110722, via Wikimedia Commons
- Portrait center crop for the right-side photo layout.
