# Lesson 6 deck images: sources

All photographs are real public-domain works. No AI-generated or stock-site images. Portrait files are crops of the sources named, made with PIL at the deck system's right-side ratio (about 0.697); subjects were kept in frame and nothing was stretched. Disclosures printed in the lesson documents are carried into the deck's speaker notes wherever they apply.

- `title-169.jpg` (16:9 crop, upscaled from the 740px source) and `convoy-port.jpg` (portrait crop)
  - Title: Twenty-five Ship Convoy, nearing Brest, France, on 1 November 1918 (Naval Historical Center photograph NH 158)
  - Creator: Robert W. Neeser, photographer, from USS Rambler (SP-211)
  - Date: November 1, 1918
  - Repository: Naval Historical Center (Naval History and Heritage Command), photograph NH 158, via the Online Library of Selected Images, ibiblio mirror
  - Source URL: https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/OnlineLibrary/photos/images/h00001/h00158c.htm
  - Public-domain basis: lesson asset derivative; see the entry for img/convoy-brest.jpg in lesson-06/img/SOURCES.md. Photograph by a United States Navy photographer on duty in 1918; a work of the United States federal government, public domain under 17 U.S.C. 105. The source file is only 740px wide, so both crops are softer than the deck's other photographs.

  - Title: In the Argonnes. Dugouts (Bufa 8622)
  - Creator: German official photograph, First World War (as printed on the National Archives card in the source file)
  - Date: First World War (filed in the National Archives' Argonne material; no narrower date is printed on the card, and none is claimed in the deck)
  - Repository: National Archives and Records Administration (NARA 17391858), via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:In_the_Argonnes._Dugouts_-_NARA_-_17391858.jpg
  - Public-domain basis: German official photograph of the First World War held by the U.S. National Archives; public domain in the United States. The deck's speaker notes identify it as a German official photograph showing dugouts in the Argonne; it is not presented as an American position.

- `hellfighters-port.jpg` (portrait crop)
  - Title: Soldiers of the 369th (15th New York) awarded the Croix de Guerre for gallantry in action
  - Creator: Photographer not credited; Records of the War Department General and Special Staffs (165-WW-127-8)
  - Date: February 12, 1919
  - Repository: National Archives, Records of the War Department, 165-WW-127-8, via Wikimedia
  - Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Harlem_Hellfighters_Soldiers_of_the_369th_(15th_New_York,_1919)_Awarded_the_Croix_de_Guerre_Medal_for_Gallantry_in_Action.jpeg
  - Public-domain basis: lesson asset derivative; see the entry for img/hellfighters-369th.jpg in lesson-06/img/SOURCES.md. Work of the United States federal government, public domain under 17 U.S.C. 105.
  - Date disclosure (carried into the deck's speaker notes): This photograph was taken on February 12, 1919, after the armistice of November 11, 1918. It shows nine decorated soldiers of the 369th Infantry aboard their transport home, not in combat.

- `armistice-port.jpg` (portrait crop, new download)
  - Title: Allied delegation outside Foch's railway carriage after reaching agreement for the armistice, Forest of Compiegne
  - Creator: Unknown author (anonymous press photograph, published worldwide)
  - Date: 11 November 1918
  - Repository: Wikimedia Commons; the Commons file page traces the image to a press photo published all over the world, reproduced in Jan Dabrowski, "Wielka wojna 1914-1918" (Warsaw, 1937)
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Armisticetrain_(slight_crop).jpg
  - Public-domain basis: Anonymous work published in 1918; copyright expired, public domain as stated on the Commons file page. The file is only 504px wide, so the crop is softer than the deck's other photographs. Foch stands second from the right, as the Commons description and the armistice literature agree.
