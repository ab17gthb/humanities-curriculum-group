# Image Sources: Imperialism and World War I Lesson 6, Americans in the War

All photographs are real public-domain works of the United States federal government. The one diagram is an original teaching diagram drawn for this lesson. No image is presented as something it is not.

- Title: Twenty-five Ship Convoy, nearing Brest, France, on 1 November 1918 (Naval Historical Center photograph NH 158)
- Creator: Robert W. Neeser, photographer, from USS Rambler (SP-211)
- Date: November 1, 1918
- Repository: Naval Historical Center (Naval History and Heritage Command), photograph NH 158, via the Online Library of Selected Images, ibiblio mirror
- Source URL: https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/OnlineLibrary/photos/images/h00001/h00158c.htm (picture data page; image file h00158.jpg in the same directory)

## img/hellfighters-369th.jpg (used in lesson-06-quick-check.html)
- Title: Soldiers of the 369th (15th New York) awarded the Croix de Guerre for gallantry in action
- Creator: Photographer not credited; Records of the War Department General and Special Staffs (165-WW-127-8)
- Date: February 12, 1919
- Repository: National Archives, Records of the War Department, 165-WW-127-8, via Wikimedia (English Wikipedia file page)
- Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Harlem_Hellfighters_Soldiers_of_the_369th_(15th_New_York,_1919)_Awarded_the_Croix_de_Guerre_Medal_for_Gallantry_in_Action.jpeg
- Public-domain basis: Work of the United States federal government, public domain under 17 U.S.C. 105, as stated on the file page.
- Date disclosure: This photograph was taken on February 12, 1919, after the armistice of November 11, 1918. It shows nine decorated soldiers of the 369th Infantry aboard their transport home. The quick check caption states this disclosure in print: the photograph shows the regiment coming home, not in combat.

## Quoted sources

No verbatim quotations appear in this lesson. The soldier's letter station (Activity, Station 4) and the camp influenza report text (Activity, Station 5) are labeled summaries written for the lesson, and each station states that status on its source line and, for Station 4, in its teacher key. The draft record (Activity, Station 1) is a labeled teaching sample following the standard Selective Service registration and classification steps, with a composite sample draftee, and states that status on its source line. Figures in the lesson follow the standard published accounting: Selective Service registration and induction figures, Army strength in France, Army battle and nonbattle death figures, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention estimate of about 675,000 United States influenza deaths, and worldwide influenza deaths stated as an estimate of at least 50 million. All estimates are labeled as estimates in the student text.
