The war in one paragraph. Using at least three rows of the chart, write a paragraph that answers the lesson question. How did containment lead the United States into Vietnam, and why did the war fail on its own terms?

Teacher Answer Key

StepWhat the United States addedThe reason givenWhat it cost
Division and refusal, 1954 to 1956Backing for Diem's South Vietnam after the Geneva division at the seventeenth parallel, and support for refusing the 1956 reunification elections.The domino theory: if one country fell to communism its neighbors would fall one after another.A permanent commitment to one half of a divided country, against an insurgency supplied from the other half.
The planned resolution, 1964The Bundy ten step plan in May, then the Gulf of Tonkin Resolution in August, giving the president authority to take all necessary measures in Southeast Asia.Reported attacks on American destroyers and the defense of South Vietnam's freedom, with American ambitions in the area expressly denied.Congress's war power, granted on the August 2 attack and a reported second attack that almost certainly never happened.
Escalation, 1965 to 1968Rolling Thunder bombing and ground troops from March 1965, growing past 184,000 by the end of 1965 and past half a million by 1968.Holding South Vietnam as the standing domino, with progress reported in body counts.A draft that fell hardest on the poor and the young, and a war measured by enemy dead rather than by ground held.
Tet and the turn, 1968No new commitment. The offensive subtracted the administration's credibility; Johnson limited the bombing and left the 1968 race.The attackers sought an uprising and the collapse of the Saigon government. Neither came.Attacker losses were very heavy and the Viet Cong never recovered. American belief in official claims of progress never recovered either.
Vietnamization to Saigon, 1969 to 1975Staged American withdrawal with increased training and equipment for South Vietnam's forces, a widened war in Cambodia and Laos, and a draft run by lottery.The defense of freedom is particularly the responsibility of the people whose freedom is threatened.Four more years of war, four students dead at Kent State, and an ending in April 1975 on the question the commitment began with, settled against the United States.

The war in one paragraph. Strong answers run the chart as one sequence. Containment, stated as the domino theory, turned a divided Vietnam into a line the United States said it could not let fall. The line was then held by steps, a planned resolution granted on disputed reports, a force grown past half a million, and a slower exit that widened the war while shrinking the American part of it. The war failed on its own terms because the aim was a standing South Vietnam, and on April 30, 1975 South Vietnam ceased to exist while its neighbors did not fall in the predicted sequence. A paragraph that uses only the buildup or only the ending has answered half the lesson question.
