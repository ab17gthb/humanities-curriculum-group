## Note on a photograph not used
The famous rooftop evacuation photograph of 29 April 1975 was taken by Hubert van Es for United Press International and is not a public domain United States government work, so it is not used anywhere in this lesson. The evacuation is documented instead with the United States Navy photograph listed last above, captioned for what it shows.
