Teacher Answer Key

StepWhat happenedAnalysis, doctrine, or actionThe evidence
The alliance breaks, 1945 to 1946The Yalta pledge of free elections broke in Poland and across Eastern Europe, and Soviet pressure reached Iran and Turkey.The ground the strategy answeredSoviet armies held Eastern Europe where they stopped in 1945, and Moscow installed a government of its choosing in Poland.
The Long Telegram and the Iron Curtain speech, 1946Kennan cabled that Soviet expansion grew from ideology and insecurity and yielded to firm resistance. Churchill named the iron curtain at Fulton.AnalysisSoviet power is highly sensitive to logic of force and usually withdraws when strong resistance is encountered at any point.
The Truman Doctrine, 1947Truman asked Congress for four hundred million dollars for Greece and Turkey and stated the policy as a worldwide rule.DoctrineIt must be the policy of the United States to support free peoples resisting attempted subjugation by armed minorities or by outside pressures.
The Marshall Plan, 1947 to 1952The United States gave about thirteen billion dollars to rebuild Western Europe. Moscow barred its satellites from taking part.Action, economicThe policy was directed not against any country or doctrine but against hunger, poverty, desperation, and chaos.
The Berlin Airlift, 1948 to 1949The Soviet Union blockaded West Berlin's land routes, and the Western powers supplied about two million people by air for eleven months until the blockade was lifted.Action, under testThe blockade closed every road, railway, and canal to the western sectors, and it ended in May 1949 having gained nothing.
NATO and the second bomb, 1949NATO bound the United States, Canada, and Western Europe in the first peacetime alliance in American history. Germany divided into two states, and the Soviet Union tested an atomic bomb.Action, military and politicalAn attack on one member was pledged to count as an attack on all, and the American atomic monopoly ended in August 1949.

The strategy in one sentence. Strong answers name the build in order and by kind. The United States built containment by turning Kennan's analysis into a public doctrine in 1947, giving the doctrine an economic instrument in the Marshall Plan, proving it under test at Berlin, and fixing it in a peacetime alliance in 1949, all before the Soviet bomb made the contest a nuclear one.
