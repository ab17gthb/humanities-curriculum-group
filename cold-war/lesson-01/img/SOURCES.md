# Image Sources: Lesson 1, Allies to Adversaries: Containment Takes Shape

## The Berlin Airlift (berlin-airlift.jpg; deck crop berlin-port.jpg; document copy in assets/)
- Title: C-54 landing at Tempelhof
- Creator: Henry Ries, United States Air Force
- Date: 1948
- Holder: Library of Congress, via Wikimedia Commons; the Commons description records that during the Berlin Airlift of 1948 and 1949, United States, British, and French planes delivered food and other goods to blockaded Berlin, and that German children stand on a hillside watching a United States plane fly overhead
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:C-54_landing_at_Tempelhof.jpg
- Public domain basis: Commons file page marks the image Public domain as a work of the United States Air Force.
