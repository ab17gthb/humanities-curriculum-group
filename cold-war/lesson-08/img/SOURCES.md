# Image Sources for Cold War Lesson 8, The Wall Falls: The Cold War Ends

All images are real public domain photographs held by the National Archives, the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library, or the United States Department of Defense and distributed through Wikimedia Commons. They were downloaded on October 22, 2026. Each download was opened and verified with PIL. Document copies are stored in the lesson's assets folder and img folder; deck versions are cropped to portrait (1046 by 1500) or to 16:9 for the title slide and stored in slides-build/img; no other alteration.

## 1. brandenburg-gate-1989.jpg (reading figure, activity Station 3, quick check figure; assets copy; deck portrait crop gate-port.jpg and deck title image title-169.jpg)

- Title: Crowds at the Brandenburg Gate, Berlin, December 1989 (photograph), as titled in the audited TPSP bank record corkran-berlin-wall-1989; the Commons file page image title reads: Crowds throng around the Brandenburg Gate following the structure's official opening on December 22nd
- What it shows: Crowds thronging around the Brandenburg Gate in Berlin on December 1, 1989. The Commons description states the gate is already freely accessible from the East Berlin side, and the crossing to the Western side at the gate opened officially on December 22, 1989.
- Creator: SSgt. F. Lee Corkran, United States Air Force, as stated on the Commons file page and in the bank record's sourceNote
- Date: December 1, 1989
- Holder: United States Department of Defense (DoD photo, USA, per the Commons file page; the file page metadata gives the short title DF-ST-91-03559), via Wikimedia Commons, file "BrandenburgerTorDezember1989.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BrandenburgerTorDezember1989.jpg
- Public domain basis: The photograph is a work of the United States federal government, a Department of Defense photograph by a United States Air Force photographer, as shown on the Commons file page (DoD photo, USA; author SSGT F. Lee Corkran).
- Bank record: This is the audited TPSP bank image, record id corkran-berlin-wall-1989, whose sourceNote reads: SSgt. F. Lee Corkran, U.S. Air Force, 'Crowds at the Brandenburg Gate, Berlin,' December 1, 1989 (U.S. Department of Defense; image described).

## 2. reagan-brandenburg-1987.jpg (deck portrait crop reagan-port.jpg)

- Title: Photograph of President Reagan giving a speech at the Berlin Wall, Brandenburg Gate, Federal Republic of Germany
- What it shows: President Ronald Reagan giving his speech at the Berlin Wall, Brandenburg Gate, West Berlin, June 12, 1987.
- Creator: White House Photographic Office, as the Reagan Library photographic office record for the June 12, 1987 Berlin remarks; the Wikimedia Commons category for the 1987 Berlin speech lists this photograph under National Archives identifier 198585
- Date: June 12, 1987
- Holder: United States National Archives and Records Administration, NARA 198585, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Photograph of President Reagan giving a speech at the Berlin Wall, Brandenburg Gate, Federal Republic of Germany - NARA - 198585"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Photograph_of_President_Reagan_giving_a_speech_at_the_Berlin_Wall,_Brandenburg_Gate,_Federal_Republic_of_Germany_-_NARA_-_198585.jpg
- Public domain basis: A work of the United States federal government held by the National Archives, as shown in the Commons category listing for the 1987 Ronald Reagan speech in Berlin, which lists the .jpg and .tif under NARA 198585.
- Access note: the Commons file page itself returned an access error to the text fetch tool during this build; the title, date, creator office, and NARA identifier above are taken from the Wikimedia Commons category listing and the DPLA National Archives contribution records returned in search, which describe the same June 12, 1987 Brandenburg Gate speech photographs by the White House Photographic Office held by the National Archives. No catalog number beyond NARA 198585 is claimed.

## 3. inf-signing-1987.jpg (downloaded and verified; not used in the final deck, held in img/)

- Title: Reagan and Gorbachev signing
- What it shows: President Reagan and Soviet General Secretary Gorbachev signing the INF Treaty in the East Room of the White House, December 8, 1987, as described in the Commons file history.
- Creator: Photographer unknown, per the White House Historical Association record for this photograph; credit line there reads Ronald Reagan Presidential Library and Museum/NARA
- Date: December 8, 1987
- Holder: Ronald Reagan Presidential Library and Museum / National Archives, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Reagan and Gorbachev signing.jpg"; the companion National Archives photograph of the same signing is NARA 198588, per the Picryl public domain listing for "Photograph of President Reagan and General Secretary Gorbachev signing the INF Treaty in the East Room of the White House - NARA - 198588"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Reagan_and_Gorbachev_signing.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Picryl listing for the National Archives record of this signing tags it a public domain photograph related to the Ronald Reagan administration with no copyright restrictions, and the White House Historical Association credits the image to the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library and Museum/NARA.
- Use note: This file was downloaded and verified as an image (3840 by 2367 JPEG) and is held in img/ for the record. It was not placed in the deck, because the fetch tool could not open its Commons file page to confirm the file page's own licensing statement line by line; the deck teaches the INF Treaty on a stat slide instead. Recorded here so the gap is visible rather than papered over.

## Gaps recorded honestly

- November 9, 1989 itself: the famous photographs of the night of November 9, 1989 at the Bornholmer Strasse crossing and on the wall are agency and press photographs under copyright, and no public domain record for the night itself was verified in this build. The lesson therefore uses the December 1, 1989 Department of Defense photograph above, captioned with its real date, and teaches November 9 in text.
- August 1991: the widely reproduced photograph of Boris Yeltsin standing on a tank outside the Russian parliament is a press photograph under copyright; no public domain version was verified, so the coup is taught in text and stat form only.
- December 1991: no public domain photograph of Gorbachev's resignation or the flag change over the Kremlin was verified in this build; the dissolution is taught in text.
- Mikhail Gorbachev portraits circulating on Wikimedia Commons from the RIA Novosti archive are licensed CC BY-SA 3.0 rather than public domain, so none is used. Gorbachev appears in this lesson through the documents and the INF record, not through a portrait.
- No catalog number is stated anywhere in this lesson for any image beyond the holder numbers recorded above, which are taken from the Commons listings and records named in each entry.
