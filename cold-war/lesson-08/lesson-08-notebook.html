Teacher Answer Key

Question of the endingWhat the documents showWhat was decided
Could the Soviet system be reformed and saved?Gorbachev took power in March 1985 over a country stagnated for more than a decade, with half empty shops, failing harvests, and a war in Afghanistan. Glasnost loosened censorship and freed prisoners; perestroika loosened central control. Open discussion exposed the economy's failure and weakened the party's claim to rule, and citizens learned that obedience was a choice.Reform was not contained. The policies meant to save the system loosened the party's control of the economy, the press, and the republics, and by 1989 the peoples of Eastern Europe and then the Soviet republics were using the opening to leave the system rather than repair it.
Could the rivals destroy weapons instead of counting them?At Reykjavik in October 1986, Reagan and Gorbachev came within reach of abolishing nuclear weapons before breaking down over missile defense. In June 1987 Reagan challenged Gorbachev at the Brandenburg Gate to tear down the wall as the unmistakable sign of reform. The INF Treaty, signed that December, required each party to eliminate its intermediate-range and shorter-range missiles and not have such systems thereafter.The INF Treaty of December 8, 1987 destroyed an entire class of nuclear weapons, the first agreement to eliminate rather than limit. Diplomacy between the two leaders continued through the ending, and the German settlement of 1990 was negotiated with Gorbachev's consent.
What opened the Berlin Wall on November 9, 1989?The government intended a limited easing of travel rules. Its spokesman, not fully briefed, announced the rules on television as effective immediately. Thousands of East Berliners walked to the crossings and demanded to pass, and the guards, holding no orders for crowds of that size, stood aside. Gorbachev had refused all autumn to use the Soviet army to hold the empire.The wall was breached that night, without a shot fired, and crowds attacked it with hammers at the Brandenburg Gate. The December 1, 1989 photograph shows crowds standing freely at the gate itself, the division's most guarded point, weeks before its formal opening to the West on December 22.
What would become of Germany and the Soviet alliance?Poland voted Solidarity into government in 1989 and Hungary opened its border, while the Leipzig demonstrations grew through the autumn. Free elections in East Germany in March 1990 returned a government committed to unification, and the two German states and the four occupying powers settled the external terms.German reunification came on October 3, 1990, with the united Germany inside NATO. The Warsaw Pact dissolved in 1991, and Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania recovered their independence. The postwar division of Europe ended by signatures and elections.
How would the Soviet Union itself end?In August 1991 hard line officials seized Gorbachev and announced an emergency; Yeltsin rallied resistance from a tank, crowds defended the Russian parliament, and army units refused orders to attack, and the coup failed in three days. In December, Ukraine voted for independence, and Russia, Ukraine, and Belarus declared the union dissolved.Gorbachev resigned on December 25, 1991, and the Soviet flag came down over the Kremlin. The Soviet Union ended by referendum, declaration, and resignation, without invasion or civil war, leaving the United States the world's only superpower over a map of new states and an arsenal divided among republics.

The lesson in one paragraph. Strong answers run the causes and the settlement together. Example: The Cold War ended because a stagnating system produced a reformer whose two policies, glasnost and perestroika, loosened the control they were meant to restore; because he refused in 1989 to hold Eastern Europe by force, so elections in Poland, crowds in Leipzig, and a garbled announcement in Berlin on November 9, 1989 could open the wall; and because the republics then used the same opening against the center, defeating a coup in August 1991 and dissolving the union that December. The ending settled Germany, reunified inside NATO on October 3, 1990, dissolved the Warsaw Pact, and freed the Baltic states. It left unsettled the future of the new states and the division of the Soviet nuclear arsenal among them.
