# Image Sources: Lesson 7, Détente, Human Rights, and the Evil Empire

All images are public domain as stated on their Wikimedia Commons file pages. Deck crops live in slides-build/img/ and were cropped from the downloads listed here. Document copies of the SALT signing and Helsinki signing photographs live in assets/. Every photograph in this lesson is used where it is under discussion and captioned from the holding record.

## Images considered and not used
- A still photograph of Reagan delivering the March 8, 1983 evangelical address was not used in the deck. The Commons copies located are a TIFF scan and speech page scans rather than a clean photographic file at deck size, so the evil empire slide is a text slide and the speech itself is read in the activity. No image gap affects the documents: the reading, activity, and notebook page carry the SALT and Helsinki photographs listed above.
