# Image Sources: Lesson 5, Cuba: Revolution and the Missile Crisis

All images are public domain as stated on their Wikimedia Commons file pages or in the audited source record that carries them. Deck crops live in slides-build/img/ and were cropped from the downloads listed here. Document copies live in assets/.

## U-2 photograph of the San Cristobal missile site (u2-sites.jpg; deck crop u2-sites-port.jpg)
- Title: Cuban missiles (Wikimedia Commons file "Cuban missiles.jpg"), the image file linked by the audited TPSP bank record for the U-2 reconnaissance photo of Soviet missile sites in Cuba
- Creator: United States Air Force
- Date: October 14, 1962, as dated in the bank record's source note. The downloaded frame itself carries an in-frame label reading "MRBM Launch Site 2, San Cristobal, 1 November 1962" and marks "former launch positions," so the frame as downloaded appears to show the site as analysts marked it during the withdrawal period. That difference between the bank record's date and the frame's own label is recorded here rather than resolved by assertion.
- Holder: U.S. government photograph, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cuban_missiles.jpg
- Public domain basis: a work of the United States federal government (United States Air Force), identified in the bank record as a U.S. government photograph.

## U-2 photograph of missile transporters and tents (u2-tents.jpg; deck crop u2-tents-port.jpg)
- Title: U2 Image of Cuban Missile Crisis
- Creator: CIA, as credited on the Commons file page
- Date: 1962 (estimated), as given on the Commons file page
- Holder: CIA, via Wikimedia Commons. The Commons file page describes the image: "This U-2 reconnaissance photo showed concrete evidence of missile assembly in Cuba. Shown here are missile transporters and missile-ready tents where fueling and maintenance took place."
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:U2_Image_of_Cuban_Missile_Crisis.jpg
- Public domain basis: Commons file page credits the CIA and gives the permission as "Courtesy of CIA"; the image is a work of the United States federal government.

## Gaps recorded honestly
- A 1959 photograph of Fidel Castro (the Library of Congress MATS Terminal, Washington, April 1959 image) and a photograph of the naval quarantine at sea were attempted for this lesson. The downloads attempted returned error pages rather than image files under the file names tried, so no Castro or quarantine-at-sea photograph is used anywhere in this lesson. Sections A, B, and E of the deck use text and stat slides instead, and no image has been substituted under a guessed credit.
