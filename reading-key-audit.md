# Reading and key alignment audit

Started 2026-10-23, from Alex's classroom read of Progressive Era Lesson 1:
the reading was fine, but its key answered with information the reading
never gave, so students had no way to produce the keyed answer from the
material in front of them.

## The rule

Every question a student is asked must be answerable from the material
the student has: the reading, the sources printed on the page, and the
lesson as taught (slides and guided notes). A key may not assert a fact
that none of those supports. Fixes go in whichever direction the item
needs:

- If the key's extra content is core and the question targets it, add
  the missing context to the reading (or the page's background note), so
  the keyed answer becomes reachable.
- If the key reaches past the material for a peripheral point, trim the
  key to what the material supports.
- Grading guidance and model answers for open questions may interpret,
  but may not introduce new facts as if students should have found them.
