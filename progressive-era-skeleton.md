# Progressive Era Unit — Assignment Skeleton

_Pilot unit for the Knoweth curriculum + planner site. Drafted 2026-10-20. This is the template every later unit follows: a full plate of options per lesson, teachers pick and choose._

## How to read this

Every lesson gets the same plate of assignment types, so the unit is consistent even where the content differs. Status marks show what exists today:

- **HAVE** — already exists (TPSP bank or built material)
- **BUILD** — needs creating
- **CHECK** — Alex may already have a version in his scattered materials; check before building

## The plate (assignment types in every unit)

1. **Core lesson** — slides plus guided notes
2. **Reading** — textbook-style reading with questions
3. **Source analysis** — one primary source, TPSP format
4. **Stations or jigsaw** — multiple short sources or images in rotation
5. **Notebook page** — interactive notebook or graphic organizer
6. **Activity** — simulation, chart work, debate, or hands-on task
7. **Quick check** — short formative, TPSP Quick Check format
8. **Writing** — short-answer or document-based writing task
9. **Review** — crossword, game, or study session material
10. **Assessment** — quiz and unit test

## Unit frame

**Essential questions**
- How did Americans respond to the problems created by industrialization, urbanization, and political corruption?
- Who did Progressive reform help, and who did it leave out?

**Shape:** 8 content lessons, 1 review day, test day. Roughly 10 instructional days, adjustable by the planner.
