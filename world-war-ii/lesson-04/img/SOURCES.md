## A note on the Eastern Front
No photograph of Stalingrad or of the Eastern Front is used in this lesson. German Bundesarchiv photographs are Creative Commons licensed rather than public domain, and the status of many Soviet photographs is unclear on their file pages. The Eastern Front is therefore taught in this lesson with text and stat slides, including the estimate of about 27 million Soviet military and civilian deaths across the whole Soviet war, labeled as an estimate for the whole war.
