Guided Notes: The War in Europe and North Africa

Lesson question: How did the Allies carry the war from North Africa to the surrender of Germany, and what did each stage cost?

Unit question: How did the Second World War transform the United States, and how far did the country live up to the democracy it claimed to defend?

Key Terms

amphibious landing: an attack from the sea onto a defended shore by forces carried in ships and landing craft

Eastern Front: the battlefront between Germany and the Soviet Union after June 1941, where the largest land fighting of the war took place

Stalingrad: the battle of August 1942 to February 1943 in which the Soviet army surrounded and destroyed a German army, the turning point of the war on the Eastern Front

strategic bombing: the use of heavy bombers against an enemy's industry, transportation, and cities, aiming to break its capacity and will to make war

D-Day: June 6, 1944, the day Allied forces landed in Normandy, France, in the largest seaborne invasion in history

Battle of the Bulge: the last major German offensive in the west, launched through the Ardennes in December 1944 and beaten back by February 1945

VE Day: May 8, 1945, the day victory in Europe was announced after Germany's unconditional surrender

Germany struck back through the Ardennes on December 16, (14) ____________, in the Battle of the Bulge, its last major offensive in the west. Summary table. North Africa and Italy: Torch landed in Morocco and Algeria in November 1942, Tunisia fell in May 1943 with about 250,000 Axis prisoners, and Sicily and Italy followed at the cost of a slow mountain war that ran to the spring of 1945. The air war: day and night bombing from England broke German oil and transportation by early 1945, at the cost of aircrews on missions such as Schweinfurt and civilian deaths estimated at 37,000 at Hamburg and 25,000 at Dresden. Normandy to the Rhine: D-Day landed about 156,000 troops on June 6, 1944, the breakout freed France, and the Bulge cost Germany its last reserves in the west. The eastern war and the end: the Eastern Front carried the largest land fighting, Soviet deaths are estimated at about 27 million across the whole war, and the two sides met at the Elbe before the surrender and VE Day on May 8, 1945.

Exit ticket 1. The Soviet Union carried the largest land fighting of the war on the Eastern Front and wanted an invasion of France to draw German divisions and strength away from the east.

Exit ticket 2. Any one stage with all three parts. North Africa aimed to put American troops into the war against Germany and clear the Mediterranean's southern shore, ended in the Tunisian surrender of May 1943, and cost a hard education at Kasserine. Normandy aimed to open the second front, put about 156,000 troops ashore on the first day, and cost the heaviest American losses at Omaha. The Bulge aimed to split the Allied armies, failed by February 1945, and cost Germany the armor and fuel reserves it could not replace.

Exit ticket 3. Accept any answer that moves through at least two stages in order and names a cost for each. The strongest answers end at the Elbe and VE Day and note that the western campaigns ran alongside the larger eastern war.
