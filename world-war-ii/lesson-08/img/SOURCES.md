# Image Sources for World War II Lesson 8, Victory and the Atomic Reckoning

All images are real public domain photographs held by the National Archives, the Naval History and Heritage Command, or Los Alamos National Laboratory and distributed through Wikimedia Commons. They were downloaded on October 21, 2026. Each download was opened and verified with PIL. Document copies are stored in the lesson's assets folder; deck versions are cropped to portrait (1046 by 1500) or to 16:9 for the title slide and stored in slides-build/img; no other alteration. The atomic photographs are used as record, captioned exactly, and are not used as decoration.

## 1. yalta.jpg (assets; deck portrait crop yalta-port.jpg)

- Title: Yalta summit 1945 with Churchill, Roosevelt, and Stalin
- What it shows: Winston Churchill, Franklin D. Roosevelt, and Joseph Stalin seated together at the Yalta Conference, February 1945.
- Creator: Photograph from the Army Signal Corps Collection in the United States National Archives
- Date: February 9, 1945
- Holder: United States National Archives and Records Administration, via Naval History and Heritage Command photograph USA C-543, distributed through Wikimedia Commons, file "Yalta summit 1945 with Churchill, Roosevelt, Stalin"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yalta_summit_1945_with_Churchill,_Roosevelt,_Stalin.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Commons file page tags the photograph Public domain as a work of the United States federal government.

## 2. potsdam.jpg (reading figure; assets; deck portrait crop potsdam-port.jpg)

- Title: Delegates of the Potsdam Conference seated around the conference table
- What it shows: The delegates of the Potsdam Conference seated around the conference table at Cecilienhof Palace, Potsdam, Germany, July 1945.
- Creator: Unknown author, not provided on the Commons file page
- Date: Taken July 19, 1945
- Holder: United States National Archives and Records Administration, National Archives ID 198893, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Delegates of the Potsdam Conference in Germany seated around the conference table. Soviet leader Josef Stalin is... - NARA - 198893"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Delegates_of_the_Potsdam_Conference_in_Germany_seated_around_the_conference_table._Soviet_leader_Josef_Stalin_is..._-_NARA_-_198893.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Commons file page tags the photograph Public domain as a work of the United States federal government held by the National Archives.

## 3. trinity.jpg (assets; deck portrait crop trinity-port.jpg)

- Title: Trinity Test Fireball, 16 milliseconds
- What it shows: The Trinity explosion 0.016 seconds after detonation, July 16, 1945; the top of the hemisphere shown is about 200 meters high.
- Creator: Berlyn Brixner, Los Alamos National Laboratory
- Date: July 16, 1945
- Holder: Los Alamos National Laboratory, photograph PA-98-0520, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Trinity Test Fireball 16ms"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trinity_Test_Fireball_16ms.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Commons file page tags the photograph Public domain; it is a Los Alamos National Laboratory photograph from the Trinity test record.

## 4. hiroshima.jpg (assets; deck portrait crop hiroshima-port.jpg)

- Title: Atomic cloud over Hiroshima
- What it shows: The cloud over Hiroshima on August 6, 1945, smoke rising 20,000 feet above the city over the burst at its base, photographed from the bombing mission.
- Creator: George R. Caron, tail gunner of the Enola Gay, as credited on the Commons file page
- Date: August 6, 1945
- Holder: United States Army Air Forces photograph, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Atomic cloud over Hiroshima"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atomic_cloud_over_Hiroshima.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Commons file page tags the photograph Public domain as a work of the United States federal government.
- Presentation note: In the deck this photograph appears once, on the divider for the August 1945 section, captioned as the record of August 6, 1945. It is not used on any other slide or document.

## 5. surrender-signing.jpg (activity Station 5 and quick check figure; assets; deck portrait crop surrender-port.jpg)

- Title: Mamoru Shigemitsu signs the Instrument of Surrender, officially ending the Second World War
- What it shows: Japanese Foreign Minister Mamoru Shigemitsu signing the Instrument of Surrender on behalf of the Japanese government aboard USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay, with Lieutenant General Richard K. Sutherland watching from the opposite side of the table.
- Creator: Lieutenant Stephen E. Korpanty, Army Signal Corps; restored by Adam Cuerden, as credited on the Commons file page
- Date: September 2, 1945
- Holder: Naval History and Heritage Command photograph SC 213700, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Mamoru Shigemitsu signs the Instrument of Surrender, officially ending the Second World War"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mamoru_Shigemitsu_signs_the_Instrument_of_Surrender,_officially_ending_the_Second_World_War.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Commons file page tags the photograph Public domain as a work of the United States federal government.

## 6. missouri.jpg (assets; deck title image title-169.jpg, cropped to 16:9)

- Title: Surrender of Japan, USS Missouri

- Creator: Army Signal Corps
- Date: September 2, 1945
- Holder: Naval Historical Center photograph USA C-2719, from the Army Signal Corps Collection in the United States National Archives, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Surrender of Japan - USS Missouri"

- Public domain basis: The Commons file page tags the photograph Public domain as a work of the United States federal government.
