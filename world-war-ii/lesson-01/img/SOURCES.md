## Gap recorded honestly
- A photograph of Charles Lindbergh speaking at an America First rally in 1941 exists on Wikimedia Commons (File:Charles Lindbergh speaking at America First rally.jpg), but its Commons file page marks it Copyrighted (Copyrighted free use, sourced from an Associated Press photograph), not public domain. It was not used. The Harris and Ewing portrait above stands in its place in the deck, captioned as a portrait.
- No photograph of the fall of France or of French refugees in 1940 was used. The Commons candidates found were Belgian refugee columns from British official collections or German federal archive photographs under CC BY-SA, not public domain. Section E is carried by the Lend-Lease photograph and a giant stat slide instead.
