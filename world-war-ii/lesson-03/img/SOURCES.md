## African American worker at the Richmond Shipyards (richmond-worker.jpg; deck crop richmond-port.jpg)
- Title: African American worker Richmond Shipyards
- Creator: E. F. Joseph
- Date: April 1943
- Holder: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, digital ID fsa.8b06587
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:African_American_worker_Richmond_Shipyards.jpg
- Public domain basis: Commons file page marks the image Public domain. The photograph is a Farm Security Administration and Office of War Information photograph held by the Library of Congress. Original caption on the file page records skilled workers rushing the SS George Washington Carver to completion at Richmond, California.
