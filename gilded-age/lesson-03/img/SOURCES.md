# Lesson 3 image sources

All images are real public-domain works held by the Library of Congress or distributed through Wikimedia Commons. Files are stored in `../assets/` and referenced by the lesson documents.

## haymarket-harpers.jpg
- Title: "The Anarchist Riot in Chicago: A Dynamite Bomb Exploding Among the Police" (Haymarket affair engraving)
- Creator: Thure de Thulstrup (1848-1930), wood engraving published in Harper's Weekly, vol. 30, no. 1534, May 15, 1886, pages 312-313
- Date: May 15, 1886
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons, file "HaymarketRiot-Harpers.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HaymarketRiot-Harpers.jpg
- Public-domain basis: Published in the United States before January 1, 1931; public domain in the United States. The Commons file page confirms this licensing. Note for classroom use: the engraving compresses the speech, the explosion, and the shooting into one moment, which the activity states openly.

## frick-portrait.jpg
- Title: Henry Clay Frick, head-and-shoulders portrait
- Creator: Bain News Service (publisher); photograph from the Bain Collection
- Date: Created between about 1910 and 1920, from a Bain News Service glass negative of an earlier portrait sitting; Frick directed the Homestead lockout in 1892
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Bain Collection, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Henry Clay Frick.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Henry%20Clay%20Frick.jpg
- Public-domain basis: Bain News Service photographs in the Library of Congress Bain Collection were published in the early twentieth century with no known copyright restrictions; the underlying portrait predates 1931.

## pullman-strikers.jpg
- Title: Troops in formation outside the Arcade Building, Pullman, Illinois, during the Pullman Strike (Commons file name: "Pullman strikers outside Arcade Building.jpg")
- Creator: Not credited on the file; contemporary photograph of the 1894 strike
- Date: 1894
- Repository: Wikimedia Commons, category "Pullman Strike," file "Pullman strikers outside Arcade Building.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Pullman_Strike
- Public-domain basis: Photograph taken and published in 1894; public domain in the United States (published before January 1, 1931). Caption in the activity describes what the photograph visibly shows, troops in formation with residents watching, rather than repeating the file name.

## hine-spinner.jpg
- Title: "A little spinner in a Georgia Cotton Mill"
- Creator: Lewis W. Hine (1874-1940), photograph for the National Child Labor Committee
- Date: About 1909 (Hine's Georgia mill photographs date to January 1909)
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, National Child Labor Committee Collection (LOC nclc series), via Wikimedia Commons, file "A little spinner in a Georgia Cotton Mill.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/A%20little%20spinner%20in%20a%20Georgia%20Cotton%20Mill.jpg
- Public-domain basis: Work of the National Child Labor Committee collection at the Library of Congress; Hine died in 1940 and the photographs were published in the United States before 1931, with no known restrictions on the LOC copies.
