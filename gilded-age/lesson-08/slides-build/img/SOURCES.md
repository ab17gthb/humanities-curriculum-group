# Lesson 8 deck images: sources

Every file in this folder is either a crop of a documented lesson asset in
`lesson-08/assets/` (see `lesson-08/img/SOURCES.md` for the full entries) or a
real public-domain image downloaded from Wikimedia Commons / the Library of
Congress. Crops change framing only; nothing is stretched, generated, or
retouched. Portrait crops are 1046 by 1500 for the deck's right-side photo
slots; the title crop is 1920 by 1080.

## Derivatives of lesson assets (cite the lesson SOURCES.md entries)

- `title-169.jpg`: 16:9 crop of `assets/jim-crow-fountain.jpg`.
  "Negro drinking at 'Colored' water cooler in streetcar terminal, Oklahoma
  City, Oklahoma," Russell Lee for the Farm Security Administration, July 1939.
  Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (FSA/OWI collection),
  via Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Negro_drinking_at_%22Colored%22_water_cooler_in_streetcar_terminal,_Oklahoma_City,_Oklahoma_by_Russell_Lee.jpg
  Public domain: work of a United States federal government employee.
  Lesson SOURCES.md entry 1. The 1939 date is disclosed in the speaker notes
  of every slide that uses this photograph.
- `fountain-port.jpg`: portrait crop of the same 1939 Russell Lee photograph
  as `title-169.jpg` (man drinking, segregation signs behind him; the cooler
  itself sits mostly outside this tighter crop). Same source, repository,
  public-domain basis, and 1939 disclosure as above. Lesson SOURCES.md entry 1.
- `wells-port.jpg`: portrait crop of `assets/ida-b-wells.jpg`.
  "Ida B. Wells-Barnett," photograph by Mary Garrity, about 1893, via
  Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mary_Garrity_-_Ida_B._Wells-Barnett_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg
  Public domain: created about 1893 and published before 1930.
  Lesson SOURCES.md entry 2.
- `washington-port.jpg`: portrait crop of `assets/booker-t-washington.jpg`,
  cropped to the photograph inside its Library of Congress mat board.
  "Booker T. Washington, half-length portrait, standing, against white
  background," C. M. Battey, about 1917. Library of Congress, Prints and
  Photographs Division, LCCN98502189, via Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Booker_T._Washington,_half-length_portrait,_standing,_against_white_background_LCCN98502189.jpg
  Public domain as listed on Wikimedia Commons. Lesson SOURCES.md entry 3.
  The about 1917 date is noted in the speaker notes of the slide that uses it.
- `bryan-port.jpg`: portrait crop of `assets/william-jennings-bryan.jpg`.
  "William Jennings Bryan, Democratic party presidential candidate, standing
  on stage next to American flag (speaking, 1896 campaign)," copyright by
  Geo. H. Van Norman, Springfield, Mass., October 3, 1896. Library of
  Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, LC-USZC2-6259, via Wikimedia
  Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:William-Jennings-Bryan-speaking-c1896.jpeg
  Public domain: created and copyrighted in 1896; copyright expired.
  Lesson SOURCES.md entry 4.
