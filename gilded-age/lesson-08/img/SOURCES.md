## Quotation check
- Bryan's closing lines in the activity ("You shall not press down upon the brow of labor this crown of thorns; you shall not crucify mankind upon a cross of gold") are the documented closing of his July 1896 Democratic convention speech, checked against published transcriptions of the speech. Elsewhere in the lesson the line is paraphrased, not quoted.
- Washington's Atlanta Compromise language (including the bucket image) is paraphrased throughout; no wording is presented as a direct quotation from Washington.
- No other quotations appear in the lesson. Summaries in the activity and writing cards are labeled as summaries.
