# Image Sources: Gilded Age Lesson 2, Big Business and Its Critics

All images are real public-domain works, downloaded into `../assets/` and used in the lesson documents. No image was generated or altered beyond resizing for print by the PDF builder.

## 1. bosses-of-the-senate.jpg

- **Title:** The Bosses of the Senate
- **Creator:** Joseph Keppler
- **Date:** January 23, 1889 (published in *Puck* magazine)
- **Repository:** Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Cartoons_by_Joseph_Keppler (file: "The Bosses of the Senate by Joseph Keppler.jpg"); the cartoon is also cataloged by the United States Senate at https://www.senate.gov/art-artifacts/historical-images/political-cartoons-caricatures/38_00392_001.htm
- **Public-domain basis:** Published in the United States in 1889, before January 1, 1931; copyright expired. The image shows giant money bags labeled for the steel, copper, oil, iron, sugar, and other trusts looming over small senators, with the people's entrance to the gallery bolted shut.
- **Used in:** lesson-02-activity.html (Witness 6), lesson-02-quick-check.html

## 2. standard-oil-octopus.jpg

- **Title:** Next!
- **Creator:** Udo J. Keppler
- **Date:** September 7, 1904 (published in *Puck* magazine)
- **Repository:** Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division
- **Source URL:** https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/artifact/next-udo-keppler-puck-september-7-1904 (United States Capitol Visitor Center artifact page for this cartoon, crediting the Prints and Photographs Division, Library of Congress; the file in this folder is the same Library of Congress scan carried in the Progressive Era Lesson 1 asset set)
- **Public-domain basis:** Published in the United States in 1904, before January 1, 1931; copyright expired. The image shows Standard Oil as an octopus grasping the steel, copper, and shipping industries, the United States Capitol, and a state house, and reaching toward the White House.
- **Used in:** lesson-02-activity.html (Witness 1)
