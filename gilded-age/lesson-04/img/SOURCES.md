## angel-island-interview.jpg
- Title: Immigration Interview on Angel Island
- Creator: Unknown photographer, United States Public Health Service historical photograph file
- Date: Early twentieth century (file dated to the station's operating era; the Angel Island Immigration Station Foundation dates the well known interview photograph to 1923 and identifies the scene as officials examining an applicant)
- Repository: National Archives (NARA ID 7452280), via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:US_National_Archives_series:_Public_Health_Service_Historical_Photograph_File,_1880_-_1943
- Public-domain basis: Work of the United States federal government (National Archives), public domain.
- Caption note: The printed caption says only that an applicant sits before officials at Angel Island. It does not claim the photograph shows a specific interrogation, following the Immigration Station Foundation's caution about this image.

## Text source note (not an image)
- The Angel Island wall poems in the activity are summarized, not quoted. The summary is drawn from the published translations in Him Mark Lai, Genny Lim, and Judy Yung, Island: Poetry and History of Chinese Immigrants on Angel Island, 1910 to 1940 (1980; University of Washington Press). No poem text is reproduced.
- The passenger manifest station summarizes the standard manifest form required after the Immigration Act of 1903 (National Archives), and its four passenger entries are labeled in the document as composites built for the activity, not records of real people.
