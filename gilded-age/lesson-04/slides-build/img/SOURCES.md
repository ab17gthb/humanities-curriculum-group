# Lesson 4 Slide Image Sources: The New Immigration

All images are public domain. Every file in this folder is a crop or resize made with PIL from a lesson asset in `../../assets/`; no image was stretched, and no new downloads were needed. For each derivative, the full documentation of the original is in `../../img/SOURCES.md`, cited below.

## title-169.jpg
- Derived from: `mulberry-street.jpg` (lesson asset), center crop to 16:9, resized to 1920x1080
- Title: Mulberry Street, New York City
- Creator: Detroit Publishing Company (photochrom print)
- Date: About 1900
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Detroit Publishing Company photograph collection
- Source URL: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/collection/detroit/
- Public-domain basis: Published in the United States about 1900 by the Detroit Publishing Company; copyright expired, and the Library of Congress places no restrictions on its Detroit Publishing holdings. See the mulberry-street.jpg entry in `../../img/SOURCES.md`.

## angel-interview-port.jpg
- Derived from: `angel-island-interview.jpg` (lesson asset), portrait crop, resized to 1046x1500
- Title: Immigration Interview on Angel Island
- Creator: Unknown photographer, United States Public Health Service historical photograph file
- Date: Early twentieth century; the Angel Island Immigration Station Foundation dates the well known interview photograph to 1923 and identifies the scene as officials examining an applicant
- Repository: National Archives (NARA ID 7452280), via Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:US_National_Archives_series:_Public_Health_Service_Historical_Photograph_File,_1880_-_1943
- Public-domain basis: Work of the United States federal government (National Archives), public domain. See the angel-island-interview.jpg entry in `../../img/SOURCES.md`.
- Caption note: Following the Immigration Station Foundation's caution about this image, the deck describes it only as officials examining an applicant at Angel Island. It does not claim the photograph shows a specific interrogation.
