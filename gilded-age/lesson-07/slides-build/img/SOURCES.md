# Image Sources: Lesson 7 slide deck, Native Americans and the Closing Frontier

Every file in this folder is a crop or derivative of a real public domain photograph, illustration, or government document. No image was generated. Lesson asset derivatives cite the entry in lesson-07/img/SOURCES.md, which holds the full documentation. New downloads are documented in full here.

## 1. title-169.jpg (title slide)
- **Derived from:** lesson-07/assets/bison-skulls.jpg (see lesson SOURCES.md entry 1)
- **Title:** Pile of American bison skulls, Michigan Carbon Works, Detroit
- **Creator:** Unknown photographer
- **Date:** circa 1892
- **Repository:** Burton Historical Collection, Detroit Public Library, via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bison_skull_pile.jpg
- **Public domain basis:** Photograph published in the United States in the 1890s; copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons tags the file Public domain (PD-US).
- **Deck treatment:** Cropped to a 16:9 band (1920x1080) that keeps the pile and both men in frame. No stretching.

## 2. bison-port.jpg (Section B divider)
- **Derived from:** lesson-07/assets/bison-skulls.jpg (see lesson SOURCES.md entry 1)
- Same title, creator, date, repository, source URL, and public domain basis as title-169.jpg above.
- **Deck treatment:** Portrait crop (1046x1500) centered on the pile and the man standing at its base.

## 3. chief-joseph-port.jpg (Section A divider) — NEW DOWNLOAD
- **Title:** Chief Joseph, Nez Perce (photogravure, plate 256)
- **Creator:** Edward S. Curtis (1868 to 1952), photographer
- **Date:** 1903
- **Repository:** Wikimedia Commons (file from the Curtis photogravure series)
- **Source URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chief_Joseph_by_Edward_Sheriff_Curtis.jpg
- **Public domain basis:** Photograph taken and copyrighted in 1903 and published in the United States before 1929; copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons tags the file Public domain.
- **Deck treatment:** Plate margins and printed caption cropped out; portrait crop (1046x1500). The subject is Chief Joseph of the Nez Perce, named in the lesson reading as a leader of the 1877 flight toward Canada. Date note: the portrait shows him in 1903, late in life, not as he appeared in 1877. The divider speaker notes say so plainly.

- **Title:** Classroom at the Indian Industrial School, Carlisle, Pennsylvania
- **Creator:** Frances Benjamin Johnston (1864 to 1952), photographer
- **Date:** 1901
- **Repository:** Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (LC-USZ62-115435; LCCN 96512679)

## 5. torlino-port.jpg (boarding schools slide)
- **Derived from:** lesson-07/assets/tom-torlino.jpg (see lesson SOURCES.md entry 4)
- **Title:** Tom Torlino, Navajo, before and after (arrival portrait, 1882; portrait after about three years at Carlisle, 1885)
- **Creator:** John N. Choate (1848 to 1902), photographer
- **Date:** 1882 and 1885
- **Repository:** Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University, via Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tom_Torlino_Navajo_before_and_after_circa_1882.jpg
- **Public domain basis:** Photographs taken in the United States in 1882 and 1885; copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons tags the file Public domain.
- **Deck treatment:** The original pair stands side by side. For the tall slide slot the two portraits are stacked vertically, 1882 on top and 1885 below, each cropped to the face and torso so neither is stretched. The slide speaker notes disclose the rearrangement. The full side by side pair appears in the lesson quick check.

## 6. indian-land-port.jpg (Section D divider) — NEW DOWNLOAD
- **Title:** Indian Land for Sale (advertisement poster)
- **Creator:** United States Department of the Interior
- **Date:** 1911
- **Repository:** Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Indian_Land_for_Sale_Advertisement.jpg
- **Public domain basis:** Work of the United States federal government, published in 1911; Wikimedia Commons tags the file Public domain.
- **Deck treatment:** The complete poster is shown, letterboxed on a plain ground to fit the tall slide slot (1046x1500); no part of the poster is cropped and nothing is stretched. The poster advertises sales of allotted Indian land, reporting 1910 sales and estimating 350,000 acres to be offered in 1911. The man pictured at its center is not named on the poster, and the deck does not name him. Date note: the poster comes from 1911, inside the allotment era (1887 to 1934), not from the year of the Dawes Act itself. The divider speaker notes say so plainly.

## 7. ghost-dance-port.jpg (Section E divider)
- **Derived from:** lesson-07/assets/ghost-dance.jpg (see lesson SOURCES.md entry 3)
- **Title:** The Ghost Dance by the Oglala Lakota at Pine Ridge Agency, South Dakota
- **Creator:** Frederic Remington (1861 to 1909), artist
- **Date:** 1890 (published in Harper's Weekly)
- **Repository:** Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (LCCN 90707734)
- **Source URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Ghost_dance_by_the_Ogallala_(sic)_Sioux_at_Pine_Ridge_Agency_..._Dakota_-_Frederic_Remington,_Pine_Ridge,_S._Dak._LCCN90707734.jpg
- **Public domain basis:** Illustration published in the United States in 1890; copyright expired. Wikimedia Commons tags the file Public domain.
- **Deck treatment:** Portrait crop (1046x1500) centered on the dancers. This is a white artist's magazine illustration, not a photograph, and the divider speaker notes say so. No photograph of the Wounded Knee dead is used anywhere in this deck, matching the lesson documents.
