# Lesson 5 deck images: sources

All images are real period photographs or prints, public domain, held in this folder as crops sized for the deck system (portrait crops about 1046x1500, title crop 1920x1080). Crops never stretch the original. Files marked "lesson asset derivative" are crops of the audited images in lesson-05/assets/, documented in lesson-05/img/SOURCES.md.

  - Title: Mulberry Street, New York City
  - Creator: Detroit Publishing Company (Detroit Photographic Company)
  - Date: about 1900
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Detroit Publishing Company Collection
  - Source URL: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/collection/detroit/ (see lesson-05/img/SOURCES.md)
  - Public-domain basis: Published in the United States in 1900; copyright expired. Library of Congress Detroit Publishing Company photographs carry no known restrictions on publication.
