# Lesson 5 image sources

All images are public domain and are held in lesson-05/assets/. Files were drawn from the audited pilot image bank for this curriculum, where they were obtained from the Library of Congress and Wikimedia Commons.

- `riis-lodgers.jpg`
  - Title: Lodgers in a Crowded Bayard Street Tenement, "Five Cents a Spot"
  - Creator: Jacob A. Riis
  - Date: 1889; published in How the Other Half Lives, 1890
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (prints also held by the Museum of Modern Art and the Museum of the City of New York)
  - Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodgers_in_Bayard_Street_Tenement,_Five_Cents_a_Spot
  - Public-domain basis: Photograph published in 1890 in the United States; copyright expired. Creator died in 1914.

- `mulberry-street.jpg`
  - Title: Mulberry Street, New York City
  - Creator: Detroit Publishing Company (Detroit Photographic Company)
  - Date: about 1900; the print carries a 1900 Detroit Photographic copyright notice
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Detroit Publishing Company Collection
  - Source URL: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/collection/detroit/
  - Public-domain basis: Published in the United States in 1900; copyright expired. Library of Congress Detroit Publishing Company photographs carry no known restrictions on publication.

- `nast-corruption.jpg`
  - Title: Cartoon on political corruption ("It is the immediate jewel of her soul")
  - Creator: Thomas Nast
  - Date: 1870s; published in Harper's Weekly
  - Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division
  - Source URL: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/
  - Public-domain basis: Published in the United States in the 1870s; copyright expired. Creator died in 1902.
