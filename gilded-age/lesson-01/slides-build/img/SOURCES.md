# Lesson 1 deck images: sources

All images are real public domain images via Wikimedia Commons or the Library of Congress. No AI-generated or stock-site images. Portrait files are center crops made with PIL from the sources below, resized to 1046 by 1500 pixels for the right-side photo slots in deck_common; the title file is a 16:9 crop resized to 1920 by 1080. Cropping only; subjects kept in frame, nothing stretched.

- `title-169.jpg`: Derivative crop of the lesson asset `promontory-1869.jpg`. See `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md` entry 1.
  - Title: East and West Shaking Hands at Laying of Last Rail
  - Creator: Andrew J. Russell, photographer for the Union Pacific Railroad
  - Date: May 10, 1869
  - Repository: Yale University Libraries, via Wikimedia Commons
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:East_and_West_Shaking_hands_at_the_laying_of_last_rail_Union_Pacific_Railroad.jpg
  - Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1869 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.
  - Note: crop trims the white mount border of the albumen print so the photograph itself fills the slide.

- `promontory-port.jpg`: Portrait derivative crop of the same lesson asset `promontory-1869.jpg`. Same title, creator, date, repository, source URL, and public domain basis as `title-169.jpg` above; see `lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md` entry 1.

  - Creator: Chicago, Burlington and Quincy Railroad, Passenger Department, Chicago
  - Date: 1892
  - Repository: Wikimedia Commons (file page states Public domain, PD US)
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Burlington_Route_Map_1892.png
