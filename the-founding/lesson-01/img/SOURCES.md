# Lesson 1 Image Sources

All images are public domain. Masters are in this folder. Document copies are in the lesson assets folder, and deck crops are in slides-build/img.

## 1. The Bloody Massacre in King Street, 1770
- File: revere-boston-massacre-1770.jpg
- Title: The Bloody Massacre perpetrated in King-Street BOSTON on March 5th 1770 by a Party of the 29th Regt
- Creator: Paul Revere, after Henry Pelham
- Date: 1770
- Holder: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division
- Source URL: https://www.loc.gov/item/2008661777/ (image file served from the Library of Congress tile service, the same file the audited TPSP bank record names)
- Public domain basis: work published in 1770, long out of copyright. The Library of Congress places no restrictions on this image.
- Use: activity Station 5, assets copy, deck portrait crop revere-port.jpg

- Date: 1768 printing of the design first published in 1766
- Holder: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division (LCCN 2004672618)

- Use: deck portrait crop colonies-port.jpg

## Subjects for which no separate image was used
- No portrait of John Dickinson or of a Townshend Acts customs scene was downloaded for this lesson. The deck uses text and stat slides for the Townshend section rather than a later artist imagining, and this file records that choice honestly.
- The Stamp Act Congress left no period image of its meeting. No image was invented for it.

## Text editions used in this lesson (recorded here as the spec directs)
- Declaration of Rights and Grievances of the Stamp Act Congress, October 19, 1765. Avalon Project, Yale Law School, transcription at avalon.law.yale.edu (Resolutions of the Continental Congress, October 19, 1765). Quoted verbatim, cuts marked with ellipses.
- The Declaratory Act, March 18, 1766. Avalon Project, Yale Law School, transcription from Danby Pickering, The Statutes at Large. Quoted verbatim, cuts marked with ellipses.
- John Dickinson, Letters from a Farmer in Pennsylvania, Letter II, 1767. The Avalon Project does not carry the full text of the Letters. The text quoted here is the Wikisource transcription of the collected edition of 1768, quoted verbatim from that transcription, cuts marked with ellipses. This substitution is recorded here and in the activity Source line.
- Capt. Thomas Preston, account of the Boston Massacre, 1770. Audited TPSP bank record preston-case-1770; edition named in the record: Public Advertiser, London, April 28, 1770, via the Gilder Lehrman Institute.
- Samuel Drowne, testimony of March 16, 1770. Audited TPSP bank record drowne-testimony-1770; edition named in the record: A Short Narrative of the Horrid Massacre in Boston, 1770, original at the Massachusetts Historical Society, spelling modernized as the record states.
- Paul Revere (after Henry Pelham), The Bloody Massacre in King-Street, 1770. Audited TPSP bank record revere-boston-massacre-1770; the record text field is the image description and the transcribed title and imprint, Library of Congress.
