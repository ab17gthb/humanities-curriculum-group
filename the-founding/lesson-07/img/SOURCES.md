## Subjects for which no public domain image is used, recorded honestly
- The Convention in session: no picture of the Convention at work was made in 1787, because the Convention sat in secret. The famous image of the signing, Howard Chandler Christy's painting of 1940, is a later artist's imagining made more than 150 years after the event. It is not used in this lesson, in the documents or in the deck, and no image in this lesson is captioned as showing the Convention itself.
- Benjamin Franklin: no Franklin portrait was downloaded for this lesson. Franklin is carried in his own words, in Madison's record of his closing speech, in the reading, at Activity Station 5, and in the deck notes.
- The delegates as a group: no group portrait from the period exists. The room is carried by Birch's State House print, the document by its engrossed and printed pages, and the two leading figures by the Stuart and Vanderlyn portraits listed above.

## Quoted texts and their editions
All period quotations in this lesson are drawn from the audited Primary Source Project bank file founding.json or from the named transcriptions below, and each document's Source line names the edition used:
- Framers of the Constitution, "Constitution, Preamble," 1787 (National Archives), bank record constitution-preamble. Quoted entire in the activity at Station 1 and in the quick check. The bank record's text reads "defence" in the British spelling the engrossed Constitution carries, and the quotations here keep it.
- James Madison, "Madison's Notes of the Debates," September 17, 1787 (Yale Avalon Project), bank record madison-notes-debates-1787. Quoted at Activity Station 2, with cuts marked by ellipses. Franklin's speech and the rising sun passage both stand in this record.
- George Washington, "Washington's Annotated Draft of the Constitution," 1787 (Library of Congress; exhibition description), bank record washington-annotated-draft-constitution-1787. The bank record's text field describes the artifact and is quoted at Activity Station 3 as a description, with the bank headnote's correction stated on the station's face: the exhibition label quoted in the text names Carpenters' Hall, but the Convention met in the Pennsylvania State House.
- Constitution of the United States, Article I, Section 2 (National Archives, Charters of Freedom transcription, archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript). Quoted at Activity Station 4, with cuts marked by ellipses. Spelling and capitalization follow the National Archives transcription, including "chuse" in the surrounding text and "three fifths of all other Persons" in the clause itself.
- Benjamin Franklin, closing speech, September 17, 1787, in James Madison's Notes of the Debates (Avalon Project, Yale Law School, avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/debates_917.asp). Quoted at Activity Station 5, with cuts marked by ellipses. The speech was written out by Franklin and read aloud by James Wilson, as both the Avalon text and the bank record state.
- No period text is quoted from memory anywhere in this lesson. No summary in this lesson is presented as a transcription. The one described artifact, Washington's annotated draft, is labeled a description on its face at Station 3.
