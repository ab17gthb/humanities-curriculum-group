Teacher Answer Key

StageDateThe rule of belongingWho stood insideWho stood outside, or paid
Plymouth1620Government by the consent of the governed, made in the Mayflower Compact before landing outside the patent.The forty-one men who signed, Pilgrims and Strangers together.Women, children, and servants were governed but did not sign. The Wampanoag stood outside entirely; Massasoit dealt with Plymouth as an ally, for Wampanoag reasons, after the epidemic of 1616 to 1619.
Massachusetts Bay1630 onwardA covenant commonwealth: Winthrop's city upon a hill, churches examining members, freemen voting, ministers paid by taxes on all.Admitted church members, the freemen, who alone voted for governor and magistrates.Everyone paid the minister's tax. Dissenters stood outside the covenant's protection: Williams banished in 1636, Hutchinson tried in 1637 and banished.
Connecticut1639The Fundamental Orders: a written frame made without a royal charter, the General Court holding the supreme power, its stated purpose the liberty and purity of the Gospel.Freemen of Windsor, Hartford, and Wethersfield, choosing governor, magistrates, and deputies each year.Voting belonged to freemen alone, admitted male church members; everyone else lived under the frame without a vote.
The wars1637, and 1675 to 1676Expansion onto Native land, fifty years of it, until Native nations answered together.English towns and their Native allies in 1637; Metacom's Wampanoag, Narragansett, and Nipmuc coalition in 1675.Hundreds of Pequot men, women, and children killed at Mystic in 1637. In King Philip's War, the deadliest per capita war in the colonies' history, captives were sold into Caribbean slavery and Native power in southern New England was broken.
Salem1692A covenant community watching for the devil inside itself, its courts admitting spectral evidence.The afflicted accusers and the court, whose testimony about spirits only they could see counted as evidence.The accused, who could not disprove a spirit's visit: nineteen hanged, Giles Corey pressed to death, before ministers attacked the evidence and the colony repented.

The covenant in one sentence. Strong answers hold strength and boundary in one thought. The community held together so long as its members accepted the covenant's terms, consent at the founding, watchfulness in the towns, and one approved faith, and it expelled whoever the terms excluded: Williams and Hutchinson were put out for teaching against the terms, Native nations were never offered membership at any price, and at Salem the terms turned inward and consumed members in good standing on evidence no one could answer.
