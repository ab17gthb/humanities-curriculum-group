# Image Sources: Lesson 4, Relief, Recovery, and the Alphabet Agencies at Work

All images are public domain as stated on their Wikimedia Commons file pages. Downloads were made from Wikimedia Commons on October 21, 2026, and each file was verified as a valid image with PIL. The three TIFF downloads (CCC enrollee planting tree, PWA construction site, NRA Blue Eagle poster) were converted to JPEG copies for use, with no other alteration beyond cropping and resizing for the deck crops. Original downloads live in assets/. Deck crops live in slides-build/img/ and were cropped from the downloads listed here.

## Drillers on the Hoover Dam construction (hoover-dam-drillers.jpg; deck crop dam-port.jpg)
- Title: "Negroes employed as drillers on the construction of Hoover Dam." (historical title as stated on the Commons file page)
- Creator: Unknown author, as stated on the Commons file page
- Date: October 3, 1932
- Holder: United States National Archives and Records Administration, record 293747
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%22Negroes_employed_as_drillers_on_the_construction_of_Hoover_Dam.%22_-_NARA_-_293747.jpg
- Public domain basis: Commons file page marks the image Public domain as a work of the United States federal government held by the National Archives. Note on use: this photograph is dated 1932, before the Public Works Administration was created, and it is used in this lesson to show the scale of federal dam construction work of the period. It is not a PWA project photograph. The PWA construction site photograph above is the PWA image in this lesson.
