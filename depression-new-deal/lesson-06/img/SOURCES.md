# Image Sources for Depression and New Deal Lesson 6, The Second New Deal

All images are real public domain images, works of the United States federal government or held by the Library of Congress, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-06/assets/` on October 21, 2026 (depression-new-deal build session). Deck crops in `slides-build/img/` were made from these same files: portrait crops at 1046 by 1500 for the photo slides and one 16:9 crop for the title slide. Each download was verified as a valid image file with PIL and checked visually against its catalog description. No image was altered beyond cropping and resizing.

## 1. wpa-camp-timberline-1936.jpg (used in Activity Station 1; title slide crop)

- Title: WPA Camp, Larch Mountain Road, Mount Hood National Forest
- Creator: United States Forest Service (photograph 340842)
- Date: 1936
- What it shows: The Works Progress Administration camp housing crews who built Timberline Lodge on Mount Hood; the Commons description records the lodge's 1936 to 1938 building costs from WPA records. Verified visually on download.
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Operation_and_Information_WPA_Camp-1936.jpg
- Public domain basis: Work of a United States federal government employee. Wikimedia Commons classifies the file as public domain.

## 2. roosevelt-signs-social-security-act-1935.jpg (used in the quick check; deck portrait crop signing-port.jpg)

- Title: Roosevelt Signs The Social Security Act
- Creator: Photograph distributed by Social Security Online; held by the Library of Congress
- Date: August 14, 1935, at approximately 3:30 pm EST, per the Commons description
- Holder: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, digital ID cph.3c23278
- What it shows: President Roosevelt signing the act at his desk, with Secretary of Labor Frances Perkins standing behind him at the right of the frame, in the dark dress and hat. Verified visually on download.
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Signing_Of_The_Social_Security_Act.jpg
- Public domain basis: Wikimedia Commons classifies the file as public domain.

## 3. wpa-paved-street-opelousas-1936.jpg (used in the notebook page; deck portrait crop wpa-port.jpg)

- Title: Street which had recently been paved by WPA workers in Opelousas, Louisiana
- Creator: Unnamed photographer for the Works Progress Administration
- Date: November 1936
- Holder: Louisiana Digital Library (state-lwp:238), via Wikimedia Commons
- What it shows: The finished paved street in Opelousas, St. Landry Parish. The photograph shows the completed street, not the crew at work, and the notebook caption says so by describing it as the street WPA workers paved. Verified visually on download.
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Street_which_had_recently_been_paved_by_WPA_workers_in_Opelousas,_Louisiana_1936.jpg
- Public domain basis: Work of the Works Progress Administration, a federal agency. Wikimedia Commons classifies the file as public domain.

## Quoted texts and teaching materials that are not images

- The reading, Activity Station 2, and the quick check quote the Social Security Act of 1935, Public Law 74-271, Title II, section 202(a), from the United States Statutes at Large (49 Stat. 620). Cuts are marked with ellipses where they occur, as each Source line states. Activity Station 3 quotes the act's definition of employment in section 210, the exception list beginning "(1) Agricultural labor; (2) Domestic service in a private home," with cuts marked.
- Activity Station 5's table gives the top rates of the Revenue Act of 1935, Public Law 74-198, as enacted: individual income tax 75 percent above $5,000,000, estate tax 70 percent above $50,000,000, corporation income tax graduated to 15 percent.
- Activity Stations 1 and 4 are summaries prepared for this lesson and are labeled as summaries in their Source lines. Station 4 describes a typical case of the labor board's early practice and states on its face that it is not the record of one named case.
