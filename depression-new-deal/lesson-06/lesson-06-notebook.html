Teacher Answer Key

1935 measurePermanent duty acceptedWho it coveredWho it left out, or its limit
Works Progress AdministrationDirect federal employment of the jobless on public projects, run by Harry Hopkins from May 1935.About eight and a half million people across its life, about three million at its height, building roads, schools, courthouses, and airports, plus the Federal One arts projects.It was a program, not a permanent entitlement: it ended in 1943, and it reached only the jobless whom state and local projects could take on.
Social Security ActStanding insurance against old age and job loss, plus aid to dependent children, signed August 14, 1935 and funded by a payroll tax paid by workers and matched by employers.Retired workers in covered employment, drawing a monthly benefit as a matter of right from age sixty-five, and workers covered by state unemployment insurance.Agricultural and domestic workers were outside the founding definition of employment, an exclusion that fell hardest on Black workers and on women.
Wagner ActFederal protection of the right to organize and bargain collectively, enforced by the National Labor Relations Board from July 5, 1935.Most private sector workers who chose a union, through board run elections, with employer interference defined as an unfair labor practice.The right depended on the board's enforcement and survived only as the board and the courts upheld it; it granted no wage or job directly.
Revenue Act of 1935Financing the new federal duties from the top of the income scale, the wealth tax.As taxpayers, the largest incomes (seventy-five percent above five million dollars), large estates, and corporations.It raised only part of the cost of the new programs and touched only a handful of taxpayers at its top rate.

The settlement in one sentence. Strong answers name the permanence. In 1935 the federal government accepted standing responsibility for work, old age, unemployment, and labor rights, funded by taxes written into law, and it drew the boundary of that responsibility so that farm and domestic workers stood outside it at the start.
