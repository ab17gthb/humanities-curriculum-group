# Image Sources for Depression and New Deal Lesson 5, The Critics: Long, Townsend, Coughlin, and the Court

All images are real public domain images held by the Library of Congress, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons into `lesson-05/assets/` on October 21, 2026 (depression-new-deal build session). Deck crops in `slides-build/img/` were made from these same files: portrait crops at 1046 by 1500 for the photo slides and one 16:9 crop for the title slide. Each download was verified as a valid image file with PIL and checked visually against its catalog description. No image was altered beyond cropping and resizing.

## 1. huey-long-filibuster-1935.jpg (used in the quick check; title slide crop)

- Title: A long filibuster, Washington, D.C. Senator Long leaving the Senate floor after a five and a half hour filibuster
- Creator: Harris & Ewing, photographer
- Date: August 27, 1935, as dated in the Commons record
- Holder: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, digital ID hec 39385
- What it shows: Long in a doorway after the filibuster, about two weeks before his assassination. Verified visually on download.
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Huey_Long_1935_LOC_hec_39385.jpg
- Public domain basis: Wikimedia Commons classifies the file as public domain. The Library of Congress places no known restrictions on Harris & Ewing collection photographs.

- Title: Senator Huey P. Long of Louisiana, half-length portrait, standing, facing left, gesturing with both arms, as he speaks

## 3. francis-townsend-microphones.jpg (used in the notebook page; deck portrait crop townsend-port.jpg)

- Title: Francis Townsend, seated at desk, with microphones
- Creator: Harris & Ewing
- Date: 1939, as dated in the Commons record of the Library of Congress file. The Townsend movement belongs to 1934 and 1935; the photograph is used as a portrait of Townsend at the microphones, and the notebook caption does not claim a 1934 date for it.
- Holder: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, digital ID hec 27728
- What it shows: Townsend seated at a desk with two NBC microphones, papers in hand. Verified visually on download.
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Francis_Townsend,_seated_at_desk,_with_microphones_hec.27728.jpg
- Public domain basis: Wikimedia Commons classifies the file as public domain. The Library of Congress places no known restrictions on Harris & Ewing collection photographs.

## Quoted texts and teaching materials that are not images

- Activity Station 1 quotes Huey Long's Share Our Wealth radio address of February 23, 1934, as printed in Long's official Share Our Wealth pamphlet (text as published by the Long Legacy Project at hueylong.com). Cuts are marked with ellipses, as the station's Source line states.
- Activity Station 5 and the quick check quote the syllabus of Schechter Poultry Corporation v. United States, 295 U.S. 495 (1935), from the United States Reports text of the syllabus. The activity prints syllabus points 1 and 2 in full and point 4 with cuts marked by ellipses, and states that point 3 is omitted.
- Activity Stations 2, 3, and 4 are summaries prepared for this lesson. Each is labeled as a summary in its own Source line, following the unit rule that Coughlin's broadcast wording is paraphrased unless a verifiable transcript is quoted. No wording in those stations is presented as a quotation.
