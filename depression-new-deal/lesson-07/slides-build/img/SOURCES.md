# Image Sources for Depression and New Deal Lesson 7, Labor Rising and the Recession of 1937

All images are real public domain photographs held by the Library of Congress and distributed through Wikimedia Commons. They were downloaded on October 21, 2026. Each download was opened and verified with PIL, and the principal photographs were inspected: the files show what their captions in the documents say they show. Deck versions are cropped to portrait (1046 by 1500) or to 16:9 for the title slide; no other alteration.

## 1. flint-window.jpg (reading figure; deck title and portrait crop)

- Title: Sit-down strikers guarding window entrance to Fisher body plant number three, Flint, Michigan
- What it shows: Strikers seated on car seat cushions inside the plant at the window entrance, guarding it during the occupation. Verified by inspection of the downloaded file.
- Creator: Sheldon Dick
- Date: 1937
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Farm Security Administration / Office of War Information collection, digital ID fsa.8c28670, via Wikimedia Commons, file "Flint Sit-Down Strike window.jpg"
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flint_Sit-Down_Strike_window.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph made for the Farm Security Administration, a United States federal agency. The Wikimedia Commons file page tags it Public domain as a work of the United States federal government.
