Teacher Answer Key

Part of the yearThe factsOne name, place, or date that proves it
The CIO organizes by industryThe Congress of Industrial Organizations, formed in 1935 under John L. Lewis, organized every worker in a plant, skilled or unskilled, into one union, against the older federation's craft by craft pattern. The Wagner Act's guarantee stood behind the organizing.CIO, 1935; John L. Lewis; craft against industry.
Flint: the sit-down winsWorkers held the Fisher Body plants from inside for forty-four days, organized with committees and a women's auxiliary, and repelled an attempt to retake a plant. Governor Murphy used the National Guard to keep order, not to evict. GM recognized the United Auto Workers.Flint, Michigan; December 30, 1936 to February 11, 1937; the Battle of the Running Bulls, January 11.
Membership, and the cost at Republic SteelUnion membership climbed from under three million in 1933 toward nine million by the end of the decade. At Republic Steel in Chicago, police fired on a Memorial Day march of strikers and families and killed ten; a Senate committee found the violence unjustified.Memorial Day, May 30, 1937; Republic Steel, Chicago; ten killed.
The court packing fightAfter his 1936 landslide, Roosevelt proposed an added justice for each sitting justice over seventy, up to six. The country read it as an attack on the Court. The Senate killed the bill in July 1937, while the Court itself upheld the Wagner Act in April and Social Security in May.Message of February 5, 1937; forty-six of forty-eight states in 1936; Senate vote, July 1937.
The Roosevelt recessionFederal spending cuts and the new Social Security payroll taxes together pulled money out of a weak economy. Production fell by about a third, and unemployment climbed from about fourteen percent in 1937 back toward nineteen percent in 1938.Payroll taxes began January 1937; production index 117 in August 1937 to 76 in May 1938.
The lesson carried into 1938In the spring of 1938 the administration asked Congress for new spending and got it, accepting deficit spending in practice: in a slump, government spending beyond its revenue puts money back into the economy.Spring 1938 spending program; deficit spending.

The lesson in one sentence. Strong answers complete the sentence with one win and one loss or cost. Example: The year 1937 gave American labor its greatest victories, recognition at General Motors and a membership climbing toward nine million, in the same year the President lost the court packing fight and the government's own cuts and taxes produced the Roosevelt recession.
