# The Depression and New Deal Unit: Build Manifest

Every finished item, its file, its page count, and the test-blueprint entries it teaches or assesses. Blueprint numbers are from depression-new-deal-skeleton.md. Every document ships as an HTML source with a PDF of the same name, built with templates/build_pdf.py and render-checked before listing. The full unit is 51 documents and 218 printed pages, plus 8 slide decks (207 slides) with a printable teacher notes document for each deck.

**Unit essential question:** How did the New Deal change the relationship between Americans and their federal government, and what did it leave undone?
