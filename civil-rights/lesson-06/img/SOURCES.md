## Note on photographs not used
The famous Bloody Sunday photographs of March 7, 1965 are copyrighted press photographs and are not used anywhere in this lesson. Bloody Sunday is taught from the statute file texts, the reading's plain statement of the attack, and the registration figures. The Selma images used here show the completed march under federal protection later in March 1965 and are captioned for what they show. No photograph of the Chaney, Goodman, and Schwerner murders or of any victim is used; the murders are stated in plain words in the reading and the notes.
