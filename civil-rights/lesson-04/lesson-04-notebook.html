The students' arithmetic in one sentence. Strong answers state the rule and its limit together. Student protest moved the federal government when it created a crisis on ground where federal power already had a claim, as the Freedom Rides did under Boynton, producing marshals and the ICC regulations of 1961. It failed to move Washington when a disciplined local opponent absorbed the protest without violence or overflowing jails, as Pritchett did in Albany, and it won without Washington at all when the crisis was aimed at a city government that could be made to answer, as in Nashville.
