# The Civil Rights Unit: Build Manifest

Every finished item, its file, its page count, and the test-blueprint entries it teaches or assesses. Blueprint numbers are from civil-rights-skeleton.md. Every document ships as an HTML source with a PDF of the same name, built with templates/build_pdf.py and render-checked before listing. The full unit is 51 documents and 234 printed pages, plus 8 slide decks (209 slides) with a printable teacher notes document for each deck (77 pages).

**Unit essential question:** How did the civil rights movement change American law and American life, and what did it leave unfinished?
