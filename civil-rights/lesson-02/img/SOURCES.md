## Subjects for which no public domain image is used, recorded honestly
- Charles Hamilton Houston: the portrait carried on Wikimedia Commons (File:Charles Hamilton Houston.jpg, dated November 20, 1939) rests on a PD-US-no-notice claim with a Bettmann Archive and Getty Images credit. That basis is not a federal government basis and was not relied on for this lesson. No portrait of Houston is used; the deck teaches his strategy in text.
- Linda Brown and the Topeka plaintiffs: the well known 1953 photographs of the Brown family and of Monroe Elementary classrooms are Carl Iwasaki press photographs held in the LIFE Images Collection via Getty Images. They are not public domain and are not used.
- Prince Edward County and the school closings: the surviving photographs of the closed schools and of the Prince Edward Academy are press and local photographs with no public domain basis verified on a Commons file page or holding archive record for this build. Station 5 of the activity is therefore a text summary on its face, and the deck teaches the closing in text and figures.
- The Southern Manifesto and Senator Harry F. Byrd: no photograph with a verified public domain basis on its Commons file page was used for either subject in this build. Both are taught in text.
