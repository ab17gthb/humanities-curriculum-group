Quick Check: The Northern Problem

Directions: Read the document and the background, then answer the questions.

Document. Report of the National Advisory Commission on Civil Disorders, Introduction, March 1, 1968, printed from the audited TPSP bank record. Edition named in the bank record: Teaching American History.

"This is our basic conclusion: Our nation is moving toward two societies, one black, one white, separate and unequal."

"What white Americans have never fully understood but what the Negro can never forget, is that white society is deeply implicated in the ghetto. White institutions created it, white institutions maintain it, and white society condones it."

Background. The commission wrote after the movement had turned north. The Voting Rights Act had been signed on August 6, 1965, and Watts rose five days later. In Chicago in 1966, open housing marches were met by mobs, and the city's Summit Agreement went unenforced. That same summer Stokely Carmichael raised the call of Black Power in Mississippi, and Huey Newton and Bobby Seale founded the Black Panther Party in Oakland. In April 1968 Martin Luther King Jr. was assassinated in Memphis while supporting the sanitation workers' strike.

1. State the commission's basic conclusion, naming the two societies and the two adjectives it gives them.

2. In the second passage, which two subjects hold responsibility for creating and maintaining the ghetto, and in the commission's verbs?

3. Using the background, name the event that followed the Voting Rights Act by five days, and state what it showed about the reach of the new law.

4. Using the background, name two answers the movement produced in 1966 to the failure of its older methods, one a slogan and one an organization, with the person or persons attached to each.

Teacher Answer Key

1. State the commission's basic conclusion. The nation is moving toward two societies, one black, one white, separate and unequal. Credit requires the direction of movement and both adjectives. (Blueprint 13)

2. Which two subjects hold responsibility, and in which verbs? White institutions created the ghetto and white institutions maintain it, and white society condones it. The first two verbs belong to institutions, the third to society. Answers that assign the responsibility to the ghetto's residents have reversed the document. (Blueprint 13)

3. Name the event that followed the Voting Rights Act by five days, and state what it showed. The Watts uprising in Los Angeles, six days of violence in August 1965 in which thirty four people died. It showed that a law striking at southern voting statutes did not reach the northern grievances residents named, police treatment, poor housing, low pay, and weak schools, in a city whose segregation was de facto rather than written in law. (Blueprint 13)

4. Name two answers the movement produced in 1966, a slogan and an organization. The slogan is Black Power, raised by Stokely Carmichael at Greenwood, Mississippi in June 1966 during the Meredith March. The organization is the Black Panther Party, founded in Oakland in October 1966 by Huey Newton and Bobby Seale. Both the names and the people are required for full credit. (Blueprint 14)

Teacher note. This check is formative. Questions 1 and 2 test close reading of the commission's own sentences, which the writing task in this lesson also uses. Questions 3 and 4 test the sequence the lesson is built on: the law signed, Watts five days later, Chicago and the unenforced agreement, and then the movement's own argument carried into a slogan and an organization in a single year.
