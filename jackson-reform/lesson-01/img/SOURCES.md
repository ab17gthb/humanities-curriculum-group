## Subjects for which no separate image was used
- No period image of the House of Representatives voting in February 1825, of a state suffrage convention, or of the New Jersey and Pennsylvania closings was downloaded for this lesson; no verifiable public domain image for those subjects was located in this build. The deck uses text and stat slides for those sections rather than a later artist imagining, and this file records that choice honestly.

## Text editions used in this lesson (recorded here as the spec directs)
- Andrew Jackson, First Annual Message to Congress, December 8, 1829, rotation passage. Edition named: James D. Richardson, editor, A Compilation of the Messages and Papers of the Presidents (Washington, 1896). The text was fetched and verified during this build against the Miller Center (University of Virginia) transcription of the message and against the Richardson volume text held at archive.org; the passages quoted agree across both. Quoted verbatim, cuts marked with ellipses and disclosed in the Source lines. Named edition, not a TPSP bank record: the bank file for this era (jackson-reform.json) carries no Lesson 1 station text.
- Margaret Bayard Smith, letter of March 1829 on the inauguration. Edition named: The First Forty Years of Washington Society, edited by Gaillard Hunt (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1906), the inauguration letter at pages 290 to 297 of that edition. The text was fetched during this build from the archive.org scan of the 1906 edition and quoted from it, including its spelling of negros. Quoted verbatim, cuts marked with ellipses and disclosed in the Source lines. Named edition, not a bank record.
- The widening suffrage (activity Station 3). Labeled teaching summary on its face, written for this lesson from the state constitutions and suffrage laws of the 1810s and 1820s. Not a quotation.
- The corrupt bargain record (activity Station 4). Labeled teaching summary on its face, written for this lesson from the public record of the election of 1824 and the House of Representatives election of February 9, 1825. Not a quotation.
- The exclusion record (activity Station 5). Labeled teaching summary on its face, written for this lesson from the New Jersey suffrage law of 1807 and the Pennsylvania constitution of 1838. Not a quotation.
