# Lesson 1 deck images: sources

Every image in this folder is a period photograph or cartoon from before
1928, public domain in the United States, as identified in the deck's
speaker notes. Portrait files are crops for the deck's right-side photo
slots; the 16:9 files are full-frame title and divider images. Crops change
framing only; nothing is stretched, generated, or retouched.

- `title-169.jpg`: Mulberry Street, New York, about 1900. Detroit
  Publishing Company photograph, Library of Congress. Public domain:
  published about 1900.
- `spinner-port.jpg`: A young spinner in a cotton mill, photographed by
  Lewis Hine for the National Child Labor Committee, early 1900s. Public
  domain: published before 1928.
- `work-port.jpg`: Breaker boys inside a Pennsylvania coal breaker, 1911,
  photographed by Lewis Hine. Public domain: published 1911.
- `breaker-port.jpg`: Breaker boys outside the Ewen Breaker, South
  Pittston, Pennsylvania, 1911, photographed by Lewis Hine. Public domain:
  published 1911.
- `tenement-port.jpg`: Period tenement photograph used on the Problem Two
  divider, published before 1928. Public domain.
- `lodgers-port.jpg`: Lodgers in a Bayard Street tenement, New York, about
  1890 — a police photograph associated with Jacob Riis's reporting.
  Public domain: published about 1890.
- `riis-port.jpg`: Portrait of Jacob A. Riis, head and shoulders, Library
  of Congress (compare LCCN2005678010). Public domain: published before
  1928.
- `nast-port.jpg`: Thomas Nast's cartoon work against the Tweed Ring,
  published in Harper's Weekly in the 1870s (the activity's station uses
  Nast's 1872 cartoon against Boss Tweed). Public domain: published in the
  1870s.
- `octopus-169.jpg`: "The Octopus" cartoon of Standard Oil, Puck magazine,
  1904 — the octopus's tentacles reach around steel, copper, shipping, the
  Capitol, and toward the White House. Public domain: published 1904.
