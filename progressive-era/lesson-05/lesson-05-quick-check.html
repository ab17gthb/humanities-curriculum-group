Part 2: Short Answer

7. Woodrow Wilson won 435 electoral votes with only about 42 percent of the popular vote. Explain how a candidate wins a landslide in electoral votes while most voters choose someone else.

8. Taft filed more antitrust suits than Roosevelt did, yet progressives treated Roosevelt as the true reformer in 1912. Give two reasons from the lesson why reputation and record parted company.

Teacher Answer Key

Part 1: 1-C, 2-D, 3-A, 4-E, 5-B, 6-F.

7. The Republican vote split between Taft and Roosevelt, so Wilson finished first in state after state with a plurality, and electoral votes are awarded by state. A divided majority loses to a united minority; Roosevelt and Taft together outpolled Wilson by over a million votes while winning 96 electoral votes between them.

8. Any two: Taft praised the Payne-Aldrich Tariff instead of fighting for the promised cuts; he fired Pinchot, the conservation movement's hero, in the Ballinger affair; his judicial, case-by-case manner looked like defense of the status quo; the U.S. Steel suit made the conflict with Roosevelt personal and public. The record (ninety suits) could not overcome the signals.

Teacher note. Formative. Question 7 is the keep-or-reteach item: students who cannot explain the plurality mechanic will miss why the split mattered, which Lesson 6 assumes when it describes Wilson governing on a minority mandate.
