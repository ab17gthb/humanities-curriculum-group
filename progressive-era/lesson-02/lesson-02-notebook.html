Teacher Answer Key

MuckrakerTargetWork and methodWhat changed
Jacob RiisTenement housing and urban poverty in New York.How the Other Half Lives (1890); flash photography plus firsthand description of the slums he walked.His photographs and his argument made tenement poverty a public issue; proof that photographs could move public opinion.
Ida TarbellStandard Oil's rebates, espionage, and forced buyouts of rivals.History of the Standard Oil Company, serialized in McClure's 1902 to 1904; built from court records and interviews.The Supreme Court ordered Standard Oil dissolved into separate companies in 1911.
Lincoln SteffensCorruption in American city governments, as a system joining bribe payers and bribe takers.The Shame of the Cities (1904); reported city by city, comparing the same pattern in each.Municipal corruption became a national issue; his systemic framing shaped later reform campaigns.
Upton SinclairThe meatpacking industry: conditions for workers and contamination of food.The Jungle (1906), a novel based on seven weeks investigating Chicago's Packingtown.The Meat Inspection Act and the Pure Food and Drug Act, both 1906.
Jane AddamsPoverty, labor conditions, and neglect in Chicago's immigrant neighborhoods.Twenty Years at Hull-House (1910); wrote from residence, running the settlement house she described.Her reporting modeled a second kind of exposure, stay, document, publish; settlement house networks supplied trained reformers across the movement.

The method. Document a specific harm with evidence the public can check, publish it to a mass audience, and let public pressure force a political response. Accept any single element (evidence, audience, or pressure) defended from the chart.
