The Referee

The 1902 coal strike does not fit neatly into any of the three sides. In one or two sentences, state what role Roosevelt played in the strike and why that role was new for a president.

Teacher Answer Key

SideThe problem it answeredWhat Roosevelt did, with one example
Controlling the corporationsTrusts and railroad combinations large enough to set prices and rates without competition or oversight.Sued under the Sherman Act (Northern Securities dissolved, 1904), created the Bureau of Corporations to investigate, and signed the Hepburn Act (1906) letting the ICC set maximum railroad rates.
Protecting the consumerContaminated and falsely labeled food and drugs sold nationally with no federal inspection.After The Jungle and his own investigation, pushed Congress to pass the Meat Inspection Act and the Pure Food and Drug Act, both in 1906.
Conserving natural resourcesForests, water, and wildlife being consumed or destroyed with no plan for the future.Set aside about 230 million acres: 150 national forests, five national parks, 51 bird reservations, and 18 national monuments under the Antiquities Act, managed by experts under Gifford Pinchot.

The referee. Roosevelt acted as a neutral arbiter between the miners and the owners, pressuring the owners with a threat to seize the mines until they accepted arbitration. Earlier presidents had used federal power on the owners' side against strikers; Roosevelt's neutrality, and the miners' partial victory, were the new pattern.
