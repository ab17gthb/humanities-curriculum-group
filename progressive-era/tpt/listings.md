## Unit bundle | full listing

**Title:** Progressive Era Unit Bundle | 8 Lessons, Review Day, and Unit Test | US History

**Description:**

The complete Progressive Era unit. Eight lessons, a review day, and a 50-point unit test, all print-ready, all built on one plate so every lesson works the same way.

Each lesson includes a reading with questions, guided notes with a filled key, and an activity or writing task. Most lessons add a notebook page or a quick check. Every file prints on US Letter in black text that copies cleanly, with the teacher answer key at the back of the same file.

What is included:

- Lesson 1, The Problems Progressives Faced. Reading, guided notes, the Reformer's Notebook stations activity, a notebook page, and a quick check built on a photograph of a Triangle fire escape.
- Lesson 2, The Muckrakers. Reading on Tarbell, Steffens, Sinclair, and Phillips, guided notes, an activity where students write their own exposé, and a notebook page.
- Lesson 3, Reforming Government. Reading on the initiative, referendum, recall, direct primaries, and the Seventeenth Amendment, guided notes, a ballot simulation for a 1908 mill town, and a quick check.
- Lesson 4, Roosevelt and the Square Deal. Reading, guided notes, conservation map work, and a three-sided notebook organizer covering trust-busting, consumer protection, and conservation.
- Lesson 5, Taft, the Split, and the Election of 1912. Reading, guided notes, a 1912 comparison chart activity, a campaign poster or speech activity, and a quick check.
- Lesson 6, Wilson and the New Freedom. Reading on the tariff, the Federal Reserve, the Clayton Act, and the Federal Trade Commission, guided notes, a sorting activity that separates the New Freedom from the New Nationalism, and a notebook organizer.
- Lesson 7, The Fight for Women's Suffrage. Reading, guided notes, a strategy memo writing task on the state road against the federal amendment, and a quick check.
- Lesson 8, The Limits of Progressivism. Reading on race, immigration, labor, and Prohibition, guided notes, and seminar prep writing on how much actually changed.
- Review day. A study guide with the term list, a table of the four amendments, questions for every lesson, and a full key, plus a 22-answer crossword with its filled key.
- Unit test. 50 points: 15 matching, 21 multiple choice, 14 source-based, working from Roosevelt's muck-rake speech, the 1912 election results, and the text of the Nineteenth Amendment. Every item is tagged in the key to the unit's published blueprint of fifteen topics, and every topic is assessed at least twice.
