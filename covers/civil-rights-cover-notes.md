# The Civil Rights cover notes

Built 2026-10-22, following the construction of the Cold War cover (cover-cold-war.html): navy background, cream wordmark, rust rule, one period photograph, contents lines, Knoweth footer. Source file: cover-civil-rights.html. Outputs: civil-rights-cover.png (1632 x 2112) and civil-rights-cover.pdf (printable, US Letter).

## Photograph

- Title: Civil Rights March on Washington, D.C. (Leaders marching from the Washington Monument to the Lincoln Memorial), National Archives identifier 542010
- What it actually is: The front rank of the March on Washington, the march's leaders walking from the Washington Monument grounds to the Lincoln Memorial on August 28, 1963, marchers and their signs behind them. The same photograph ships in the unit itself, in Lesson 5 (assets/march-leaders.jpg; the lesson's deck crops leaders-port.jpg and title-169.jpg derive from it), and the unit test presents the march through the audited bank record's visual description.
- Creator: Rowland Scherman, United States Information Agency, Press and Publications Service, as credited in the National Archives record carried on the Commons file page and in Lesson 5's SOURCES.md
- Date: August 28, 1963
- Repository: National Archives at College Park, via Wikimedia Commons
- Identifiers: National Archives identifier 542010; Commons file "Civil_Rights_March_on_Washington,_D.C._(Leaders_marching_from_the_Washington_Monument_to_the_Lincoln_Memorial)_-_NARA_-_542010.jpg"
- Source URLs: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Civil_Rights_March_on_Washington,_D.C._(Leaders_marching_from_the_Washington_Monument_to_the_Lincoln_Memorial)_-_NARA_-_542010.jpg
- Public domain basis: A work of the United States federal government, a United States Information Agency photograph held by the National Archives, public domain as a federal work. Lesson 5's SOURCES.md records the same basis, and the audited Primary Source Project records for the march (march-on-washington-1963, march-on-washington-scherman-1963) carry the Scherman attribution for the day's federal photographs.
- The copy held locally is covers/civil-rights-march-leaders-1963.jpg (1920 x 1278), the unit's own shipped copy of the photograph, taken from Lesson 5's assets. The cover HTML renders from it directly; at 1920 px wide it prints cleanly at the cover frame's size, so no separate downscaled render copy was needed. The cover frame crop (object-fit: cover, object-position center 30%) holds the front rank of the leaders and the signs above them; visually checked.
