## Photograph

- Title: East and West Shaking Hands at Laying of Last Rail
- Creator: Andrew J. Russell, photographer for the Union Pacific Railroad
- Date: May 10, 1869, Promontory Summit, Utah
- Repository: Yale University Libraries, via Wikimedia Commons (the Library of Congress holds prints of the same Russell negative)
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:East_and_West_Shaking_hands_at_the_laying_of_last_rail_Union_Pacific_Railroad.jpg
- Public domain basis: Photograph taken in 1869 and published in the United States before 1929. Copyright expired.
- The image comes from the unit's own lesson assets: gilded-age/lesson-01/assets/promontory-1869.jpg, where it is used in the Lesson 1 quick check. Full sourcing for the lesson's images is in gilded-age/lesson-01/img/SOURCES.md.
