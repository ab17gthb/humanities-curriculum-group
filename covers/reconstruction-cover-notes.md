## Image

- Title: "The first vote" / AW [monogram]; drawn by A. R. Waud.
- What it actually is: the wood engraving of Black men casting ballots under the Reconstruction Acts, published in Harper's Weekly on November 16, 1867, the era's defining picture of the new electorate the unit's Lesson 4 teaches. The Library of Congress sheet held locally includes the paper's masthead above the engraving, dating the document on its face. No image of the Klan, of a riot, or of any victim appears on the cover, following the unit's stated image discipline; nor is any of the three cartoons the unit teaches by description only (Nast's "Emancipation," Nast's "Worse Than Slavery," and Frost's 1876 ticket cartoon) used as the cover or as decoration anywhere in this integration.
- Creator: Alfred R. Waud (artist), Harper's Weekly (publisher)
- Date: November 16, 1867
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division
- Identifiers: LOC item 00651117 (LCCN 00651117), reproduction number LC-DIG-ppmsca-37947 (digital file from original print), call number Illus. in AP2.H32 1867 (Case Y) [P&P], record https://www.loc.gov/item/00651117/
- Public domain basis: a wood engraving published in the United States in 1867 and long out of copyright on any reading; the Library of Congress record for the item carries the rights advisory "No known restrictions on publication." The record was verified directly through the Library of Congress's own item record (JSON) for this integration.
- The copy held locally is covers/first-vote-waud-1867.jpg (4536 x 6526), the same verified Library of Congress file the unit ships in Lesson 4 (lesson-04/img/first-vote.jpg), where the lesson's SOURCES.md records the verification chain (Library of Congress file by way of Wikimedia Commons, public domain tag confirmed through the Commons API). The bank record first-vote-waud-1867 carries the same engraving. The cover frame crop (object-fit: cover, object-position center 30%) holds the masthead, the flag, and the voters' row; visually checked.
