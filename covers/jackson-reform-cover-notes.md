## Image

- Title: Andrew Jackson
- What it actually is: Thomas Sully's oil portrait of Andrew Jackson, painted in 1845, the year of Jackson's death, when he was seventy-eight. It is the portrait of the man the unit's first four lessons argue about, the people's president of 1828 grown old, and it ships in the unit itself, in Lesson 1 (img/jackson-sully.jpg), where the lesson's SOURCES.md carries its record and the notebook page and deck portrait crops derive from it.
- Creator: Thomas Sully
- Date: 1845
- Repository: National Gallery of Art, Washington
- Identifiers: accession number 1942.8.34, credit line "Andrew W. Mellon Collection," medium oil on canvas, dimensions 51.8 x 43.8 cm, object page https://www.nga.gov/Collection/art-object-page.1128.html
- Public domain basis: the painting is dated 1845 and Sully died in 1872, long out of copyright, and the National Gallery of Art releases images of its public domain works under its open access policy (the Gallery's record for this object, opened directly at nga.gov for this cover, carries the artwork overview, provenance, and credit line above; the Gallery's partner record lists the rights as CC0). The record was verified directly through the holding institution's own object page for this integration.
- The copy held locally is covers/jackson-sully.jpg (1274 x 1654), the same file the unit ships in Lesson 1, originally downloaded through the Wikimedia Commons file path service from the file "Andrew Jackson by Thomas Sully.png," whose dimensions match this copy exactly. The cover frame crop (object-fit: cover, object-position center 22%) holds the head and shoulders with the military coat's collar and epaulette at the frame's foot; visually checked.
