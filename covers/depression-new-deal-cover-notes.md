## Photograph

- Title: "Destitute pea pickers in California. Mother of seven children. Age thirty-two. Nipomo, California," known as Migrant Mother (Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, item 2017762891)
- What it actually is: Florence Owens Thompson with three of her children at a pea pickers' camp in Nipomo, California, photographed by Dorothea Lange in March 1936. The negative was retouched in the 1930s to erase a thumb holding a tent pole in the lower right corner; the cover uses the standard retouched file, the version the unit test prints.
- Creator: Dorothea Lange, photographer, for the Farm Security Administration
- Date: March 1936 (per the Library of Congress catalog record)
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, Farm Security Administration/Office of War Information Collection
- Reproduction number: LC-DIG-fsa-8b29516 (digital file from original negative). Call number: LC-USF34-009058-C [P&P]
- Source URLs: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2017762891/ and the Wikimedia Commons file page for the same negative, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lange-MigrantMother02.jpg
- Public domain basis: The Library of Congress rights advisory for the record reads "No known restrictions" (see the FSA/OWI photographs advisory, LOC Prints & Photographs reading room). The photograph is a work of a United States federal employee made for a federal agency, and the Commons file page marks it Public domain.
- The image is held locally as covers/migrant-mother-1936.jpg (1280 x 1664, the copy the unit itself ships in Lesson 1 and the unit test). The cover frame crop (object-fit: cover, object-position center 22%) holds on Thompson's face and the two children leaning away on either side.
