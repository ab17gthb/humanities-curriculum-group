## Photograph

- Title: USS Maine (ACR-1) starboard bow view (Library of Congress Lot-3370-7)
- What it actually is: A starboard bow view of the battleship USS Maine, the ship whose explosion in Havana harbor on February 15, 1898, became the rallying cause of the Spanish-American War. The photograph shows the ship intact and underway, not the wreck.
- Creator: Photographed by J.S. Johnston, 1898 (per the Library of Congress description carried on the Wikimedia Commons file page)
- Date: 1898. Date disclosure: the Wikimedia Commons file page carries a "Date" field of 19 May 2016, which is the date the National Museum of the U.S. Navy posted its digitized copy to Flickr, not the date of the photograph. The photograph itself is dated 1898 in the file's description ("Photographed by J.S. Johnston, 1898"), and the Commons file was cropped 2 percent horizontally and 11 percent vertically and rotated 1.7 degrees in 2023, per its file history. The cover crops the image further to fill its photo frame, as the other unit covers do.
- Repository: Courtesy of the Library of Congress (Lot-3370-7), via the National Museum of the U.S. Navy Flickr posting and Wikimedia Commons
- Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:USS_Maine_(ACR-1)_starboard_bow_view,_1898_(26510673494).jpg
- Public domain basis: The file is identified on Wikimedia Commons as a work of a sailor or employee of the U.S. Navy taken or made as part of that person's official duties, and therefore a work of the U.S. federal government in the public domain in the United States; it was also reviewed on Flickr and confirmed under the Public Domain Mark. The photograph was taken in 1898, so any copyright has in any case expired.
- The image is held locally as covers/uss-maine-1898.jpg (2890 x 1805, the full resolution of the Commons original).
