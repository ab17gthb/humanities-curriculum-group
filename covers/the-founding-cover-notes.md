## Image

- Title: The Bloody Massacre perpetrated in King-Street BOSTON on March 5th 1770 by a Party of the 29th Regt (the Boston Massacre engraving)
- What it actually is: Paul Revere's hand-colored engraving of the Boston Massacre, copied from Henry Pelham's design and printed in Boston within weeks of the shootings of March 5, 1770. The cover frame crops the central scene: the British firing line at right, the fallen colonists at left, the smoke between them, and Revere's own imprint line, "Engrav'd Printed & Sold by Paul Revere Boston," visible at the foot of the scene. The same engraving ships in the unit itself, in Lesson 1 (img/revere-boston-massacre-1770.jpg; the lesson's deck crop revere-port.jpg derives from it), where the lesson's SOURCES.md carries its record, and it is one of the seven documents of the unit's lead premium DBQ (DBQ176).
- Creator: Paul Revere (1735 to 1818), after Henry Pelham
- Date: 1770
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division
- Identifiers: Library of Congress item 2008661777, call number FP - XVIII - R452, no. 1 (A size) [P&P], digital file service/pnp/ppmsca/01600/01657
- Public domain basis: an engraving published in 1770, long out of copyright. The record was opened directly at the Library of Congress for this cover (item 2008661777), and its rights advisory reads "No known restrictions on publication." The unit's Lesson 1 SOURCES.md records the same basis.
- The copy held locally is covers/the-founding-revere-massacre-1770.jpg (873 x 1024), the largest copy the Library of Congress file service serves for this item, downloaded fresh for this cover; it is a larger copy of the same file the unit ships. The cover frame crop (object-fit: cover, object-position center 55%) holds the firing line, the victims, and the imprint line; visually checked.
