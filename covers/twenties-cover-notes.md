# The Twenties cover notes

Built 2026-10-22, following the construction of the Imperialism and World War I cover (cover-imperialism-wwi.html): navy background, cream wordmark, rust rule, one period photograph, contents lines, Knoweth footer. Source file: cover-twenties.html. Outputs: twenties-cover.png (1632 x 2112) and twenties-cover.pdf (printable, US Letter).

## Photograph

- Title: Charles Lindbergh and airplane "Spirit of St. Louis" (Library of Congress, LCCN2016888581)
- What it actually is: Lindbergh in flight gear shaking hands with an Army officer beside the Ryan NYP Spirit of St. Louis, the aircraft's name lettered on the fuselage at the right of the frame. The photograph is a Harris & Ewing glass negative, 4 x 5 in.
- Creator: Harris & Ewing, 1927 (per the Library of Congress catalog record)
- Date: 1927, the year of the transatlantic flight (May 20–21, 1927).
- Repository: Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, Harris & Ewing Collection
- Source URLs: https://lccn.loc.gov/2016888581 and the Wikimedia Commons file page, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_Lindbergh_and_airplane_%22Spirit_of_St._Louis%22_LCCN2016888581.jpg
- Public domain basis: The file is marked Public domain on Wikimedia Commons. It is a Harris & Ewing photograph from 1927 held by the Library of Congress; works published in the United States in 1927 are in the public domain, and the Library of Congress places no restrictions on this image.
- The image is held locally as covers/lindbergh-1927.jpg (10008 x 7752, the full resolution of the Commons original, a TIFF-derived JPEG of the glass negative). The cover HTML renders from a downscaled copy, covers/lindbergh-1927-cover.jpg (2600 x 2014), because the headless renderer crashed decoding the full-resolution file; the frame crop (object-fit: cover, object-position center 35%) trims most of the glass negative's ragged left and right edges, as the other unit covers crop their photographs.
