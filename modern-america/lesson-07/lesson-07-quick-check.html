Quick Check: The Rescue and Its Terms

Directions: Read the document and the background, then answer the questions.

Document. Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008, Public Law 110-343, Section 2, Statutes at Large via GovInfo.

"The purposes of this Act are (1) to immediately provide authority and facilities that the Secretary of the Treasury can use to restore liquidity and stability to the financial system of the United States; and (2) to ensure that such authority and such facilities are used in a manner that (A) protects home values, college funds, retirement accounts, and life savings; (B) preserves homeownership and promotes jobs and economic growth; (C) maximizes overall returns to the taxpayers of the United States; and (D) provides public accountability for the exercise of such authority."

Background. Congress passed the act in October 2008, weeks after Lehman Brothers failed on September 15 and credit markets froze. The act created the Troubled Asset Relief Program, and Section 115 released its authority in stages, from $250,000,000,000 outstanding at any one time up to $700,000,000,000. The Financial Crisis Inquiry Commission later concluded that the crisis was avoidable and named failures of regulation, corporate governance, and borrowing among its causes. In February 2009 the government added a stimulus of about eight hundred billion dollars. Unemployment still reached 10.0 percent in October 2009.

1. State the first purpose of the act in the statute's own terms, naming what the Secretary of the Treasury is given and the two conditions he is to restore.

2. The second purpose lists four requirements on how the authority is used. Name the requirement that concerns taxpayers' money and the requirement that concerns the public's knowledge, in the statute's words.

3. Using the background, state the starting limit and the final limit on TARP purchases under Section 115, and name the event of September 2008 that stood behind the act's passage.

4. Using the background, state the Commission's verdict on whether the crisis had to happen, and state the unemployment figure that followed the rescue and the stimulus, with its month and year.

Teacher Answer Key

1. State the first purpose. The act gives the Secretary of the Treasury authority and facilities to restore liquidity and stability to the financial system of the United States. Both nouns are required: the authority is the means, the two conditions are liquidity and stability. (Blueprint 13)

2. Name the two requirements. The taxpayer requirement is that the authority be used in a manner that maximizes overall returns to the taxpayers of the United States. The public knowledge requirement is that it provides public accountability for the exercise of such authority. Answers that name protecting home values or preserving homeownership have chosen the household requirements instead and earn partial credit. (Blueprint 13)

3. State the two limits, and name the event. Section 115 began at $250,000,000,000 outstanding at any one time and rose by stages to $700,000,000,000 outstanding at any one time. The event was the failure of Lehman Brothers on September 15, 2008, after which credit markets froze. (Blueprint 13)

4. State the verdict and the figure. The Financial Crisis Inquiry Commission concluded that the crisis was avoidable. Unemployment reached 10.0 percent in October 2009, after both the rescue and the stimulus, which is the background's point: the rescue steadied the financial system without preventing the deepest job losses of the downturn. (Blueprint 14)

Teacher note. This check is formative. Questions 1 and 2 test close reading of the statute's own purposes, which the activity's Station 2 also uses. Question 3 tests the staged structure of the rescue and its September 2008 setting. Question 4 tests the aftermath's central tension, a system steadied and a household economy still falling, which the writing task takes up directly.
