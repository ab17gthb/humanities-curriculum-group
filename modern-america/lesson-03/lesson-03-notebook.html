Teacher Answer Key

Part of the lessonWhat happenedWho gainedWho paid
The Gulf WarIraq invaded Kuwait in August 1990. A coalition of twenty eight countries, with United Nations authorization and the consent of Congress, drove Iraq's army out in six weeks in early 1991.Kuwait, liberated. The United States, which demonstrated it could build and lead a coalition as the sole superpower.The United States paid the war's cost, and the victory opened the peace dividend argument rather than settling it.
The ADAPresident Bush signed the ADA on July 26, 1990. Congress found some 43,000,000 Americans had disabilities and faced persistent discrimination.Americans with disabilities, who gained a federal ban on discrimination, reasonable accommodation at work, and access to public accommodations.Employers and businesses paid the cost of accommodation and access, within cost limits and phase in periods written into the law.
NAFTA and tradeNAFTA removed most tariffs among the United States, Canada, and Mexico from 1994. The WTO followed in 1995 and China trade relations in 2000. Trade rose toward three tenths of the economy by 2010 (BEA).Consumers, through lower prices, and exporters and investors, on the supporters' account, with rising productivity.Factory workers and towns whose production moved abroad, on the critics' account, argued in 1993 by organized labor and Ross Perot.
Factories and servicesManufacturing employment fell from about 17.3 million in 1990 to about 11.5 million by 2010 (BLS). Outsourcing and automation shrank goods jobs while service jobs grew.Service employers and workers in growing service fields, and consumers of cheaper goods.The Rust Belt's mill towns and their workers, who lost jobs, population, and the tax base the mills had carried.
The boom and welfareUnemployment fell to 3.9 percent in 2000 and the budget ran surpluses from 1998 to 2001. The 1996 law replaced AFDC with TANF, with work after two years and a five year lifetime limit.Workers who found jobs in a tight labor market, and taxpayers in the surplus years, on the law's supporters' account.Families who reached the time limits without steady work, on the law's critics' account.

The dividend in one sentence. Strong answers hold both halves. The dividend was paid in part, in the surplus years and low unemployment, and not as the single transfer its name promised, because the same decade charged defense towns, factory workers, and welfare families costs the top line figures do not show.
