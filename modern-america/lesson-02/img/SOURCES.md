No copyrighted work is quoted anywhere in this lesson.

## Subjects for which no public domain image is used, recorded honestly
- A correction on this build's own downloads: one Commons fetch for a Reagan ranch portrait returned, under a ranch filename, a White House photograph of Nancy Reagan at a Just Say No rally. Pixel inspection caught the mismatch before delivery. The file is kept in assets/ under its true name (nancy-reagan-just-say-no.jpg), is public domain as a federal work, and is not used anywhere in the lesson. The Section D slide uses the swearing-in photograph recorded above instead.
- The PATCO strike: the strike photographs returned by the Commons search for this build carry a Creative Commons share alike license, not a public domain basis, and no federal photograph of the August 1981 strike with a verified public domain basis was used. The deck teaches PATCO in text and a stat slide, and the activity teaches it from the president's own statement.
- The Tower Commission: the photograph of the president receiving the report in the Cabinet Room exists in the federal record, but no copy with a public domain basis verified on its file page was downloaded for this build. The report is taught from its text.
- The Challenger launch: NASA launch and explosion photographs are public domain, but the deck uses the McNair portrait instead, as the sober record of one of the seven, and the address is taught from its text.

## Quoted texts, with word counts of the passages printed
- Ronald Reagan, First Inaugural Address, January 20, 1981 (Gilder Lehrman Institute text), as recorded in the audited TPSP bank record reagan-first-inaugural-1981. Public document. Activity Station 1 prints 92 quoted words in three passages, cuts marked with ellipses. The quick check prints 68 quoted words. The reading prints the 16 word crisis sentence inside its prose.
- Economic Recovery Tax Act of 1981, Public Law 97-34 (United States Statutes at Large, volume 95, pages 172 and 176 to 183, GovInfo edition, package STATUTE-95). Public document. The statute PDF was retrieved from GovInfo for this build and its text extracted and read directly. Activity Station 2 prints 137 quoted words verbatim: the act's statement of purposes (42 words, 95 Stat. 172), its short title sentence (13 words), the Section 101 heading phrase "Rate cuts; rate reduction credit" (5 words, 95 Stat. 176), the 1981 rate reduction credit as written into the code at Section 6428(a) (64 words, 95 Stat. 182), and the last line of the new 1982 rate table for married individuals filing joint returns (13 words, 95 Stat. 176 to 177). The rate tables themselves are taught as a labeled summary on the face of the station, resting on the tables at 95 Stat. 176 to 182: new schedules for taxable years beginning in 1982, in 1983, and after 1983, each lower than the last, top rate 50 percent against 70 percent under the prior law.
- Ronald Reagan, Remarks and a Question-and-Answer Session With Reporters on the Air Traffic Controllers Strike, August 3, 1981 (Public Papers of the Presidents; listed in the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library's speech index for August 3, 1981, text as carried by the American Presidency Project). Public document. Activity Station 3 prints 124 quoted words verbatim, set inside a labeled summary of the strike resting on the public record: the demands (a $10,000 increase, a 32 hour week, a better retirement package), the firings of August 5, 1981 (more than 11,000 controllers), and the decertification of PATCO in October 1981.
- Report of the President's Special Review Board (the Tower Commission), February 26, 1987 (Government Printing Office), Part IV, "What Was Wrong." Public document. Activity Station 4 prints 120 quoted words in four passages, cuts marked with ellipses, verified against the report text as carried by the American Presidency Project excerpts page. Sentences carrying transcription errors in that excerpts page were not quoted.
- Ronald Reagan, Address to the Nation on the Challenger Disaster, January 28, 1986 (Miller Center text), as recorded in the audited TPSP bank record reagan-challenger-address-1986. Public document. Activity Station 5 prints 139 quoted words in three passages, cuts marked with ellipses. The reading prints the 12 word fainthearted sentence inside its prose.

## Statistics named in the documents, with their sources as printed
- Unemployment reaching 10.8 percent in the autumn of 1982 and the recession dates: Bureau of Labor Statistics, named in the reading and deck notes.
- Interest rates above 19 percent under Chairman Paul Volcker, inflation above 13 percent in 1980 and below 4 percent by 1983: the standard Federal Reserve and Bureau of Labor Statistics record, stated in the reading at that precision.
- National debt from about 900 billion dollars in 1981 to about 2.9 trillion dollars by 1989: Treasury figures as carried in the unit skeleton's reading scope, stated with "about" in every document.
- Controllers: about 13,000 struck and more than 11,000 were fired, the figures carried in the unit's canonical vocabulary definition of the PATCO strike and used consistently across the lesson.
