Teacher Answer Key

Piece of the storyWhat it wasKind: failure, diagnosis, or organizationThe evidence
The postwar settlementThe expectation after 1945 of rising wages, cheap energy, and a federal government that managed the economy and kept unemployment low.The order that broke downBy the middle 1970s all three parts of the settlement had failed at once.
Stagflation and the energy crisisStagnant growth, high unemployment, and high inflation together, worsened by the oil shocks of 1973 and 1979.FailureInflation reached 13.5 percent in 1980 and unemployment 8.5 percent in 1975, Bureau of Labor Statistics annual averages.
Watergate, the pardon, and 1975Nixon's resignation on August 9, 1974, Ford's pardon in Proclamation 4311, the fall of Saigon, and New York City near default.Failure of trustThe pardon granted was full, free, and absolute, for the period January 20, 1969 through August 9, 1974.
Carter's diagnosis, 1979The Crisis of Confidence address of July 15, 1979, locating the threat in a loss of national confidence and a turn to consumption.DiagnosisCarter said a majority believed the next five years would be worse than the past five, and two thirds of the people do not even vote.
Proposition 13 and the tax revoltThe California ballot measure of June 1978 that cut property taxes sharply and capped them at one percent of full cash value, opening the national tax revolt.Diagnosis and organizationArticle XIII A of the California Constitution caps the tax and limits growth in assessed values until a property is sold.
The New Right and the Moral MajorityThe conservative coalition built on direct mail, single issue groups, and research institutes, joined in 1979 by Falwell's Moral Majority mobilizing evangelical voters.OrganizationThe coalition joined white southern voters courted by the southern strategy to suburban taxpayers in the West and North.

The decade in one sentence. Strong answers name the builders and the grievance together. By 1980 the politics that replaced the postwar settlement had been organized by the tax revolt campaigns that proved the tax issue in California in 1978, the New Right's direct mail machinery and single issue groups, and the Moral Majority's church networks, working on a country whose wages, prices, and trust in government had all failed in the same decade.
