## Gaps recorded honestly

The one Commons photograph found (Howard Jarvis after the Proposition 13 victory, Los Angeles Times, via UCLA Library) is licensed CC BY 4.0, not public domain, and was not used. The tax revolt slides are text and stat slides.
- No public domain photograph of an Equal Rights Amendment ratification march with a verified public domain basis was located on a Commons file page for this lesson. The ERA station is text only.

## Text sources quoted in this lesson, with editions
- Jimmy Carter, Crisis of Confidence Address, July 15, 1979 (Miller Center text), as carried in the audited TPSP record, id carter-crisis-of-confidence-1979, in modern.json. Quoted in the activity (Station 1), the quick check, the writing evidence bank, and the deck notes. Cuts are marked with ellipses in the documents. This is a presidential address, a public document.
- Gerald R. Ford, Proclamation 4311, Granting Pardon to Richard Nixon, September 8, 1974 (Ford Presidential Library), as carried in the audited TPSP record, id ford-proclamation-4311-1974, in modern.json. Quoted in the activity (Station 3), the writing evidence bank, and the deck notes. Cuts are marked with ellipses. This is a presidential proclamation, a public document.
- Equal Rights Amendment, House Joint Resolution 208, as passed by Congress, 1972 (Congressional Research Service text), as carried in the audited TPSP record, id equal-rights-amendment-1972, in modern.json. Quoted in full at Station 5 of the activity (153 words, a public document of Congress).
- California Constitution, Article XIII A, Section 1(a), added June 6, 1978 by Proposition 13 (California Legislative Counsel, leginfo.legislature.ca.gov). Section 1(a) is quoted verbatim at Station 2 of the activity and in the deck notes; the wording was verified against the Legislative Counsel text as reproduced in the official Codes Display and two independent reproductions of the article. The article's assessment rules (rollback, two percent annual growth until sale, two thirds vote for many special taxes) are taught at Station 2 as a labeled summary on the station's face, not as a quotation.
- Bureau of Labor Statistics annual averages, as published by the Bureau: CPI inflation 1973 6.2 percent, 1974 11.0 percent, 1979 11.3 percent, 1980 13.5 percent; unemployment 1975 8.5 percent, 1979 5.9 percent, 1980 7.2 percent. Printed as a data table at Station 4 of the activity with the source named on its face, and used in the reading, guided notes, quick check, writing bank, and deck. Federal statistics, public documents.
- Richard M. Nixon's resignation letter, August 9, 1974 (National Archives, via Wikisource), as carried in the audited TPSP record, id nixon-resignation-letter-1974. Named in the reading and deck as a one sentence letter; the letter itself is not quoted in this lesson.
- The Herblock "Mugging" cartoon of October 23, 1973 (Library of Congress), as carried in the audited TPSP record, id herblock-mugging-1973, exists in the bank as a description only. It is not reproduced and is not quoted in this lesson's documents. No copyrighted cartoon image or text is used.
- Regents of the University of California v. Bakke, 438 U.S. 265 (1978). The decision is summarized in the reading, guided notes, and deck (fixed quota struck down, race allowed as one factor in admissions). No passage is quoted in this lesson.
