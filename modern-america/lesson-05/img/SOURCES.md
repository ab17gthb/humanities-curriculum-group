## Images considered and NOT used, recorded on purpose
- Aerial photograph of downtown Phoenix: the Commons file Downtown Phoenix Aerial Looking Northeast is licensed CC BY-SA 3.0, a Creative Commons license, not public domain. It does not qualify under this unit's public domain only rule and was not used. The Sun Belt is taught through Census Bureau data records and stat slides instead.
- Detroit photography: well known Detroit ruin and abandonment photography is the work of private photographers under copyright. No verified public domain federal image of Detroit's decline was found in this build, so Detroit is taught through the census data record and a stat slide, with no photograph.
- No photograph of the wartime internment camps is used in this lesson. The lesson teaches the internment through the commission report's text and the 1988 signing photograph.

## Quotation log (copyright discipline)
All quoted texts in this lesson are public documents: federal statutes, a federal commission report, and federal statistics. No copyrighted work is quoted anywhere in this lesson.

### Immigration Reform and Control Act of 1986 (audited bank record immigration-reform-control-act-1986; edition: enrolled text, verified against the Statutes at Large, per the record's sourceNote)
- Activity, Station 2: two passages quoted from the bank record, one from Section 101 (unlawful employment of aliens, new Section 274A) and one from Section 201 (legalization, new Section 245A), with cuts marked by ellipses and disclosed in the Source line. Public document, quoted from the audited record.

### Personal Justice Denied (audited bank record personal-justice-denied-1983; edition: Commission on Wartime Relocation and Internment of Civilians, Personal Justice Denied, 1982 to 1983, National Archives, per the record's sourceNote)
- Activity, Station 3: the commission's conclusion and its June 1983 recommendations quoted from the bank record, with cuts marked by ellipses and disclosed in the Source line. One cut replaces a passage set off by em dashes in the record with an ellipsis, so the file keeps this unit's rule of no em dashes; the words on either side of the cut are the record's own. Public document, quoted from the audited record.

### Immigration and Nationality Act of 1965 (Public Law 89-236, 79 Stat. 911)
- Activity, Station 1 is a labeled summary record, not a quotation. The operative enrolled text could not be fetched and verified from GovInfo or Congress.gov in this build (the Statutes at Large scan on Wikisource returned an access error), so the station summarizes the act's provisions from the public record of the law and says so on its face, following the unit build spec's rule for unverifiable texts. No sentence in the station is presented as the statute's exact words.

### Census data records (United States Census Bureau)
- Activity, Station 4: foreign born population and share, 1970 to 2010, from decennial censuses and the American Community Survey for 2010, rounded as stated on the station's face (about 9.6 million and 4.7 percent in 1970; about 40 million and about 13 percent in 2010).
- Activity, Station 5 and the quick check data record: Detroit population about 1.85 million in 1950 and about 714,000 in 2010; Phoenix about 107,000 in 1950 and about 1.45 million in 2010; South and West gains in House seats in every reapportionment after the censuses of 1970 through 2010. All from Census Bureau decennial censuses and reapportionment records, rounded, with the source named on the face of each document.
