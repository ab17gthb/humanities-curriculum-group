Band 2 Skills Task: Two Republics, Compared

Directions: Comparison is the work of this band: the same republic at two moments, read side by side. Part 1 sets Jefferson's republic (1801 to 1809) against Jackson's democracy (1829 to 1837) on four measures. Part 2 reads one institution, the national bank, through both eras' eyes. Part 3 asks for a verdict.

Part 2: The Bank, Read Twice

1791 to 1819: In the first era, the national bank was chartered, allowed to lapse, chartered again, and protected by the Supreme Court. Summarize, in two or three sentences, what the bank was for, in the eyes of the governments that built it.

1832: Now summarize Jackson's veto reasoning in two or three sentences: what was the bank, in his eyes?

The turn: The bank's functions did not change between 1819 and 1832. In one or two sentences: what changed, the institution, or the premises about democracy through which it was read?

Part 3: The Verdict

Wider or deeper? Argue in a paragraph which era's democracy admitted more of the people, and which era's democracy claimed more power over the nation's life, using at least one fact from each era for each measure. The two halves of your verdict do not have to point to the same era.
