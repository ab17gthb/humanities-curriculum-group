# Band 3 Template Day: Manifest

Built 2026-10-23, directly in the HCG chat per the credit-lean rule. Status:
TEMPLATE for Alex's review. Not integrated into the site, planner, or storage.
The remaining seven bands follow this day's pattern once Alex approves it.

Band 3: Slavery, War, and the Unfinished Settlement. Reviews Entry 5
(Sectional Crisis and Civil War) and Entry 6 (Reconstruction) of the course
review blueprint (see ../registry-draft.md).

## Documents (5 files, 21 printed pages)

- day-3-review-packet (student, 6 pp): the band in brief, both units'
  essential questions restated verbatim, a 31-row timeline (1850 to 1877),
  the argument of each unit in four headed sections, a People of the Band
  reference, and three carry-in questions for the practice set.
- day-3-term-sprint (student, 6 pp): 40 terms, 20 per unit, spread across
  all eight lessons of each. Parts A and C are matching sets with the
  definitions printed scrambled; Part B is eight use-the-term prompts that
  re-work eight of the forty for recall.
- day-3-practice-set (student, 4 pp): 18 questions, 30 points, in the unit
  tests' style: 12 multiple choice (1 pt) and 6 short answers (3 pts). Every
  item carries a printed "Reviews Entry N · Unit, Lesson" line naming its
  reteach target.
- day-3-skills-task (student, 2 pp): causation. "Two Settlements, Two
  Failures": organizers for the Compromise of 1850 and the Reconstruction
  settlement, then a verdict essay on which failure was more avoidable, with
  a counterpoint requirement.
- day-3-teacher-guide (teacher, 3 pp): the full front matter (placement,
  pairings, prior knowledge, state-neutral standards, content statement,
  timing and materials), run-of-day notes, the sprint key, the practice key
  with a reteach note on every short answer, and skills-task scoring
  guidance (10 points).

## Verification (all run on the finished files)

- Term sprint generated by script (gen_sprint.py) from the two units'
  audited Rycal staging data, so no definition was hand-copied: all 40
  printed definitions byte-identical to the units' guided-notes strings,
  and all 40 key letters in the teacher guide resolve to the correct
  verbatim definition (checked programmatically both ways).
- Zero em dashes and zero instances of the banned "matters" construction
  family in all five documents.
- Audience rule: student documents carry no product, sales, or teacher talk
  (scanned); the teacher guide carries the pedagogy.
- Pagination: every document ends at a deliberate boundary after one fix
  round (packet's last page was a quarter full; a People of the Band
  reference was added rather than stretching spacing; sprint and practice
  tails rebalanced by spacing and writing-space adjustments, then verified
  page by page).

## Honest flags

- Two crowd-pleasing figures were caught in drafting and removed rather
  than printed unverified: a count of Black officeholders ("some two
  thousand") and a percentage for Lincoln's 1860 electoral vote. The packet
  now makes both points without numbers. The one figure kept is the
  standard count of about 180,000 Black soldiers in United States Colored
  Troops service, which the Sectional Crisis unit's own materials support.
- No source is quoted anywhere in the day. Every excerpt-bearing element of
  the plan was deferred: the packet condenses the units' verified prose,
  and the audit posture of the originals is inherited, never re-derived.
- The skills rotation is set by this template as a draft for the full
  build: Band 1 chronology, Band 2 comparison, Band 3 causation, Band 4
  continuity and change, Band 5 comparison, Band 6 causation, Band 7
  continuity and change, Band 8 documents (per the plan, day 8's task is
  document-based).
- Part B of the sprint deliberately re-uses eight terms from the matching
  sets: recognition first, then recall, inside one sitting.
