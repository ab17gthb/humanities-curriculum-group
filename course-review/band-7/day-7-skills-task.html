Part 2: Two Courthouse Photographs

Scene one: A southern county courthouse, registration day, 1948. Describe in three or four sentences what a photograph would show: who is present, who is absent, and what the registrar's office is doing.

Scene two: The same courthouse, registration day, 1968. Describe what the photograph shows now, and name the federal machinery standing behind the difference.

The turn: Now name two things the 1968 photograph cannot show that had also changed since 1948, and one thing it cannot show that had not changed. One or two sentences each.
