# Study Guides: Manifest

Built 2026-10-23 by gen_study_guides.py from course data: band packet
timelines, planner-data.js essential questions, audited staging JSON
(term lists and key definitions, byte-verbatim), the bands' Carry Three
questions, and authored material in guide_data.py (15 unit argument
paragraphs, 24 review-question key answers).

- Early: 6 units, 336 terms, 51 pages.
- Modern: 9 units, 504 terms, 67 pages.
- Combined: 15 units, 840 terms, 116 pages.

Verification: every key definition checked against its own unit's
staging file (840 of 840 in Combined; the term "assimilation" is defined
twice inside the Gilded Age unit itself and both print verbatim in their
lesson positions). Student prose carries zero em dashes and none of the
banned family; lesson headings take the unit guides' colon form.
