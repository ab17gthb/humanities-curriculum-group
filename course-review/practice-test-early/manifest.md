# Practice Tests: Manifest

Built 2026-10-23 by gen_practice_tests.py with content in test_data.py,
in the unit tests' exact format: Matching 15, Multiple Choice 20,
Sources 15, fifty points, full teacher key in the same file.

Verification: every matching term validated against its unit's audited
staging file and its printed definition taken verbatim from it; the
printed matching keys were re-verified independently from the written
HTML (each key letter points at the row carrying that term's true
definition, 15 of 15 in each test). Every MC key spread is 5/5/5/5.
Coverage recomputed from the item tags: every entry in each variant's
blueprint is assessed at least twice (Combined minimum exactly two, by
design). Source excerpts are public-domain or official texts with cuts
marked; the Truman and Johnson addresses are presidential messages to
Congress, the AUMF is statutory text. Eleven pages per test, matching
the unit tests' length.
