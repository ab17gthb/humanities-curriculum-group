Teacher Answer Key

EpisodeWhat happenedThe power used or claimedWhat it settled or left open
The unwindingThe Sedition Act expired and its prisoners were pardoned, the citizenship wait returned to five years, the whiskey excise was repealed, the army and navy were cut, and Gallatin paid down the debt on customs and land sales.Strict construction as a governing program: the federal government held to the powers the Constitution names.It settled the Republican program at home. It left Jefferson governing on a reading of the Constitution the purchase would break within two years.
The undelivered commissionMarbury's commission, signed and sealed under Adams, sat undelivered. Madison refused delivery and Marbury sued, asking the Court to order it.The midnight judges' life tenure against the incoming president's refusal to complete his predecessor's appointments.It settled nothing by itself. It handed Marshall the case in which the Court would claim judicial review.
The Barbary WarJefferson refused new tribute to Tripoli, sent a small navy, lost the Philadelphia to capture in 1803, and saw Decatur burn it in February 1804. The war closed in 1805.Naval force in place of tribute, used by an administration elected to cut the navy.It settled tribute to Tripoli. It left a sixty thousand dollar payment for the Philadelphia prisoners, and payments to other Barbary rulers until 1815.
The purchaseNapoleon offered the whole of Louisiana. The treaty of April 1803 ceded France's claim for fifteen million dollars, and the Senate ratified in October.The president's treaty power, stretched to acquire foreign territory and foreign peoples for the Union.It settled ownership of the Mississippi's west bank on paper. It left the territory's boundaries, and its people's citizenship, to be worked out afterward.
The scrupleJefferson wrote to Breckinridge that the Constitution had made no provision for holding foreign territory and the executive had acted beyond it. He drafted an amendment and abandoned it.An executive confession, offered in a private letter, with a cure proposed and never carried out.It settled the honesty of the record. It left the constitutional defect standing: no amendment approving the purchase was ever adopted.
The transferThe flag rose at New Orleans in December 1803 over about fifty thousand people, many enslaved, governed by appointment from Washington while they petitioned for citizens' rights.Appointed territorial government over people the treaty had promised incorporation into the Union.It settled possession. It left open whether an empire of liberty could govern acquired peoples as subjects and stay a republic of citizens.

The lesson in one sentence. Strong answers name the Breckinridge letter of August 12, 1803, because it is the one document where Jefferson himself sets the purchase beside his principles and records the gap: no provision for holding foreign territory, an act beyond the Constitution, and an amendment drafted and abandoned. Credit answers naming Marbury for the same work, a president's refusal answered by a court's enlarged power, when the reasoning is carried by the chart.
