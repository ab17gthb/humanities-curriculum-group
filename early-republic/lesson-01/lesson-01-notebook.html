Teacher Answer Key

Piece of the republicWhat it wasKind: invention, program, answer, or eventThe evidence
The invention of 1789Congress created the Departments of State, Treasury, and War in 1789, their heads formed Washington's cabinet, and the Judiciary Act organized the Supreme Court and lower federal courts.InventionThe Constitution named the offices and listed the powers but did not build the machinery.
Hamilton's four measuresFund the war debt at full value, assume the state war debts, charter the Bank of the United States, and lay an excise tax on distilled whiskey.ProgramHamilton argued powers could be implied as well as listed, under the necessary and proper clause.
The opposition and the partiesJefferson and Madison answered with a strict reading, the argument moved into the newspapers, and Federalists and Democratic-Republicans formed around it.AnswerFunding at full value rewarded speculators who had bought soldiers' certificates for a fraction of their worth.
The Whiskey RebellionWestern Pennsylvania farmers resisted the excise in 1794, and Washington answered with a militia army of some thirteen thousand men; the rebellion dissolved and two men convicted of treason were pardoned.EventThe new government would enforce its own laws, where the government under the Articles had been able only to watch.
The cotton ginWhitney's machine of 1794 pulled short staple cotton fiber through a slotted grate, leaving the seeds behind, and made the crop profitable across the inland South.InventionSlavery gained a new economic engine, and cotton planting and enslavement expanded across the South.
Neutrality, treaties, and the FarewellThe Neutrality Proclamation of 1793 held the country out of the war between France and Britain, the Jay Treaty won the frontier posts, and the Farewell Address of 1796 warned against party spirit and permanent alliances.ProgramWashington published the address in a Philadelphia newspaper and refused a third term, setting the two term precedent.

The republic in one sentence. Strong answers name machinery and a warning together. Washington's government made the Constitution real by building a cabinet and courts the document had not described and a financial program of funding, assumption, a bank, and an excise that gave the union credit and revenue, and it left office warning against the party spirit its own program had created and against the permanent foreign alliances its neutrality had avoided.
