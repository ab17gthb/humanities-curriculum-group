## Subjects for which no separate image was used
- No period image of the Bank of the United States building or of the Neutrality Proclamation broadside was downloaded for this lesson. The deck uses the Trumbull Hamilton portrait and text slides for those sections rather than a later artist imagining, and this file records that choice honestly.
