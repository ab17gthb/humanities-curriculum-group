Teacher Answer Key

The questionWhat was proposed, or claimedThe settlement adoptedWhat it postponed, or rested on
The hemisphereBritain proposed a joint declaration with the United States against European interference in the new American republics. Adams argued for declaring American principles alone, rather than as a cockboat in the wake of the British man of war.Monroe's annual message of December 2, 1823 declared the continents closed to future European colonization and European interference dangerous to the United States, with no American interference in existing colonies or European wars.The declaration rested on the British navy, whose trade interest ran the same way. The United States could not have enforced it alone in 1823, and later generations had to decide what it required.
Missouri's termsMissouri applied in 1819 as a slave state with the Senate at eleven free and eleven slave states. The Tallmadge Amendment would have barred bringing more enslaved people in and freed children born there at twenty five.The House passed the amendment on sectional lines and the Senate killed it. The settlement admitted Maine as a free state and Missouri as a slave state, holding the Senate balance.Missouri's constitution barred free Black citizens from entering the state. Congress fudged the question rather than resolving it, and Missouri's admission waited until 1821.
The territory's futureCongress needed a rule for the remaining Louisiana Purchase lands that both sections would accept after the Tallmadge fight.Thomas's line at 36 degrees 30 minutes, signed March 6, 1820: slavery forever prohibited north of the line, except as punishment for crime, and permitted south of it.Missouri stood north of the line and entered with slavery anyway. The line said nothing about territory acquired later, and its proviso required free territory to return people escaping into it.
The warningJefferson, retired at Monticello, read the settlement and wrote to John Holmes on April 22, 1820.He called the question a fire bell in the night and the knell of the Union, and the settlement a reprieve only, not a final sentence.A geographical line joined to a marked principle, moral and political, would never be obliterated, and every new irritation would mark it deeper and deeper.

The season in one sentence. Strong answers hold the settlement and its ground in one thought. The settlements of 1820 held for the moment because both sections could accept a paired admission and a drawn line, and Europe had no expedition ready to test a declaration sheltered by British sea power, and the ground under them was already moving because the line taught the country to argue about slavery as geography, every new territory would reopen the vote the Tallmadge Amendment first split by section, and the Doctrine's claim waited on American power that did not yet exist.
