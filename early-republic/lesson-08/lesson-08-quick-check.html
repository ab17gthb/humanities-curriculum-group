Quick Check: Blood on Which Soil

Directions: Read the two passages and the background, then answer the questions.

Document A. "But now, after reiterated menaces, Mexico has passed the boundary of the United States, has invaded our territory and shed American blood upon the American soil. She has proclaimed that hostilities have commenced, and that the two nations are now at war."

Source: James K. Polk, Message to Congress, May 11, 1846 (Avalon Project, Yale Law School).

Document B. "And whereas this House desires to obtain a full knowledge of all the facts which go to establish whether the particular spot of soil on which the blood of our citizens was so shed was, or was not, our own soil, at that time; therefore"

Source: Abraham Lincoln, "Spot" Resolutions in the United States House of Representatives, December 22, 1847 (Papers of Abraham Lincoln, document D200434b).

Background. Texas entered the Union by joint resolution in 1845, claiming the Rio Grande as its boundary, while Mexico held the boundary to be the Nueces River, farther north. Polk ordered American troops to the Rio Grande, and a Mexican force attacked a party of dragoons in the ground between the two rivers in April 1846. Congress recognized a state of war on May 13, 1846. The Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo of February 2, 1848 confirmed the Rio Grande and ceded California, New Mexico, and the country between to the United States for fifteen million dollars. The Wilmot Proviso, offered in 1846, would have barred slavery in territory taken from Mexico, passed the House repeatedly, and died in the Senate.

1. In Document A, state the three actions Polk says Mexico has taken, in his order.

2. In Document B, state the single question of fact the House says it wants answered, and name whose claim that question tests.

3. Using the background, state the two rivers the boundary dispute ran between, and explain why the location of the April 1846 clash decided whose account of the war's beginning could stand.

4. The war ended in a treaty and a proviso that failed. State one thing the treaty of February 1848 settled and one thing the proviso's failure left unsettled, and connect both to the lesson question.

Teacher Answer Key

1. State the three actions. Mexico has passed the boundary of the United States, has invaded its territory, and has shed American blood upon American soil. All three actions, in that order, are required for full credit. (Blueprint 15)

2. State the question of fact, and whose claim it tests. The House wants a full knowledge of the facts that establish whether the particular spot of soil on which the blood of citizens was shed was, or was not, American soil at that time. The question tests Polk's claim in Document A, since the war message treats the soil as settled American ground. (Blueprint 15)

3. State the two rivers, and the work of the location. The dispute ran between the Nueces River, the boundary Mexico held, and the Rio Grande, the boundary Texas claimed and the United States adopted at annexation. The dragoons were attacked in the ground between the rivers in April 1846. If that ground was American, Mexico invaded; if it was Mexican or disputed, American troops ordered into it had produced the clash, and Polk's account of a war begun by Mexico could not stand as stated. (Blueprint 15)

4. State one settlement and one unsettled question. The Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo of February 2, 1848 settled the boundary at the Rio Grande and transferred California, New Mexico, and the country between to the United States for fifteen million dollars, closing the war between the two nations. The failure of the Wilmot Proviso left unsettled whether slavery would exist in the territory taken from Mexico, since the House passed the bar repeatedly and the Senate blocked it. Together they answer the lesson question: Americans told themselves the war was defense of American soil, and the war left slavery in the cession standing open. (Blueprint 15)

Teacher note. This check is formative. Questions 1 and 2 test whether students can hold a claim and a question about the same fact separate, the skill the activity grading guide rewards. Questions 3 and 4 test the second half of the blueprint line, the treaty settlement of 1848 against the proviso that failed.
